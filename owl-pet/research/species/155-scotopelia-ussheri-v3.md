## Evidence boundary

Primary field photographs and text support an older free-living juvenile that was substantially smaller and stubbier than its parent, slightly paler, loose/fluffy in first-generation feathers, and capable of flight with virtually adult-like dusky-barred rufous wings. The paper estimates 4–5 months from an assumed laying season; this is not a known hatch date. A separate 2022 photographic record supports a whitish downy juvenile and adult plumage six months later but does not supply an exact early age or nest setting.

Adult boundaries: dark eyes; no ear tufts; mixed plain rufous/cream/white upperparts with only a few blackish marks; barred remiges/rectrices; pale finely dark-shaft-streaked underparts; bluish-grey bill, yellowish cere, and bare yellow-orange tarsi/toes. Riverine rainforest, raffia palms, narrow slow channels and overhanging fishing perches are supported. No source supplies sex-specific measurements, so adults must remain near-equal. No nest is documented in the sources read.

## Design decision

Use the better-documented action-capable older juvenile rather than repeating v2's age-unknown downy chick. Show a secure two-foot raffia branch grip and formed barred wing without implying exact age certainty or universal development. Keep the adults near-equal and avoid Pel's Fishing Owl's uniformly barred upperparts.
