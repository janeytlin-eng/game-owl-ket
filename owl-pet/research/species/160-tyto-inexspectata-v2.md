## Conflict correction and design decision

- The v1 phrase “documented age-unknown dependent fledgling” was too strong. The most relevant visible-juvenile field record is explicitly disputed in primary literature and may refer to *Tyto rosenbergii*.
- A hole-nesting report exists only through a secondary chain quoted by Fitzsimons; no nest interior, chick appearance, substrate or age sequence was read. v2 keeps a forest perch and makes no nest claim.
- Adult direction remains conservative: rufous facial disc/forecrown, dark eyes, pale bill, gray-brown to golden-rufous upperparts with restrained pale/dark spots, ochre barred wing/tail and fine-spotted pale underparts. A secondary source says female larger, but no quantified standing-height ratio was found, so adults remain near equal.
- The center bird is an **age-unknown older-young design inference** with compact, down-softened wing/tail and natural two-foot support. Exact age, iris chronology, feather sequence, flight ability and nest context remain unknown. scientifically_verified=false.
