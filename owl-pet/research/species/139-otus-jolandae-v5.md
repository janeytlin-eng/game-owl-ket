## Current name and historical assignments

Current valid name: *Otus jolandae* Sangster, B. F. King, Verbelen & Trainor, 2013 (ITIS TSN 1063240; current standing valid, credibility “verified — standards met”). The original description records the holotype’s prior assignments as *Pisorhina albiventris*, *Scops albiventris* and *Otus magicus albiventris*. These are historical specimen assignments, not a license to merge the Lombok bird with current *O. magicus albiventris*.

- Adult facts: dark cinnamon-brown crown/nape/upperparts with blackish shaft streaks and fine bars; warm cinnamon/rusty-buff breast darker than the whitish, finely barred belly; one pale scapular row with blackish tips; golden-yellow iris, brown/dark bill, dull-ochre feet; tarsal feathering reaches the toe bases.
- Sex evidence: one adult female (BMNH 97.11.1.56) had narrower dark bars/shaft stripes on crown and ear tufts, a paler and less extensively rusty-buff breast, and larger round/oval white median-covert spots than the holotype and another male. Other females differed only in throat details. This supports subtle individual/sex-linked variation, not a fixed size ratio.
- Immature evidence: female AMNH 629942 differed from the adult holotype in having scattered white-to-buffy-white juvenile feathers with dark spots/bars on crown and nape, plus buff-based rictal bristles and buff throat. The paper does not give exact age, iris chronology, wing/tail development, flight ability or a juvenile size ratio.
- Habitat/behaviour: calling birds used 15–20 m trees in solid forest or tree patches in open landscape; a low-elevation record came from secondary forest with 7–9 m palms. Recorded 25–1350 m. This supports a humid Lombok foothill/secondary-forest setting and normal branch support, not a nest.
