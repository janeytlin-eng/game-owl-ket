## Problem → evidence → change → recheck

1. **Problem:** first render gave the juvenile a tail extending beyond the folded wing and mature wing/tail finish.
2. **Evidence:** Taufiqurrahman et al. (2018), printed pp. 31–32, states formed wings projected beyond a short dark tail for the dated branch-stage juveniles.
3. **Edit 1:** shortened/obscured the long tail and softened the juvenile's adult-like wing finish.
4. **Recheck:** long tail was removed, but the tail became almost invisible, preventing a clean relation check.
5. **Edit 2:** revealed only a small short dark tail fringe ending before the folded wing tip while preserving text, adults, support, habitat and count.
6. **Final recheck:** full frame plus juvenile/adult crops opened. The inversion is corrected enough for a concept-stage pass; the precise wing-tip/tail-tip boundary remains partly occluded and therefore is not claimed as scientific verification.

## Remaining unknowns / limitations

- Exact juvenile hatch age, flight competence and sex are unknown.
- Adult population-level sexual size relation remains unknown.
- The edited short tail is intentionally subtle and partly occluded; a later science-calibration pass should compare against additional dated juvenile photographs if they become available.
- Adult and juvenile palette is a stylized tactile concept, not a measured colour standard.
