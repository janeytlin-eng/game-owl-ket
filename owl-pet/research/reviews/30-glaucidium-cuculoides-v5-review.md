## Evidence boundary

The HKBWS species account supports 22–25 cm adults, yellow eyes, strong fine barring, no face-like nape pattern, a spotted juvenile crown/forehead, natural-hole nesting and an undated yellow-eyed final fledgling stage. Hong Kong Bird Report 2009–10 pp. 127–128 supports a fledged juvenile photographed 5 June 2010 but does not provide day-age or flight threshold. Sankamethawee & Pierce (2019), pp. 157–160, supports usual cavity nesting and an exceptional failed ground attempt, not juvenile plumage or sex size. Mikkola upload was unavailable, so no page claim was added.
