## Sources actually read this cycle

### DAV-SCHERZINGER-2014 — primary, reused and re-read

Wolfgang Scherzinger, Yun Fang, Yue-Hua Sun & Siegfried Klaus, 2014, “Revised description of Pere David’s Owl *Strix davidi* based on field observations in Central China”, *Ornithologischer Anzeiger* 53:54–93. Full PDF opened; pp.54–55 establish field work 1995–2013 and warn that guide illustrations often inherited errors. Primary morphology on pp.59–63 and comparison pp.74–76 supports dark plain mantle/back, white scapular row, long tail, dark-brown eyes, pale-yellow bill, feathered tarsi/toe tops, pearl-spotted rim and mainly radial facial streaks—not a bold concentric target. The paper explicitly identifies strong concentric facial rings in Mikkola/other literature as erroneous transfer. Fig.11 and text pp.68–69 directly support an approximately four-week nest-box young: woolly dull-grey mesoptile with fine pale-brown undulating bars, tawny/rusty mask, dark-brown eyes, lilac-pink lids, pale eye frame, pale corneous-yellow bill and furry toe tops. Wings/tail/flight threshold remain insufficiently specified for a universal claim.

### DAV-MIK2013-348 — uploaded guide, re-read

Heimo Mikkola, *Owls of the World: A Photographic Guide* (2013), uploaded PDF pp.350–351 / printed pp.348–349. It is useful for common name, broad dark adult identity, long barred tail, dark eyes, yellow bill, high-elevation mixed/conifer habitat and secondary description of dirty-whitish grey-brown-barred mesoptile. Its “females are always much larger” and prominent concentric face statements conflict with the newer primary field series; neither is encoded as a visible size ratio or bold ring.

## Evidence → design decisions

- Adults: near-equal structural crown height; left male three-quarter alert glance down the branch, right female relaxed sideward head turn with torso not mirrored. No blanket female-larger rule.
- Adult identity: dark chocolate-brown upperparts, restrained white scapular drops, radial facial streaks, dark almond eyes, pale-yellow bill, long tail and fully supported feathered feet. Avoid Great Grey-style rings and generic pale Ural Owl whitening.
- Young: directly evidenced about-four-week stage only. Rounded grey woolly barred mesoptile, darker tawny mask, dark eyes/pinkish lids, pale bill, both feet braced on the bare box sill; compact down-obscured wing and tail, no flight action.
- Context: rough artificial study box in high-elevation central-China conifer forest. Bare sill/no nest material is a study observation, not a universal natural-nest rule.
- Unknown: exact hatch day of depicted individual, flight threshold, complete feather-tract sequence, population-wide sex-size ratio, and global species-versus-subspecies consensus.
- Scientific status: `false`; expected result `PASS_WITH_LIMITATIONS`.
