## Evidence synthesis and design decision

The new holotype photograph corrects the earlier tendency to make the adult belly almost plain: retain cream-white ground but show irregular brown barring on breast, flanks and lower belly. The living image supports amber-orange eyes and compact natural support but has no sex/age metadata. Adults therefore remain near-equal rather than applying a generic female-larger rule. No species-specific juvenile feather sequence, exact age, iris transition, flight threshold, nest contents or sex-size series was found. V3 keeps an **age-unknown older juvenile design inference** with closed eyes, a down-softened compact wing, concealed tail and bilateral branch support. The exterior cavity remains a habitat cue only.
