# Run154 native curved groom into actual GLB — unpublished test
Reuses the exact SHA256-verified original **Run153** unapproved .blend, not a new
photorealistic young owl. Converts the REAL native fibre curves to mesh
geometry (two LOD densities), exports GLB, then reloads each GLB into fresh
Blender scene and renders 4 native Cycles views each. Script and measurements
determine whether actual execution succeeded. Full run log, JSON files and
any actually generated contact sheet live in research-evidence-run154-REAL-GLB-HAIR-NO_RELEASE/.
Full binary models and render frames are private research artifacts only.

The underlying Run153 stylized sculpt was already art rejected: detached eye
and face elements, sparse spiky fibres, weak profile/back and poor hollow.
Exporting hair geometry is a *technical* portability experiment, NOT an
improvement to the juvenile's anatomy or a visual quality pass.
Do not show this as an acceptable sample, deploy it, infer a phone WebGL
result, or claim a rig/animation or a professionally groomed finished pet.
No mobile device was tested, no extra tool payment made.
