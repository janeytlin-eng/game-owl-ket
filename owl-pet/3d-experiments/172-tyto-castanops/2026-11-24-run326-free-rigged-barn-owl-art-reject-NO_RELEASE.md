Used existing real Chromium/Three.js GLTFLoader/SwiftShader WebGL2 harness with 390x780 simulated mobile viewport. Rendered 8 yaw angles in the original wings-open rest pose, then evaluated the original GLB's native Idle animation at a fixed timestamp and rendered the **same 8 camera angles with naturally folded skeletal wings**. Both browser tests returned technical load success, 8 distinct views each, 16 real WebGL2 screenshots. A non-blocking 404 for an incidental static resource was logged.

**Concrete rig finding:** bounding-box width was about **0.94476 m** with wings spread versus **0.18086 m** with the original Idle fold, approximately **80.9% reduction**. The genuine wing bones and mesh weight animation work; screenshot pairs changed about **21.47–24.53%** of pixels (not an artistic grade; camera re-framing also changes). This shows a potentially reusable animation topology reference, **not a qualified chick**.

Independently opened the original v3 target, the 16-view photo sheet, and enlarged frontal/oblique and rear screenshots. **ART_REJECTED.** The adult has a narrow upright silhouette, regular rigid wedge-shaped flight feathers, golden adult back, long visible tail/outer wings, simplified pale facial mask, prominent exposed toes and claws without old-tree hollow support. On front the body is sharply outlined and not plush; on rear the folded wings remain rigid separated flat strips. The original infant concept has a broadly round cream-white cloud-like chest/head and very short wings hidden by real fluffy down. The adult source differs fundamentally across all views. Painting it cream or adding a few fibres would not correct its skeletal age and feather-stage mismatch. Do not distribute or portray it as the #172 electronic pet.
