## Current and historical names

Current repository target remains the southeastern subspecies *Aegolius harrisii iheringi* (Sharpe, 1899), a child taxon of valid species *A. harrisii*. ITIS lists *A. harrisii iheringi* as a recognized subspecies; Avibase maps the original combination/protonym *Gisella iheringi* and shows current checklist recognition. Species-level historical synonym: *Nyctale harrisii* Cassin, 1849. The subspecies is not counted as a second species.
