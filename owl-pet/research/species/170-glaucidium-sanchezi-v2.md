## Evidence actually read

### TPO-HOWELL-ROBBINS-1995-P17-18-22
Howell, S. N. G. & Robbins, M. B. 1995. “Species limits of the Least Pygmy-Owl complex.” *Wilson Bulletin* 107(1):7–25. Primary museum/species-limits study. PDF: https://sora.unm.edu/sites/default/files/journals/wilson/v107n01/p0007-p0025.pdf . Printed pp.17–18 and 22–23 read 2026-11-01.

Facts: *G. sanchezi* is recognized from the former *G. minutissimum sanchezi*; north-east Mexico, c.900–2,100 m; relatively long tail, six pale tail bars and reduced/finer pale crown flecks. Adults are dichromatic: males grey-brown/greyish olive-brown with pale cinnamon wing markings and whitish tail bars; females are distinctly redder/cinnamon with buff/cinnamon pale bars. Wing-chord samples average 87.3 mm for males (n=6) and 91.0 mm for females (n=5), supporting only a modest female size direction, not a large visual scale gap.

Limits: specimen samples are small and the paper supplies no juvenile feather chronology, flight threshold or nest-interior description.
