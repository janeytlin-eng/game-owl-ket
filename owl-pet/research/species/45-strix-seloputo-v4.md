## Evidence synthesis

Mikkola printed pp.322–323 (PDF pp.324–325) explicitly leave adult sex-size difference unknown and describe a pale fledgling whose face and primaries begin to resemble the adult while its back remains distinctive. The dated Pasir Ris chronology bounds one family at 2–4 days after fledging: about half adult size, downy, branch-walking, balancing by wing flaps, with remiges lengthening over weeks and rectrices still shorter. “Adult-like primaries” is therefore direction/incipient form, not mature length or crisp spot rows. The 2025 Malaysian Bird Report observation is limited to branch support/vulnerability in an age-unknown juvenile.

## Design boundaries

- Adults: near-equal displayed scale, same plumage direction, pose only.
- Juvenile: evidence-bounded 2–4-day post-fledging brancher; half adult scale; prominent down; pale poorly bounded face; distinctive diffuse barred back; short growing primaries; much shorter tail.
- Action: supported balance-walking, both feet gripping, one short wing partly open; not flight.
- Setting: one documented rain-tree/fern family context, not universal nest architecture.
- Unknown: hatch age, universal chronology, independent-flight threshold, sex-specific growth, universal nest geometry.
