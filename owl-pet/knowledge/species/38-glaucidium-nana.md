# 38 Austral Pygmy-Owl — *Glaucidium nana*

## Taxonomy
- Current working name: *Glaucidium nana* (P. P. King, 1827).
- Recorded synonyms/older combinations: *Glaucidium nanum* and *Strix nana*.
- Historically sometimes treated within *G. brasilianum*; current project treatment follows species-level recognition.

## Supported design facts
- 17–21 cm, roughly 55–100 g; female averages can be heavier, but no uniform exaggerated ratio is asserted.
- No ear tufts; yellow eyes; greenish-yellow bill/cere; gray-brown and red morphs plus intermediates.
- Gray-brown adults have pale dorsal spots, whitish eyebrows, off-white underparts with short streaking/mottling, and a tail with numerous thin rufous bars.
- Downy chick is white. Mesoptile has deep yellow, sometimes orange-tinged eyes, an unspotted crown, and less distinct body patterning than adults.
- Supported environment: open woodland/forest edge, thorn shrubs, parks and farmland; Andean-Patagonian range context.
