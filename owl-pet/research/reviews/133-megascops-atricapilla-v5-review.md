## Reusable production rule

For an age-unknown photographed young owl, correct an overlong regular wing by shortening and down-veiling it only to the degree visible in the evidence. Do not convert that visual correction into an exact age, fledging or flight-capability claim. This rule was applied and visually rechecked here; broader validity remains to be tested.
