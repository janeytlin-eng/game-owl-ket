## Evidence summary

Primary Gabon field observations support low open stick platforms about 2.5–3 m above large rivers, one or two circa-40-day young, occasional reuse of another bird's nest, and extremely slow loss of juvenile down. Secondary identification synthesis supports dark eyes, short yellowish bill, fine black vermiculation on brown upperparts, pale scapular accents, pale sandy streaked underparts, weak facial disc, bare lower tarsi/feet, and a pale mesoptile juvenile sometimes washed cinnamon.
