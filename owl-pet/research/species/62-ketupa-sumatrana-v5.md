## Design boundary

- Adults near-equal in visible height and overall pattern; the one-pair adult sex differences in Foley et al. are not treated as a species-wide rule. Pitch-black eyes, large yellow bills, long horizontal brown tufts, brown/buff barred upperparts and fine blackish/whitish underpart barring.
- One unsexed approximately day-27 Johor nestling: able to stand upright and preen but still at the original hatch position. White/down-soft body with conservative fine dark barring beginning to appear; no long layered adult wing, no projected tail, no branch or flight pose. Soil, bougainvillea stems and planter edges provide continuous bilateral support.
- Rooftop resort planter is shown as the documented study site, not a universal natural nest. Adults are nearby educational-composite observers, not claimed to have been simultaneously photographed in this exact arrangement.
- Unknown: exact hatch date, sex, continuous feather chronology, cross-population transfer, adult sex ratios and precise day-27 remige geometry. `scientifically_verified=false`.
