## Evidence actually read this cycle

### `HUH-BC2013` — primary nest monitoring, printed p.175

At one Atlantic-forest nest in a live angico tree fork 15.2 m above ground, protected by three vertical branches and dense epiphytes, the 18 November young had mostly pale-grey body and wings with horizontal black barring, a black heart-shaped mask with white border, yellow bill and legs, and walked actively. On 27 November pale grey still dominated. The description came from pole-camera observations and does not provide exact hatch age, measured wing/tail proportions or a continuous tract schedule.

**Design boundary:** pale-grey-dominant down/mesoptile impression, low-contrast horizontal dark bars, short rounded soft-looking folded wing, very short or mostly concealed tail, dark eyes, yellow bill/feet, both feet on the flat branch/epiphyte nest floor; capable standing, no depicted flight.

### `HUH-MUNOZ-2019` — primary Colombian field record, printed pp.16–17/Figs.3–5

A young found on the ground on 30 August was placed on a low branch while parents attended; by 7 September it was observed making short flights between branches. Exact age, sex and feather dimensions were not measured.

**Boundary:** useful corroboration that an older age-unknown young can perch and later make short branch-to-branch flights, but not evidence for exact first-flight timing. V5 deliberately uses the more directly described 2010 nest stage and does not depict flight.
