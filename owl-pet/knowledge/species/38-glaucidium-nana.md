## v4 research correction — 2026-10-30

**AUPO-MARKS-2018 — Marks et al., JRR 52(1):118, DOI 10.3356/JRR-17-29.1.** Full correction letter read. The 15–17 d incubation repeated in Ibarra 2014/2015 was unsupported because laying and hatching were not dated at the same nest. Supported evidence is 26–28 d in captivity and 27–29 d at three wild nest boxes; repository working range corrected to 26–29 d. This is not a fledgling-age proxy.

**AUPO-IBARRA-2015 re-read, printed p.36 / PDF p.8, Fig.5.** Supports a branch-perched fledgling photographed 11 Feb 2013, but not exact age, full feather sequence, flight threshold or independence. V4 uses that supported posture/stage and does not encode an exact one-month age. Science remains false.
