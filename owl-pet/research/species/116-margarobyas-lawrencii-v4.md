## Fresh external research actually read

1. Kirkconnell & Wiley (2017), *Cotinga* 39:10–23, especially printed pp.11, 13–15, Table 1 and Table 2. Their 2006–12 field programme examined active breeding cavities of Bare-legged Owls in Bermeja and Mera-Molina. The species is recorded as a breeding cavity-nester in seasonally inundated forest with sabal palm savanna; loss of standing cavity-bearing palms reduced nesting habitat. This supports a natural standing-palm cavity environment but not the internal cavity geometry or juvenile feather sequence.
2. Suárez (2022), *Bulletin of the British Ornithologists’ Club* 142(1):10–74, printed pp.59–60. More than 30 modern individuals and fossil material showed substantial size/osteological and external variation across Cuba; the western `exsul` subspecies was treated as invalid. The paper also confirms historical `Gymnoglaux` records map to `Margarobyas lawrencii`. This blocks a rigid subspecies morph or a universal sex-height ratio; it does not provide sexed living-body measurements or juvenile plumage.
3. Existing repository records remain the only actually-read source for the broad older mesoptile description: broadly adult-like but less spotted above, sienna upperparts/breast and paler belly. No source supplies exact age or flight ability.
