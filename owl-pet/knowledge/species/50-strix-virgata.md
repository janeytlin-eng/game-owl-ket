# 50 · Mottled Owl · *Strix virgata*

## Scope
- Current working name: *Strix virgata* (Cassin, 1849); historical combinations *Ciccaba virgata* and *Syrnium virgatum* retained.
- Planned stage: adult male, adult female, and fuzzy-white cavity juvenile documented in a 2016 Yucatán field observation.
- Status: VERIFIED production archive; not scientifically verified.

## Evidence actually read
1. The Owl Pages: medium owl; dark-brown upperparts flecked/barred pale, pale-buff facial disc, dark eyes, whitish/buff underparts with brown streaking; natural tree-hole nest; breeding biology explicitly little known.
2. Animal Diversity Web (University of Michigan): 1–2 eggs; tree holes/broken palms or old nests; current/older *Ciccaba* treatment noted.
3. Backyard Nature field observation, Hacienda Chichén, Yucatán, 12 June 2016: photographed fuzzy-white young in tree hollow, later both parents nearby; supports a cavity-bound fuzzy juvenile without precise age.
4. Avibase/current list: current *Strix virgata* mapping; historical name conflict retained.

## Design boundaries
- Use a cavity-bound fuzzy-white juvenile from the documented stage; do not claim exact age, flight ability or continuous feather sequence.
- Adult dark morph direction for the locality: dark brown mottling, pale buff facial disc, dark brown eyes, whitish-buff underparts with vertical brown streaking; no ear tufts.
- Female may be fuller but no exaggerated standing-height difference because accessible sex measurements were not cross-verified.
- Generic humid Yucatán/Central American forest cavity; no exact subspecies claim.

## Unknowns
- Exact juvenile age and feather sequence, sex-specific linear measurements, and subspecies-level morphology.
