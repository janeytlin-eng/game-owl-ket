# #161 Blakiston's Fish Owl — *Ketupa blakistoni*

## Recognition card
- Enormous fish owl; conspicuous sideways-slanting tousled ear-tufts.
- Faded lemon-yellow eyes; gray to bluish-gray cere/bill.
- Ashy/tawny-brown face without a strongly pale facial-disc rim; small white throat patch.
- Brown upperparts with dark shaft streaks; restrained buff/yellow wing and 7–8 tail bars.
- Pale gray-yellow-brown underparts with long dark shaft streaks and fine wavy cross-bars.
- Cream-feathered tarsi, bare lead-gray toes, dark claws.

## Sex treatment
Sexes are alike in plumage. Show the female fuller/heavier only; do not impose a fixed height ratio. Hayashi's small reported mass ranges overlap despite a higher female upper range.

## Juvenile treatment
Use the documented approximately 50-day late-nestling stage: smoke/gray-brown fluffy mesoptile, dark facial mask, short tail, soft rounded developing wings and a supported cavity crouch. Do not render a miniature adult, crisp adult barring, long hard remiges, a flight pose or unsupported feet.

## Habitat and support
Large natural broadleaf cavity in riverine broadleaf/mixed forest near a partly ice-free stream is supported. Keep each adult gripping a broad branch and the nestling's belly/feet supported on cavity debris.

## Science status
Concept-science calibration remains pending. Unknowns include exact 50-day flight ability, continuous juvenile feather sequence, quantified universal sex ratio and individual/subspecies palette.
