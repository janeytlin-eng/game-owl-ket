Native Cycles rendered and the workflow archived original 500×500 front / 45° / side / back PNGs, plus a 1,000×1,000 contact sheet. Native import logged **one mesh and 6,660 polygons**. Opened and visually inspected both the real Blender four-view contact sheet and the service's own browser viewport screenshot.

**Art gate: ART_REJECTED.** Front and 45° views are an angular, collapsed wedge/fan, with large flat stretched facets, not a round fluffy chick. The side view looks like an elongated wedge with hard/shattered polygon mesh. The rear resembles tightly stacked broken plates or scales rather than tiny natural mesoptile feather fibers. **There is no believable heart-shaped facial disc, open owl eyes, beak, compact supported legs, short hidden wings or tree hollow.** The mesh is pale only because Blender's untextured default appearance under neutral lighting; it has **no cream-white fur/groom/PBR materials**. It is materially farther from the v3 concept than Run139's rejected textured 193k-face owl. Zero images, textures, material slots, and animations are structural evidence against presentation quality.

Given this categorical failure at the **front/side/back morphology** gate, no official mobile WebGL test was performed; a browser provider showing a mesh in desktop Three.js proves only that its preview rendered, not phone GPU performance, product touch interaction, or pet animation.
