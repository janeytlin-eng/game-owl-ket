## Design boundary

- Adults: large orange-rufous fish owls, messy lateral ear tufts, yellow eyes, weak facial discs, coarse dark crown/underpart streaks, dark tail with pale narrow bands, black bill. Tarsus feathering is visible only high/front; lower tarsus and toes are clearly bare yellow. Adults near-equal height; female only subtly fuller.
- Juvenile: approximately 40-day Wuling nestling, unsexed; nearly adult-sized but lower, down-softened head/torso, smaller lateral tufts, developed rounded folded juvenile wings with irregular tawny/dark pattern, and a conspicuous developing tail softer than adults.
- Support/action: calm daytime rest or preen, fully supported in humus/epiphyte-filled old-tree Y-fork; no flight or branch-launch.
- Unknown: exact feather geometry, continuous sequence, first-flight threshold, sexed display ratio and transfer beyond Wuling.
