- **URL:** https://www.cepf.net/sites/default/files/udsm_ngezi_biodiversity_survey.pdf
- **Read:** printed pp. 54–55 (web extraction lines 2862–2892).
- **Facts:** calls were recorded throughout the night, especially sundown to midnight, from mid-canopy; owls occupied dense-foliaged forest, clove plantation, mangrove and graveyard forest patches. The January survey explicitly recorded no breeding activity.
- **Decision:** dense Pemba mid-canopy context is supported; no nest, eggs or breeding-stage reconstruction.
- **Limits/confidence:** strong for this January survey and sites; absence of observed breeding is not proof of species-wide breeding absence.

### PESO-FUCHS-ETAL-2008-PMC2483963 — phylogeny and old-name correction (new, peer-reviewed original research)
- **Title/authors/year:** “Tracing the colonization history of the Indian Ocean scops-owls…”, Jérôme Fuchs et al., 2008, BMC Evolutionary Biology 8:197.
- **URL/DOI:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2483963/ ; https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2148-8-197
- **Read:** abstract/results and Table 1.
- **Facts:** six-locus analyses place *O. pembaensis* close to mainland *O. senegalensis*, despite older treatment with or near *O. rutilus*; estimated Pemba colonization split 1.7 mya (95% HPD 0.7–3.3).
- **Decision:** preserve *O. rutilus pembaensis* only as a historical crosswalk and avoid borrowing Malagasy morphology.
- **Limits/confidence:** phylogeny/taxonomy only; not a plumage, sex or developmental source.

## Evidence-to-design boundary

### Adults
- Near-equal height and compact build; no blanket female-larger rule.
- Same warm russet/light-brown morph direction for both sexes; no sex-coded colour.
- Short relaxed tufts, yellow adult irides, fine mottling/barring and pale scapular accents.
- A slightly longer-looking male folded wing is allowed only as a subtle guide-derived cue, not a body-size gap.

### Young
- Depict **AGE-UNKNOWN MESOPTILE**, not hatchling and not claimed fledgling.
- Pale whitish/creamy-buff down with vague yellow-brown barring; white-barred, unstreaked crown; dark/indeterminate iris.
- Short soft rounded partly formed wing, mesoptile-dominant contour, short somewhat banded supported tail.
- Stationary on a broad branch with both feet supported; no flight claim.

### Habitat and nest
- Dense leafy Ngezi/Pemba mid-canopy is supported.
- No nest, hole, eggs or breeding behaviour: the field report observed no breeding during its January survey and no source here establishes nest geometry.

## Unknowns retained

- wing/flight chronology;
- nest location, substrate and geometry;
- robust sex-specific body-size rule;
- downy-chick appearance.
