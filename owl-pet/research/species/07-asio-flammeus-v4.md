## Correction and design boundary

The previous v3 index row `SEO-MARTINEZ-CLIMENT-2000` misattributed DOI 10.1093/condor/102.1.216. The correct authors are Beatriz E. Arroyo, Thomas De Cornulier and Vincent Bretagnolle. The old stable ID is retained historically but deprecated; v4 uses the corrected ID above.

V4 chooses an approximately 14–16-day ground-walker: outside the scrape and moving through grass, but explicitly unable to fly. This stage is supported by COSEWIC's 12–18-day walking interval and the primary paper's 10–15-day onset of secondary differences. The design does not reproduce an unviewed sex-specific secondary pattern. Adults remain near-equal in displayed height; the female is only subtly fuller/darker because colour and measurements overlap.

Mikkola describes yellow-eyed mesoptile, while the Canadian synthesis describes juveniles with brownish eyes. V4 therefore uses conservative dark amber/yellow-brown transitional eyes and records the conflict rather than declaring one universal colour.

Unknown: exact individual day/sex/subspecies, iris transition, detailed secondary pattern, feather metrics, first-flight threshold, universal female:male ratio and universal nest geometry.
