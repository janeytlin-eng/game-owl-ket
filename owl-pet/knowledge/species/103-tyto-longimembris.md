## Identification and stage boundary
Adults have dark eyes, narrow ivory-horn bills, dark-brown/buff/yellow-orange upperparts with fine silvery spots and very long legs whose distal tarsus/toes are bare. Male is paler/white below; female is warmer buff/chestnut and only subtly fuller in the plate. The center young is an approximately 55-day Australian captive-reference mesoptile: rich golden-brown powder-puff down, partial pink bare face, dark eyes, pale bill and short down-covered developing wing buds. It is not a wild-age reconstruction or flight claim.

## Habitat/nest boundary
Australian institutional evidence supports tall grass, sedge, swampy plains and ground nesting in trodden grass/tunnels. The scene is an unlined grass scrape; no cavity or basket.
