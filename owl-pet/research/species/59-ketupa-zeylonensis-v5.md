### BFOW-HKBWS-AVIFAUNA-2026
- **Title / institution / access:** Brown Fish Owl species account, *The Avifauna of Hong Kong* / Hong Kong Bird Watching Society; read 2026-11-05.
- **URL / section:** https://avifauna.hkbws.org.hk/species/0190/027400 — Identification, Distribution & Habitat Preference, Breeding; web page has no page numbers.
- **Facts:** adults 48–55 cm, loose floppy ear tufts, usually yellow eyes, greyish bill, long unfeathered legs and pale-yellow feet; grey-brown head/mantle with heavy black-brown streaks; white throat; pale buff-brown underparts with fine crossbars and dark streaks. Some juveniles are paler on mantle/coverts/scapulars, have sparser/narrower breast streaks and smaller ear tufts. Hong Kong birds use well-wooded sites near water. Seven 2009–2021 nest sites included roofs/balconies, ledges, buttress roots and a natural hollow; observations distinguish downy chicks, unfledged juveniles, first flights and post-fledging dependency.
- **Limits/conflicts:** synthesis from Hong Kong records, not a measured individual feather series; date ranges overlap and cannot assign this plate an exact age; image-light angle affects apparent iris colour. It supplies no sexed linear measurements.
- **Design use:** adult identity, bare legs/feet, locally plausible waterside woodland and paler/smaller-tuft juvenile phenotype. It does not support an exact age or a universal nest form.

### BFOW-KFBG-2021 — re-read
- **Title / author / institution/date:** “What secrets can we discover at a Brown Fish Owl’s nesting site?” / Aiko Leong / Kadoorie Farm and Botanic Garden / 1 October 2021.
- **URL / exact sections:** https://www.kfbg.org/en/KFBG-blog/post/What-secrets-can-we-discover-at-a-Brown-Fish-Owls-nesting-site ; lines 45–64 and the two photo captions; no page numbers.
- **Facts:** one New Territories pair reared one young; the captioned nestling had well-developed feathers shortly before nest departure, then became more mobile and explored branches near the nest. The nest-area material included wood and bark fragments.
- **Limits:** no hatch date, sex, measured feather lengths or controlled flight test. One tree nest does not describe all nest forms.
- **Design use:** one age-unknown, well-feathered, just-mobile brancher with bilateral branch support immediately beside the tree nest; no flight claim.

### BFO-INDIANBIRDS-2013-P147-151 — re-read
- **Citation / URL:** Raju Vyas, Kartik Upadhayay, Mital R. Patel, Rahul D. Bhatt & Pritesh Patel, “Notes on the breeding of the Brown Fish Owl *Ketupa zeylonensis*,” *Indian BIRDS* 8(6):147–151. https://indianbirds.in/pdfs/IB_8.6_HighRes.pdf
- **Facts:** 15–20-day chicks were active and fluffy-faced with smaller body/wing feathers; small ear tufts developed later. Near nest departure, the fledglings resembled adults and were almost adult-sized, stayed 4–6 days in the same tree canopy and remained parent-dependent. Both parents fed young; observers differentiated the pair by size when together.
- **Limits:** Gujarat *K. z. leschenaultii* sequence does not date the Hong Kong individual. The article gives no universal visual size ratio; “fledgling” and leaving the nest do not prove sustained flight capability.
- **Design use:** formed folded wing and short-to-medium developing tail are compatible with an older dependent young bird, but keep residual down, irregular juvenile pattern and supported branch action. Adult size difference is restrained to subtle fullness only.

### BFOW-BINDU-BALAKRISHNAN-2015-P97-98 — re-read
- **Citation / DOI:** T. N. Bindu & Peroth Balakrishnan, “Observations on the Breeding of the Brown Fish-Owl *Ketupa zeylonensis* in Kerala, Southern India,” *JBNHS* 112(2):97–98. https://doi.org/10.17087/jbnhs/2015/v112i2/104938
- **Facts:** nine nesting attempts reused a mango-tree crevice 5.2 m above ground within 30 m of a pond; nestling period 43–49 days (mean 46±3, n=3).
- **Limits:** a small Kerala sample and one repeatedly used site; no day-by-day feather plate. Do not assign the Hong Kong young bird a day-age or universalize the crevice/nest material.
- **Design use:** broad older-nestling timing context only; KFBG/HKBWS control locality and visible stage.

## Design boundary

- **Unknown:** exact age/sex/subspecies, continuous feather chronology, first-flight competence, quantitative sex-size ratio and transfer among Hong Kong, Gujarat and Kerala populations.
