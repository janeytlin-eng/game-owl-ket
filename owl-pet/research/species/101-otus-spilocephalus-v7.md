## Evidence read

The Taiwan Ministry of Agriculture bird guide, drawing its text from the second edition of *The Avifauna of Taiwan*, describes a roughly 20 cm owl with **small ear tufts**, yellow adult iris, pale-yellow bill, feathered tarsi, bare yellow-brown toes, rufous-brown flight feathers and a complex but non-regular ventral pattern. It places the race in dense low- and mid-elevation broadleaf vegetation and notes concealed daytime perches. The Taiwan Encyclopedia of Life repeats the small-tuft diagnosis and credits Lin Wen-Hong and Liu Hsiao-Ju / *The Avifauna of Taiwan* (updated 2013-05-10). The National Taiwan Museum record independently supports alike adult plumage, adult yellow eyes, cream-yellow bill, yellow-brown juveniles with vague markings and natural tree hollows. Mikkola printed p.148/PDF p.150 and the 2005 forestry report remain the evidence for a fluffier, low-contrast older juvenile and branch-supported fledglings that mostly flap/walk with weak flight.

## Actual-image problem and correction

The reopened v6 plate preserves the reviewed short, down-dominant juvenile wing and full branch support, but both adults carry conspicuously long horn-like tufts. That conflicts with the new official small-tuft cross-check and even the v6 prompt's own “avoid oversized ear tufts” boundary. V6 also paints the older fledgling with the same bright yellow irides as adults even though the species-specific sources read here do not establish juvenile iris chronology.

V7 therefore shortens the adult tufts to low, modest triangular tufts, keeps adult eyes yellow, and uses subdued dark-neutral juvenile irides so that an unresolved trait is not presented as confirmed. This is a conservative design treatment, not a claim that juvenile eyes are dark at a known age.

## Limits

Exact hatch age, juvenile iris chronology and sex, continuous feather-tract sequence, first-flight threshold, quantified adult dimorphism and universal hollow dimensions remain unknown. Institutional pages summarize the Taiwan race and are not a substitute for a longitudinal development series. V7 remains `scientifically_verified=false`.
