## Sources actually read

- `HUH-MUNOZ-2019`: Muñoz-García et al., *Spizaetus* 28:15–20, printed pp.17–19, Figs.2–5. A chick found on the ground on 30 August 2019 was returned to a low *Carapa guianensis* branch; both parents attended and delivered prey. On 7 September the same young was in the canopy and made short flights between branches.
- `HUH-BC2013`: Bodrati & Cockle, *Ornitología Neotropical* 24:169–182, reused for the documented juvenile progression: pale-gray body/wings with black horizontal bars, black heart-shaped mask with white border, yellow bill/legs, and increasing black through late November.
- `HUH-MIK2013-340`: uploaded Mikkola guide, printed pp.340–341/PDF342–343, reused for adults: round head, no ear tufts, sooty-black plumage with fine white wavy lines, black chin/bib, dark brown iris with pink eyelid rim, yellow bill, feathered tarsi and bare ochre toes.

## Design inference and unknowns

The center bird uses the older fledgling stage because direct field evidence supports branch perching and short flights. Developed folded wings and a short tail are necessary, but exact length and feather geometry were not measured. Pale gray remains dominant; adult-like bright white wing-spot rows are excluded. Both feet visibly grip one branch. Exact age, sex, first-flight date, continuous feather sequence, fixed adult sex-size ratio and universal nest/habitat choice remain unknown.
