# 163 · Mountain Pygmy Owl · *Glaucidium gnoma*

## Taxonomy
- Current IOC v15.2: Mountain Pygmy Owl, *Glaucidium gnoma*.
- Historical broad Northern Pygmy-Owl literature may include now-split populations; v1 uses current Mountain-specific sources only.

## Verified facts
- Mikkola printed p.375 / PDF p.377: 15–17 cm, 48–73 g; tiny round untufted owl; females larger/heavier on average; yellow eyes, horn-yellow bill, pale brows, fine crown speckles, pale-spotted back, nape false eyes, short pale-barred tail, plain throat/lower-breast center and streaked/mottled sides. White downy chick; mesoptile unspotted gray crown and grayer cere.
- Owl Research Institute: same-height adult sexes with female heavier/browner direction; montane conifer/oak forest and old woodpecker cavities. Its broad spotted-head/dark-bill young note is not merged with the mesoptile stage.
- Owl Pages: corroborates adult, montane pine/pine–oak habitat and breeding uncertainty.

## V1 boundary
- Adults stand at equal height; female only subtly fuller/browner.
- Young is age-unknown mesoptile: gray fluffy, unspotted gray crown, grayer cere, short soft folded wing/tail, fully cavity-supported, no flight.
- Exact age, iris, continuous feather sequence, flight ability and cavity geometry remain unknown; science pending.
