# 201 Mantanani Scops Owl — *Otus mantananensis*

- Scope: Philippine subspecies *O. m. romblonis* for the juvenile stage; current species retained.
- Historical mapping: *Otus romblonis*; *Otus scops romblonis*.
- Adult boundary: small island scops owl; gray/rufous-brown population variation, short tufts, pale dark-rimmed face, yellow eyes, gray bill; adults near-equal because no sex-size direction was found.
- Juvenile boundary: identified age-unknown Semirara *romblonis* portrait; fluffy tawny-brown fine barring, reduced tufts, pale yellow-ochre eyes, gray bill, compact down-covered wing, short concealed tail and complete support.
- Habitat scene: lowland/foothill forest edge; no invented nest.
- Unknown: exact age, flight ability, continuous feather sequence, nest geometry, quantified dimorphism, population-wide juvenile generality.
- Sources: [research card](../../research/species/201-otus-mantananensis-v1.md) and [source index](../../research/SOURCE_INDEX.md#2026-10-28--201-mantanani-scops-owl--otus-mantananensis-v1).
- Image: [concept](../../design/concepts/201-otus-mantananensis-v1.png).
- Scientific verification: false.


## v3 correction — 2026-11-07

- Adult sex scale: near-equal; voice pitch does not establish body size and Mikkola reports no sexual-size data.
- Adult diagnostics: grey-brown/intermediate morph, short irregular tufts, dark ruff, yellow eyes, greyish-horn bill, pale scapular row, streaked/barred pale underparts, heavily feathered tarsi.
- Juvenile boundary: exact nominate juvenile is unknown. Marshall's fully grown-young record supplies stage occurrence but not plumage. The Photo Ark *romblonis* juvenile is cross-population only. V3 uses an older, branch-supported, rounder bird with compact soft wing/tail and does not claim age, sex or flight ability.
- Habitat: sea-level forest/wooded edge with coconut and Casuarina cues; no invented nest.
