## Manual visual QA from real browser screenshots
All twelve full-size PNGs were extracted from the actual remote artifact. A 3×4 montage (baseline/matte/unlit × front/right/rear/rear-lit) was opened and visually checked, not just inferred from CI green status or SHA. The **baseline rear is almost completely black**. Matte PBR restores cream colour at all angles and the face pigment remains visible, with 90° sides and full rear coherent in 3D space. Unlit makes the bird brighter but flatter. Even the visually best matte comparison is **ART_REJECTED**: front has enormous button/cartoon eyes and hard heart emblem rather than natural feathers; side is a smooth, wingless-looking pear with an awkward protruding eye and no believable buried short wing; back is largely a blank shell with sparse coarse strands, not layered cream mesoptile; legs and toes do not convincingly grip an old eucalypt tree hollow. Most down remains stiff, scratchy and insufficiently soft. Technical improvement ≠ release approval. This diagnostic does **not** produce a new or qualified 3D pet.
