Inside the Trellis 2 Generator

The details that make this generator more than a quick demo.

True 3D Reconstruction

The model infers the unseen sides of your object and builds real geometry — not a flat card with a depth trick.

Adjustable Face Count

Pick 5K, 10K, 20K, 30K, or 40K faces per mesh. Keep polygons low for games and AR, or go high for print-quality surfaces.

Fast Turnaround

A warmed-up run typically lands in 15–60 seconds. Only the first generation of the day may need a couple of minutes to wake the engine.

Standard GLB Export

GLB opens everywhere: Blender, Unity, Unreal, Three.js, iOS Quick Look, and most slicers for 3D printing.

Built-In AI Image Editor

A second tab lets you edit images with text prompts — restyle, relight, or clean up an image before turning it into 3D.

Live 3D Preview

Rotate and zoom the generated model right on the page, so you know exactly what you're downloading before you click.
