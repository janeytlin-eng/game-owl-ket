## Four-view visual comparison and rejection

**Run81 ART_REJECTED:** pointed cone/egg head, jagged angular colored face and disconnected looping edge tubes at oblique/profile view, plastic round dot eyes, hard sparkly short fibres, exposed ankle spheres, suspended feet and no plausible down-covered wing architecture. Compared with Run80 it removes the detached disk, but that alone does not make a believable juvenile owl.

**Run82 ART_REJECTED:** rounded crown and removed floating face loops are genuine corrections; rear/far-view shelf winding is fixed. However the total body now reads as a hanging egg/teardrop; the natural heart facial ruff is **almost absent**, eyes appear as glued black dots, feet seem detached and unsupported under the pointy belly, the side/back have no believable short wing or age-appropriate feather layout, and the cream grooming looks mottled, sparse and brittle rather than dense soft mesoptile. Browser WebGL snapshots reproduce the failures. **No sample is fit for presenting as a high-quality owl-pet character.**

The narrow test result: replacing the rigid face plate with a material-only mask removes one defect but also loses the hallmark heart-shaped ruff; a stronger *genuinely sculpted organic cranial/cheek/brow structure* with actual groom and plausible young-owl anatomy is still needed. Neither simple procedural lofting nor accumulating short filament polygons is acceptable. Need references appropriate to the age and independent right/back anatomy plus proper asset/phone validation; do not treat the single owner concept as proof.
