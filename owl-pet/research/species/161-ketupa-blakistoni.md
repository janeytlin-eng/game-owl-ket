# #161 Blakiston's Fish Owl — *Ketupa blakistoni*

## Taxonomy
- Current project/IOC name: *Ketupa blakistoni* (Seebohm, 1884).
- Historical combination used by Mikkola and older literature: *Bubo blakistoni*.
- The IOC taxonomic update explicitly records the genus transfer.
- Scope remains the project's fixed 251 extant species; this card does not recalculate checklist totals.

## Evidence facts
- Adult total length 660–685 mm in Hayashi's cited sample; male mass 3150–3450 g, female 3360–4600 g.
- Sexes are similar in plumage. Adult identity includes massive build, sideways-slanting tousled ear-tufts, faded lemon-yellow eyes, grayish bill/cere, ashy-brown upperparts with dark shaft streaks, pale gray/yellow-brown underparts with dark shaft streaks and fine wavy cross-bars, a white throat, 7–8 pale tail bars, cream-feathered tarsi and bare lead-gray toes.
- Natal down is white; a near-black mask develops from about 10–12 days; mesoptile is smoke-brown/gray with faint transverse spotting.
- Hayashi's Photo 2 directly shows an approximately 50-day late nestling. Usual fledging is 48–60 days, with one cited 69-day case.
- Habitat is riverine broadleaf/mixed forest or moist taiga near rivers/lakes/marshes; open water that remains ice-free is important. Natural nests are generally very large broadleaf-tree cavities; nest boxes and rarer alternatives also occur.

## Design decision
Use a supported approximately 50-day cavity nestling, not an unverified hatchling. It may have formed developing wings because it is near the usual fledging window, but wings remain shorter/rounder/softer than adults, with fluffy mesoptile, a short tail and diffuse—not crisp adult—pattern. Do not claim flight. Adults share plumage; female is fuller/heavier but not assigned an exaggerated fixed height difference.

## Unknowns / conflicts
- The small sex mass ranges overlap, while Mikkola summarizes females as averaging more than 1 kg heavier. Direction is supported; a universal visual ratio is not.
- Exact 50-day flight skill, toe exposure, subspecies palette and continuous juvenile feather chronology are unknown.
- The selected natural cavity is evidence-supported habitat context, not a claim of one standardized geometry.
