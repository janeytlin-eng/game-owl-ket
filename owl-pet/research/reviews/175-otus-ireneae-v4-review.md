1. **Initial adult tufts were too conspicuous and sharp.** Mikkola p.134 describes small ear-tufts. Edit 1 reduced and rounded both adults’ tufts. Full image and both adult crops confirm low-profile tufts without erasing the scops-owl silhouette.
2. **Initial juvenile wing rows were too crisp/adult-like.** The guide supplies no exact feather chronology and only says juvenile resembles adult. Edit 1 shortened, rounded and softened the folded wing. Juvenile crop confirms a developed but softer, shorter wing with full branch support.
3. Adult size/morph and juvenile ability were rechecked against the evidence boundary; no female-larger rule, sex-linked morph, exact age or flight claim was introduced.
