## Genuine distinct material-and-geometry experiment
Rather than repeat the opaque needle geometry from Run207 or the volumetric extrusion from Run214, removed the old needle geometry from the *actual, unchanged Run207b 3D negative-control sculpt*. Sampled points and normals on the native continuous 3D head/body mesh; built thousands of spatially oriented and curved crossed feather-card surfaces (each with multiple row segments); made a new procedural **raster RGBA wispy-fiber texture** and a glTF2 PBR `alphaMode=MASK` material. This uses real glTF triangle meshes and GPU-compatible alpha-mapped raster textures, **not** SVG, 2D illustration or a claimed finished model.

- **Run215:** 4,580 sampled actual surface roots, 9,160 physically oriented cards, 106,868 triangles; exported and independently reimported native 3,115,180-byte GLB, SHA256 `1b0eac493ba70ca0eeb30cb0813d1b86f5c209a24bb274a5302d2f736099eaa5`.
- **Run215b:** attempted a genuine densification / shorter-and-wider fiber revision, 5,984 roots, 11,968 cards, 123,716 triangles; new 3,767,836-byte GLB, SHA256 `b2faae7e6a3b2f504f068686e4c9cc8a407e8eed16642edf033633d492e81c2a`.

Both GLBs were reimported by Trimesh, each with 21 geometry groups, then **actually** imported by VTK glTF importer and rendered via software OpenGL/Xvfb with seven different 3D camera poses (front/back/left/right/quarter/top/bottom): 14 full PNGs, two seven-view contacts. ## Visual gate — explicit art refusal
The change makes the fur **somewhat less like opaque little sticks**, but still looks like rigid bristles, little speckles and a wiry hairline, not soft down; Run215b density is not a convincing improvement. The retained underlying sculpt is decisively wrong: glued-on hard heart badge, protruding round toy eyes, fake feet and circular wood torus, overgeneric oval/egg sides and back with no anatomically credible feather zones. This is neither recognizable high-quality fluffy juvenile *Tyto* nor a natural, supported hollow scene. **ART_REJECTED. No true mobile hardware/WebGL benchmark was conducted** and no current rendering pass is misrepresented as phone acceptance. Since aesthetic failure is decisive, neither GLB is approved or published.
