## Evidence boundary

Uploaded Mikkola printed pp.189–190 / PDF pp.191–192 was directly re-read this run. It supports no sex-size direction, broad morph variation, yellow eyes, pale greyish-black bill/cere, strong feet with distal tarsus bare 6–9 mm, and a fluffy adult-like juvenile with narrow head/crown/neck bars and fainter spots. Sangster et al. (2013) is newly mapped for adult Moluccan-complex morphology and confirms partly bare tarsi and stronger irregular wing/upperpart pattern. The 1875 Keulemans/Sharpe *Scops leucospilus* plate remains the only juvenile comparator used, but it is historical art, not a dated field series.

V3 depicts the northern-Moluccan `leucospilus` reference so the paler adult palette and historical juvenile plate are not mixed with the nominate Seram palette. Historical *Scops leucospilus* maps to current *Otus magicus leucospilus*; Lombok birds formerly assigned to broad *O. magicus* are current *O. jolandae* (#139) and are excluded.
