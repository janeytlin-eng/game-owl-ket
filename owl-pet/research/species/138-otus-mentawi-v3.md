## Source facts vs design inference

- **Source facts:** adults about 20 cm; rufous morph lacks a nuchal collar and has whitish marked scapulars, herringbone/ocellated underparts, brown eyes, yellowish-horn bill and feathered tarsi. Juveniles observed in May 2017 were slightly smaller, pale greyish-brown and thin-streaked below, darker above, reddish-faced, reddish-brown-eyed and pale greyish-horn-billed/toed; their wings extended beyond a short dark tail.
- **Design inference:** a compact three-character branch composition and low village-edge rambutan setting translate field context into the established game-concept format.
- **Unknown / not asserted:** fixed female-larger ratio, exact juvenile age, independent flight capacity, complete growth chronology, universal nest site and exact palette.

4. Preserve v2's useful near-equal adults, secure support and age-unknown caution; do not invent a nest.
