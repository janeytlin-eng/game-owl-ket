# 54 · Spotted Eagle-Owl · *Bubo africanus*

## Scope
- Current working name: *Bubo africanus* (Temminck, 1821).
- Planned stage: adult male, adult female, and a supported 4–6-week exploratory juvenile on a low rock beside the nest scrape.
- Status: VERIFIED production/archive; scientific calibration remains pending.

## Evidence actually read
1. SANBI species page: typical gray-brown/yellow-eyed/fine-barred identification, open scrub–savanna–grassland–rocky habitat, juvenile hiss and bill-click behavior.
2. Biodiversity and Development Institute 2022 account: sexes alike in colour and apparent size; juvenile resembles adults but has weaker ear tufts and retained fluffy down; young begin leaving/exploring at about 4–6 weeks; broad nest-site range.
3. Ansara-Ross et al. 2008, DOI 10.2989/OSTRICH.2008.79.1.11.366: among measured traits, only tarsus length differed significantly between sexes in their *B. africanus* sample; do not force generalized female-larger standing scale.
4. SABAP1 species text, PDF p.1: ground scrapes, tree cavities/stick nests, and cliff/building cavities or ledges are all recorded; open/man-made habitats are used.

## Design boundaries
- Adults: medium-sized eagle-owl with conspicuous ear tufts, yellow eyes, pale ochre facial disc with dark edge, gray-brown mottled upperparts, off-white lowerparts with fine brown barring. Keep standing height near equal; labels, not invented plumage dimorphism, distinguish sexes.
- Juvenile: 4–6-week exploratory stage, fluffy pale gray-buff down still obvious, small developing ear tufts, short soft wing and tail feathers; low stable rock support beside a shallow sheltered scrape. It may stand/crouch and look alert but must not fly or display adult-length layered remiges.
- Dry southern African rocky savanna edge with sparse grass and shrub shelter; no dense rainforest.
- Do not claim exact age, sex, subspecies, or a universal ground-nesting rule.

## Unknowns
- Exact continuous feather sequence, regional/subspecies variation, and precise sex-specific standing dimensions remain unresolved.
