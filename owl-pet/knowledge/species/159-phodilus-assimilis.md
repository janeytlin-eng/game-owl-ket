## Design decisions for v1
Adults: near-equal-sized Sri Lankan bay owls with very large dark eyes, pink-washed heart-shaped facial disc, short rounded wings, dark-brown upperparts densely spotted white and black, and conspicuously barred wings and tail. The overall palette is darker and browner than #158 *P. badius*. No unsupported stable visible sex difference is added.

Juvenile: age-unknown chick at a natural tree-cavity rim, fully supported by the cavity edge, with soft pale down, a simplified developing heart face, short rounded developing wings and a very short tail. No exact day-age, flight ability or mature remiges are asserted.

Habitat: humid Sri Lankan evergreen forest, with the plate kept simple enough to preserve the three-bird comparison.

## Review and archive
The first plate was inspected and the juvenile's visible wing was too long, layered and adult-spotted. A targeted edit replaced it with a short rounded, down-covered developing wing; the final was re-opened and passed the evidence-bounded concept review.
