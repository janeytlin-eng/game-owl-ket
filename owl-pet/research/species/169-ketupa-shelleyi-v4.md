## Sources actually read or reread

1. **SHEO-BOUET-1961-P619** — Georges Bouet, *Oiseaux de l'Afrique tropicale (deuxième partie)*, ORSTOM/Larose, 1961, printed p.619, lines 4907–4912. https://horizon.documentation.ird.fr/exl-doc/pleins_textes/divers17-09/43696.pdf
   - Museum-based account under *Bubo shelleyi* / *Huhua shelleyi*: a sex-indeterminate Paris specimen had wing 450 mm, tail 242 mm, tarsus 80 mm and bill 57 mm; grey-white bill, slate-grey cere, pale claws blackish at their tips, and dark iris. Adults of both sexes are described with uniformly blackish chocolate-brown crown, ear tufts, mantle, back and wing coverts except for a patch of white feathers on the posterior crown.
   - Design support: add one small, partially concealed white posterior-crown patch to both adults; retain dark iris, heavy pale bill and slate-grey cere. The single specimen measurements do not license a sex-size ratio.
   - Limits: historical museum synthesis, not living-colour photography or a population sample; the white patch may be partly hidden by angle/feather posture and must not expand into Fraser-like white scapular patches.
2. **SHEO-TOBIAS-WILLIAMS-2022-P73-79** — Tobias & Williams, 2022, *Bull. ABC* 29(1):72–79, especially pp.73–75, Figs.1–3. https://www.tobiaslab.net/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Tobias-Williams-2022-Shelleys-Eagle-Owl.-Bull-ABC.pdf
   - Reread field photograph and NHMUK comparison: enormous bulky dark owl; dark face/eyes/eyelids; heavy pale horn bill; shaggy blackish tufts; dark uniform upperparts without conspicuous Fraser-like white scapular patches; broad barred underparts, narrow flight-feather bars, short tail, mossy forest branch.
   - Limits: unsexed adult observation; no juvenile sequence or sex-size inference.
3. **SHEO-KIRSCHEL-ETAL-2022-P221-222** — Kirschel et al., 2022, *Bull. ABC* 29(2):221–222. https://www.labsehgal.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Kirschel-et-al.pdf
   - Reread direct 2005 Cameroon observation: very large, overall dark brown, heavily dark-barred underparts, dark eyes with eyelids paler than the irides; perched about 50 m high in mature primary forest for 20–30 minutes.
   - Conflict/design use: preserve eyelid colour as variable/uncertain rather than forcing either pink or uniformly black; v4 uses dark neutral lids with only a restrained cooler rim.
4. **SHEO-MIKKOLA-2013-P288** — repository page record only: chick undescribed; mesoptile brown/white barred with adult-like remiges and rectrices.

## v3 problem → v4 evidence boundary

- Actual review of v3 found the center mesoptile still carried a long, hard, fully layered adult-like folded wing and a long fully exposed barred tail. The sources support formed remiges/rectrices, not adult-complete proportions or flight ability.
- V4 keeps recognizably formed, softly barred juvenile remiges/rectrices but shortens and softens the folded-wing contour, hides most of the short tail behind the branch, and gives the bird a low two-foot-supported crouch. No exact age or flight claim is made.
- Bouet's adult account adds a small white posterior-crown patch. It is not a shoulder patch and must not be expanded into Fraser's Eagle-Owl patterning.
- Adults remain near equal in skeletal/apparent scale. Sexes are alike; no quantified dimorphism is available.
- No nest is drawn because a species-specific nest remains unknown in the read sources.
