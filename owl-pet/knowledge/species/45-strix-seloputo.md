# 45 · Spotted Wood Owl · *Strix seloputo*

## Scope
- Current working name: *Strix seloputo* Horsfield, 1821.
- Concept stage: adult male, adult female, recently fledged juvenile.
- Scientific status: concept only; not scientifically verified.

## Evidence actually read
1. Mikkola 2013, *Owls of the World: A Photographic Guide*, printed pp. 322–323 (PDF pages 324–325), species 149.
   - Adult: 44–48 cm; no ear-tufts; sex size difference unknown; coffee-brown upperparts with black-edged white spots/bars; orange-buff facial disc, dark-brown eyes, greenish-black bill/cere, feathered legs/toes.
   - Juvenile: natal down whitish; mesoptile upperparts mainly barred white and dark brown; fledgling is very pale, with face and primaries beginning to resemble adult while the back remains distinctive.
   - Habitat: mangroves/coastal forest, plantations, cleared forest, urban parks/villages.
2. BirdLife DataZone current species factsheet (accessed 2026-10-13): retains *Strix seloputo* and a very large resident/breeding range; used for current-name and range cross-check only.
3. xeno-canto current species page (accessed 2026-10-13): follows IOC taxonomy and retains *Strix seloputo*; used only as a current-name cross-check.

## Design boundaries
- No stable adult visual dimorphism or measured sex-size difference was found; male and female remain nearly equal in standing scale, with only pose/body-language variation.
- Juvenile is a recently fledged mesoptile, not a newborn. It may perch with clear support. It stays paler and softer than adults; back barring can be present, but long layered adult remiges/tail are forbidden.
- Nominate Southeast Asian direction is used; no claim of exact locality or subspecies reconstruction.
- Scientific unknowns: exact fledging age, sex-specific linear measurements, continuous feather sequence, and locality-specific plumage.
