## Evidence comparison

- EGE species account: young leave at about 28–32 days while major flight feathers remain incomplete; family group persists.
- Vassen juvenile photograph: supports only broad juvenile visual cues; exact age and sex are absent.
- Sova Dinova 2024 sample: adult female mean wing/tail/mass exceeds male means, but distributions overlap and external juvenile sex is unreliable.

## Limitations
