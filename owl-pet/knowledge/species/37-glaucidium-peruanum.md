# 37 Peruvian Pygmy-Owl — *Glaucidium peruanum*

## Taxonomy
- Current working name: *Glaucidium peruanum* König, 1991.
- Other English name: Pacific Pygmy-Owl.
- Historical treatment: formerly included in *G. brasilianum*; retained here as a monotypic species.

## Supported design facts
- 15–17 cm, 58–65 g; females only slightly larger and about 5 g heavier, so no exaggerated sexual-size difference.
- No ear tufts; yellow eyes; greenish-yellow bill/cere; brown, gray, and red morphs occur.
- Brown morph has a finely light-spotted crown, pale dorsal spots, bold longitudinal underpart streaking, and a relatively long tail with broken pale bars.
- Downy chick is white. Mesoptile resembles the adult but has a plain, unspotted crown.
- Western-Andean riparian thickets, mesquite, semi-arid woodland, cactus/thorn scrub, parks, and agricultural areas are supported habitats.
