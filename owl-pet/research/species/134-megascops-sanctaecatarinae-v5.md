## Design decisions versus unknowns

Source facts: variable brown/grey/rufous adult morphs; coarse/scaly breast; voice critical; tuft length and iris colour not diagnostic; age-unknown charcoal-grey juvenile visible at a cavity; old-name mapping; riparian/recovering and southern Atlantic Forest contexts.

Design inferences: near-equal adult scale because available sex evidence overlaps; distinct adult poses to avoid cloned silhouettes; one coherent cavity/Araucaria-edge habitat plate; juvenile upper body and support only. These are conservative presentation choices, not biological findings.

Unknown and not claimed: population sex-size ratio; exact juvenile age/sex/hatch date; full juvenile wing/tail/foot anatomy; fledging/flight/independence; iris chronology; identity of any depicted individual; whether the illustrated cavity is an active nest.
