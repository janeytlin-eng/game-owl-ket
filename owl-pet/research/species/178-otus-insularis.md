# #178 Seychelles Scops Owl — *Otus insularis* v1

- **Project ID:** 178
- **Action:** NEW
- **Current scientific name:** *Otus insularis* (Tristram, 1880)
- **Historical name:** *Gymnoscops insularis* (protonym; not used for current project naming)
- **Science status:** not scientifically verified

## Evidence summary

Mikkola printed p.138/PDF p.140 was read and viewed. Adults are practically earless, yellow/green-yellow eyed, streaked/mottled and fully bare-legged; females are more than 20 g heavier. A 2002 primary biometric study independently supports larger/heavier females and no sex-linked colour morph. A 2004 field study documents tree-cavity nests 7–25 m high and 4–6 week fledging from two nests, but not detailed juvenile plumage.

## Design boundary

- Same-plumage adults; female clearly but moderately fuller/heavier.
- Juvenile only as an older adult-like but softer bird, neutral-dark shaded iris, fully supported beside a simple cavity opening.
- No invented downy chick, nest lining, exact age, iris chronology or flight threshold.
- Bare tarsi and long toes must be visible but anatomically natural.
