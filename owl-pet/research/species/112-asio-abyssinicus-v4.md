## Source facts versus design decisions

- Source fact: nominate adults have broad/fused dark marks and dense irregular underpart panels; sexed series does not support a plate-wide visible size rule.
- Design decision: retain near-equal adult crown height, orange eyes, black bills, flexible central tufts, feathered tarsi and irregular—not tiled—dark/ochre pattern.
- Source fact: the photographed juvenile has loose charcoal/gray-brown down, short emerging tufts and concealed tail on natural support.
- Design decision: use an age-unknown older perched juvenile with broken wispy clumps and ragged down edges; forbid repeated ringlets, curls or cord-like strands; keep developing wings compact/mostly buried and tail hidden; do not imply flight.
- Source fact: Dinsho headquarters is dry Afromontane forest with *Hagenia*, *Juniperus* and *Hypericum* and is a reliable observation locality.
- Design decision: one continuous moss/lichen-covered *Hagenia/Juniperus*-like branch in a cool Dinsho highland forest; no nest, eggs, prey or feeding scene.
