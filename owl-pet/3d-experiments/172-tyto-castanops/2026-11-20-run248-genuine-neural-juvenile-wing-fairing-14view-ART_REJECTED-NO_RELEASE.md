## Actual multi-view art verdict: **REJECTED**

Opened the corrected 14-view browser image and directly compared A/B front, side, oblique, rear, top and underside to the **far-right cream mesoptile**, not to a mature Tyto. There is a small real difference in torso and wing boundary but *not enough to accept the model*. The folded side wing still forms an elongated, layered **adult remige slab**, nearly reaching the tree platform. Rear is a stiff mostly featureless white oval without convincing multi-layered down. Face is recognizable as heart-shaped, but disproportionately rigid, flattened, and with recessed stylized eyes. Body posture is too tall, relatively narrow and upright; no short, wholly hidden infant wing, no concealed tail. Cream material looks crumbly/painted, not fluffy down. The Y-up eucalypt ledge remains a scalloped beige cookie/stone plinth, not a weathered true natural hollow with broad safe foot contact. Variant B does not truly correct these flaws despite more genuine vertex adjustment. Neither A nor B should be labelled better artistic baseline or displayed as finished pet.

The software rendering demonstrates that real GLB geometry loads but **cannot demonstrate iPhone/Android FPS, GPU memory, alpha overdraw or touch stability**. No physical phone measured. Therefore **TRUE_NATIVE_3D_PASS / SOFTWARE_WEBGL2_PASS / ART_REJECTED / PHONE_UNTESTED / NO_RELEASE**. All checked native outputs are research-only; none advertised to the user as sample.
