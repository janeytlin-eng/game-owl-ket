### RAJ-MARSHALL-1978-P20
- **Title / author / year:** *Systematics of Smaller Asian Night Birds Based on Voice* — Joe T. Marshall, 1978, Ornithological Monographs 25.
- **URL:** https://sora.unm.edu/sites/default/files/journals/om/om025.pdf
- **Type / accessed:** museum-series/systematic monograph; accessed 2026-11-04.
- **Scope / page:** *Otus brookii*, adults; printed p.20, PDF text lines 1397–1420.
- **Facts:** exceptionally soft plumage; sparse enlarged black pattern; deep fuscous upperparts; whitish inner ear-tuft webs and hindneck collar; yellow iris; heavy tarsal/basal-toe feathering.

- **Design use:** soften/irregularize adult patterns and toe feathering; three collar suggestions stay broken/subdued.
