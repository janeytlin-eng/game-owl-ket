## Iteration lesson

Problem: earlier revisions treated one Palmchat nest event as the dominant juvenile context and lacked an actually viewed older-juvenile reference. Applied change: separated the two events and generated v5 with a well-feathered but still down-softened older juvenile; retained near-equal adults. Verification: full image plus crops pass without edit. Rule added: when a direct field image supports a later juvenile, do not erase legitimately formed wing/tail feathers merely to avoid adultification; instead reduce pattern crispness, keep stage labels non-numeric and preserve visible support.
