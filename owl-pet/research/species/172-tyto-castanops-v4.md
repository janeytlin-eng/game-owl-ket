## Newly read and indexed

### TMO-DEBUS-1993-P169-172-187
S.J.S. Debus. 1993. “The Mainland Masked Owl *Tyto novaehollandiae*: a Review.” *Australian Bird Watcher* 15(4):168–191. Full PDF read at https://afo.birdlife.org.au/afo/index.php/afo/article/download/768/751 .

- Printed p.169, Plate 24: captive Tasmanian dark-morph juvenile male beside a much larger adult female; feathered legs visible.
- Printed pp.171–172: Tasmania dimorphism index 12.8; cited mean masses 525 g male and 850 g female. This supports strong reverse sexual size/mass direction, not a 1.62× standing-height rendering.
- Printed p.187: Tasmanian young may leave the nest hole while still downy, and downy tips may persist on ventral feathers for months.
- Limits: the paper is primarily a mainland review; the Tasmanian values and fledging note synthesize Mooney sources/personal communication. It does not provide exact hatch age, a continuous castanops feather series, flight threshold or universal body-height ratio.

## Reused / re-read evidence

- **TMO-YOUNG-BELL-MOONEY-2021-P13-18:** juvenile/adult female and adult male roost ecology; forest–pasture/riparian mosaic, mature eucalypt hollows; the juvenile was aged mainly by behaviour and was near independence, so it does not define a downy stage.
- **TMO-DEBUS-WOOD-2007-P49-52:** nominate captive sequence; ~5-week bird thickly downed with developed mask and no visible feathers; cream castanops comparison is indirect. Supports morphology boundaries but not an exact castanops age label.
- **TMO-MIKKOLA-2013-P110:** repository-preserved reading only: large Tyto, female larger/darker, two down coats, dark eyes, feathered legs and powerful feet.

## Problem → evidence → v4 change

- **Problem:** v3 is scientifically conservative but static; repeated stationary cavity poses underuse the documented ability boundary.
- **Evidence:** Debus 1993 p.187 explicitly permits a downy Tasmanian young bird at/just outside the nest-hole boundary, while not proving flight or exact age.
- **Change:** retain age-unknown cream down and concealed tail, but show one low cautious step from a broad hollow lip. Both feet visibly contact connected wood surfaces; wings stay compact, folded and down-buried.
- **Adult scale rule:** strong female mass/wing direction becomes markedly fuller/heavier and only moderately taller; never translate 525 g versus 850 g into proportional standing height.
