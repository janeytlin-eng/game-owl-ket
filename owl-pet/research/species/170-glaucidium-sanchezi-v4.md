## Sources actually read or reread

1. **TPO-HOWELL-ROBBINS-1995-P16-18-T2** — S.N.G. Howell & M.B. Robbins, “Species Limits of the Least Pygmy-Owl Complex”, *Wilson Bulletin* 107(1):7–25, 1995, pp.16–18, Table 2. https://sora.unm.edu/sites/default/files/journals/wilson/v107n01/p0007-p0025.pdf
   - Examined 151 specimens across the complex, including 13 *G. sanchezi*. Females are redder than males; adult male wing mean 87.3±1.4 mm (n=6), female 91.0±2.4 mm (n=5), significantly different. Six pale tail bars, reduced/fine crown flecks, relatively long tail; males more olivaceous/less rufous, females cinnamon-brown/redder. Bold white scapular/wing spots belong to *G. parkeri*, not this species.
   - Design: female may show only a subtle longer-wing/fullness direction, not a giant overall body; sex colour difference is evidence-based. No juvenile inference.
