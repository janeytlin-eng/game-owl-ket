- **Historical names:** *Pisorhina solokensis* Hartert, 1893; species protonym *Scops brookii* Sharpe, 1892
- **Science status:** not scientifically verified / PASS_WITH_LIMITATIONS target
- **Preserved:** v1 and v2 concepts, generated masters and user history remain unchanged.
- **Accessed:** 2026-11-07

## Why v3

The v2 evidence table correctly noted that Marshall’s few specimens varied in colour and size, yet its prompt still encoded “female subtly fuller.” That is not a demonstrated sex-size result for this species. V3 removes every fixed sex-size cue: the adults must be near-equal and may differ only in posture. V3 also narrows the juvenile statement to an older, branch-supported Sumatran mesoptile and does not convert one photographed orange-eyed stage into a universal iris-development rule.

## Sources actually read or re-read

1. **RAJ-GOODDIE-2015-P70-81** — Gooddie 2015, *Forktail* 31:70–81, direct PDF opened and account read at printed pp.74–75 / extracted lines 616–639. The presumed *solokensis* adult at Danau Ranau had blackish crown versus rufous face, white supercilia/ear tufts and pale yellow-orange iris. The natural cavity was 2.5 m high in an old 5 m tree, c.50 cm across, with soft mossy material, at c.1,300 m. This source describes no juvenile.
2. **RAJ-MARSHALL-1978-P20** — Marshall 1978, *Ornithological Monographs* 25, direct SORA PDF opened at printed p.20 / lines 1408–1420. Exceptionally soft plumage, deep fuscous upperparts, nearly white inner ear-tuft webs/collar, yellow iris, heavy tarsal and basal-toe feathering are supported. The author explicitly could not verify the Sumatran subspecies from the small, individually variable series. The preceding statement that females are larger applies to the *bakkamoena* group, not *O. brookii*; it must not be transferred.
3. **RAJ-ITIS-686780-1063383-2026** — ITIS report re-opened 2026-11-07: TSN 686780 lists *O. b. solokensis* as a valid subspecies and *Pisorhina solokensis* as synonym/original combination; no morphology or development.
4. **RAJ-AVIBASE-8F4D4FC5-2026** — Avibase taxon page read 2026-11-07; current concept is the Sumatran montane-forest subspecies, protonym *Pisorhina solokensis*, type locality Solok/Padang Highlands. Taxonomy/distribution only.
5. **RAJ-FLICKR-VINCE-2019** — Sumatra field photograph page by Vince_Adam Photography opened 2026-11-07. Adult visual metadata supports dark brown body, blackish crown, white ear tufts, thick underpart streaks and orange iris. The accompanying text notes juveniles of nominate *brookii* are undescribed; it is not a developmental series for *solokensis*.
6. **RAJ-MIK2013-P168** and **RAJ-BOWMAN2014-JUV** — reused repository evidence. The uploaded-guide page was not available to reopen in this execution; its prior page-specific transcription and the previously viewed Kerinci Seblat field photograph support an older reddish-brown, fine-cross-barred, branch-perched mesoptile with small tufts and formed soft wing/tail. Exact age, sex and flight ability remain absent.
