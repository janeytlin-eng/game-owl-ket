## New evidence actually read

### MOR-NZBO-HANZAB-2025 — new, institutional synthesis

New Zealand Birds Online species account PDF, 295_Morepork, https://www.nzbirdsonline.org.nz/assets/95597/1725854541-295_morepork.pdf , development section actually read 2026-10-29.

The synthesis reports wing/dorsal pins bursting from day 8, tail pins visible day 10, quills bursting day 19, wing spotting visible day 24, and at one month a feathered dorsal surface with downy head/underparts. New Zealand fledging is 38–40 days; fledglings have a short tail and downy head/underparts and are about 90% adult weight. These are compiled from several historical/captive/field sources, not one continuous universal series. Strong for choosing a recent-fledgling structural boundary, not exact artwork age.

### MOR-DOC-2026 — new, official species account

New Zealand Department of Conservation, “Morepork/ruru,” https://www.doc.govt.nz/nature/native-animals/birds/birds-a-z/morepork-ruru/ , accessed 2026-10-29. Chicks are mainly attended until fully feathered and fledge around 37–42 days. Official broad synthesis; no feather-tract detail.

## Reused/corrected evidence

- MOR-STEPHENSON-MINOT-2006, primary Mokoia Island study, *Notornis* 53:308–315, pp.308–311: two measured chicks; white natal down becomes dark grey by day 15, pins/brown fluff around day 16, white spots/stronger claws by day 25, one fledged day 39; leaf litter/wood dust in cavity. Small sample.
- MOR-TEARA-2009: white then brown down; tree forks/epiphytes/hollows; young leave at 35–40 days and remain with adults. Caption/source only, image not reproduced.
- Correction: v2 intentionally used the early grey-down/pin stage. V3 selects the separately well-supported recent-fledgling stage, so functional wings and a short tail are required while head/neck/underparts remain fluffy. This is not an admission that v2 was fabricated; it is a better-evidenced different stage.
