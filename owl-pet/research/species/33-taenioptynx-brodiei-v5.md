## Design boundary

- Adults use definitive grey plumage, not rufous as a fixed adult sex/morph.
- Adult male and female are near-equal in displayed height; female only subtly fuller.
- Adult male rear turn exposes paired nape false eyes; female remains front/three-quarter to avoid redundant staging.
- Centre bird is explicitly the documented 56-day fully grown fledgling: fewer crown/back markings and incomplete occipital pattern.
- Wings are formed but compact and folded; tail is short. No claim of first flight, agility or independence.
- All birds are naturally supported in a Taiwan forest setting. Nest context remains distant and non-expository.

## Unknowns retained

- First-flight timing and exact flight performance at 56 days.
- Continuous wing/tail feather-growth measurements.
- Transfer of Taiwan developmental timing to all subspecies/populations.
- Individual sex of the documented fledgling.
- A precise universally applicable female:male visual ratio.
