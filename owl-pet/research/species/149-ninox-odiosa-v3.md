### NBBOO-GOULD-SHARPE-1884-TEXT — newly read historic specimen account

Gould & Sharpe, *The Birds of New Guinea and the Adjacent Papuan Islands*, vol. 1, species account “Ninox odiosa,” issued 1884, public-domain PDF at https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/cf/The_birds_of_New_Guinea_and_the_adjacent_Papuan_islands_-_including_many_new_species_recently_discovered_in_Australia_%28IA_birdsnewguineaa1goul%29.pdf , accessed 2026-11-07. OCR lines 4186–4304 were read. The account is based on a sexed adult male collected 30 July 1879 and figured life size. It describes pale chocolate-brown, nearly uniform upperparts; conspicuous irregular white scapular/greater-coverts marks but only a few smaller white spots elsewhere; dull umber head profusely narrow-barred brownish white; a broad white streak over each eye; chocolate ear-coverts with darker bars and whitish shaft-lines; white throat with black-tipped side feathers; predominantly white underbody with narrow brown center-streaks; chocolate breast sides mottled white; yellow iris, ash bill, drab feet. Total length 10 in, wing 6.4, tail 4.2, tarsus 1.3. This corrects v2's overly generalized “broad dark chest band / pale mottled lowerparts” boundary.

The same account says the species was then known only from New Britain and distinguishes it from *N. punctulata*. It provides no female comparison, nest, juvenile plumage or development.
