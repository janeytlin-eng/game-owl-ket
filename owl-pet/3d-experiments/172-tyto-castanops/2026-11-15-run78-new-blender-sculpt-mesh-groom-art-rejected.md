## **ART_REJECTED — NO PUBLIC SAMPLE**

Visual inspection against the user’s actual rightmost fluffy child-owl crop finds fundamental remaining failures:

1. Heart mask has 3D surface contact in Run78b but still resembles a rigid ornamental face shield with protruding concentric mechanical eyes; facial shaping remains unconvincing especially in true profile.
2. 12,500 curved down ribbons look like straw/bristly needles rather than dense, soft, short cream second down; the feather silhouette is bright but materially wrong.
3. Body remains mostly two round bulges with **no credible short, down-hidden wing-fold or rear feather/tail anatomy**, in side/rear and in the WebGL screenshot.
4. Three-toe and tarsal bases appear artificial and overexposed, and the wavy narrow hollow support does not read as broad old eucalyptus hollow lip.
5. One front illustration cannot substantiate the invented hidden-view anatomy; no independent appropriate-age reference, genuine mobile GPU, production-standard PBR or rigged animation gate.

**Do not present these two GLBs as successful electronic pet prototypes and do not deploy.** Further local micro-warps or more cards are unlikely to meet the threshold. The new independent free Blender geometry+groom pipeline **does run**, which removes the previous tool-access blocker; true credible manual/assisted juvenile sculpt+groom and real phone GPU visual/performance checking are still needed.
