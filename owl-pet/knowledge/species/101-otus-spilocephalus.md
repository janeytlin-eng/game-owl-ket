# 101 · Mountain Scops Owl — *Otus spilocephalus*

## Current representative

- Current version: [v6](../../design/concepts/101-otus-spilocephalus-v6.jpg)
- Design scope: Taiwan subspecies *Otus spilocephalus hambroecki*
- Historical names: *Ephialtes spilocephalus*, *Ephialtes Hambroecki*; *Otus stresemanni* appears in older taxonomic treatments.
- Scientific status: concept reviewed, not scientifically verified.

## Evidence facts

- Adults of the Taiwan race are described as alike in plumage; no quantified sex-size ratio was recovered.
- Yellow iris, cream-yellow bill, short tufts, tea/rufous-brown bark-like plumage, feathered tarsi and bare pink-brown distal toes are supported by Taiwan museum/government records and Mikkola printed p.148.
- Mikkola describes an older juvenile as duller reddish-brown and fluffier, with narrow dark crown bars, broader back bars and very faint underpart bars.
- A Taiwan forestry report records late-June fledglings on branches next to natural-cavity nest trees, mostly flapping/walking with weak flight.

## Design inference and limits

V6 depicts one age-unknown older fledgling beside a natural hollow. It retains compact formed but down-dominant wings and visible two-foot branch support without adult-regular remex rows or a long tail. Exact age, juvenile iris/sex, continuous feather sequence, first-flight threshold, quantified adult dimorphism and universal hollow dimensions remain unknown.
