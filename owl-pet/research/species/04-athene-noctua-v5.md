## Fact / inference boundary

- Fact: adult is small, flat-headed and tuftless, yellow-eyed, brown with pale brows, pale upperpart spots and streaked pale underparts; sexes are similar-plumaged and biometric overlap is substantial.
- Fact: c.25–30-day birds may leave or move around the cavity while flight is limited and parental dependence continues.
- Fact: juvenile wing coverts are browner with a smaller buff patch than adults.
- Design inference: three birds on one broad cavity-adjacent branch/ledge; center chick low and fully supported, with down-softened body and short rounded developing wings. This is not a measured individual or fixed posture.
- Unknown: exact day, sex, iris change, primary/rectrix length, daily feather tract, first successful flight, standing height ratio and subspecies transfer.
