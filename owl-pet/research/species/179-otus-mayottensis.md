## Verified adult boundary

Mikkola printed p.139 / uploaded PDF p.141 was read and viewed: 24 cm, about 120 g, short tufts, mottled grey-brown adult, contrasting nuchal collar, sparse pale scapular areas, strongly streaked/barred throat and vermiculated underparts, yellow eyes, greyish-horn bill, feathered tarsi and pale toes. Female wing is slightly longer, but no quantified whole-body dimorphism is established.

eBird's species account and visible adult photographs corroborate a compact dark owl with small tufts, brown upperparts/underparts, rare rufous morph and use of remnant rainforest, secondary forest, gardens and plantations. Fuchs et al. (2008) support a distinct Mayotte lineage; Avibase preserves the original subspecific name.

## Unknowns and blocked decision

The guide explicitly records “Juvenile Unknown”. Targeted searches under the current and old names found no verified species-specific juvenile plumage, dated developmental stage, soft-part chronology, flight threshold or nest-stage image. The Rasmussen et al. 2000 paper remains an unverified lead because the original full text could not be opened in this run.

Therefore no male/female/juvenile board was generated. Do not borrow a juvenile from *O. rutilus*, *O. capnodes*, *O. pauliani* or another Indian Ocean scops-owl. Revisit only after a species-specific source supports a renderable juvenile stage.
