# 47 · Brown Wood Owl · *Strix leptogrammica*

## Scope
- Current working name: *Strix leptogrammica* Temminck, 1832.
- Concept stage: adult male, adult female, mesoptile juvenile.
- Status: concept image v1 generated, edited, reviewed and archived; not scientifically verified.

## Evidence actually read
1. Mikkola 2013, printed pp.326–327 (uploaded PDF pp.328–329), species 151: 34–45 cm, 500–1100 g, wing 286–400 mm; female clearly larger and at least 200 g heavier; no ear tufts; dark eyes, greenish-horn bill, cinnamon/rufous nuchal collar, rufous/chestnut breast band and thin dark barring below. Natal down light rufous-buff; mesoptile very pale rufous/ochre-buff with faint russet bars.
2. The Owl Pages juvenile photograph metadata: a wet young *S. leptogrammica* photographed in Assam, India, supports a branch-perched young bird as a possible supported posture, but supplies no exact age.
3. Samarawickrama 2003 nesting-ecology publication record for Sri Lanka was located, but the full text was not retrieved; it is retained only as an unverified lead and does not support morphology.

## Boundaries
- Female mass difference does not establish a precise standing-height ratio.
- Use a described mesoptile, not an invented day-age.
- Historical *newarensis* material is excluded from the prompt because modern checklists may treat Himalayan Owl separately.
- Exact locality/subspecies, continuous juvenile feather growth and nest architecture remain unknown.
