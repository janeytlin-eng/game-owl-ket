## Scientific boundary

- Adult female-average wing/eighth-primary/tail/mass direction is supported by `SCOPS-BOANO-ETAL-2020`; overlapping ranges prohibit a fixed display ratio, fixed crown-height difference or colour cue. The two adult crown heights therefore remain close and the female is only subtly fuller/longer-winged.
- Juvenile sex difference was not significant in the cited biometric sample. No juvenile sex is assigned.
- The juvenile is an age-uncertain recent fledgling/brancher. Exact age, feather-tract chronology, first-flight threshold, sustained-flight ability and subspecies are not claimed.
- The Mediterranean pine setting is a plausible presentation environment, not a claim that all populations use it.

## Iteration rule

Morphometric mean differences with broad overlap may guide a subtle directional cue only. They must not be converted into a universal male/female height ratio, plumage colour mapping or juvenile sex marker. A formed juvenile wing can be retained, but its contour and markings must remain visibly fresher, narrower, shorter and down-softened relative to adults.

## Remaining unknowns

Exact juvenile age, sex, subspecies, feather-tract chronology, first-flight threshold and population-wide transfer of the selected nestling/fledgling evidence remain unknown. Repository integrity review does not convert this concept into scientific approval.
