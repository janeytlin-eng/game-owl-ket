## Supported design boundaries
- Small, compact, round-headed pygmy-owl without ear tufts; yellow eyes; gray-brown crown/nape, richer brown upperparts, pale-barred tail, pale underparts with reddish-brown streaking, and dark nape false-eye marks.
- No stable visually exaggerated adult sex difference is supported by the sources read; male and female remain near-equal in standing scale, with posture rather than invented plumage separating them.
- Juvenile reference is age-unknown: retain a mottled gray head and notably plainer/unmarked back from the 2008 paper; use short, soft wing/tail development and natural branch support. Do not label a day-age or render a downy nestling.
- Environment: humid lowland/foothill tropical evergreen or mature secondary forest; no nest reconstruction is attempted because no directly read nest-stage source supports it.
