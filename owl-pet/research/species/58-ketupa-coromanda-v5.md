- **Title/authors/year:** “Breeding of the Dusky Eagle Owl *Ketupa coromanda* in Telangana, and its status in southern India” — Sriram Reddy & Gopalakrishna Ramachandran, 2023.
- **URL:** https://indianbirds.in/pdfs/IB_19_2_HighRes.pdf
- **Type/pages:** primary field report, *Indian BIRDS* 19(2):44–47; official journal PDF read at printed pp.44–47.
- **Facts:** adults and a large dry-stick nest were c.20 m high in a tall *Dalbergia paniculata* beside dry-deciduous reserve forest. On 13 March 2021 a camouflaged juvenile attempted to move among neighbouring branches while the parents watched; the authors estimated it at 8–10 weeks from appearance and behaviour. Both observations were near a large waterbody, well-wooded forest and rice cultivation. Mating behaviour identified the female, which appeared slightly larger than the male.
- **Limits:** the age is a field estimate, not a known hatch date; branch movement is not a controlled flight test; one behaviourally sexed pair does not establish a universal display-height ratio; the paper supplies photographs but no continuous feather-tract measurements.
