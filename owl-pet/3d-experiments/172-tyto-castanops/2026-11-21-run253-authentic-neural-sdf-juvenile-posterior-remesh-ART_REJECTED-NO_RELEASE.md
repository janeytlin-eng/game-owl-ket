## Actual no-fee native 3D experimentation, not sprites or simple geometric assembly

Contrary to previous Run248–252 continuous vertex deformation, this pass **actually reconstructs the original Hunyuan learned owl core volumetrically**, based on its filled native voxel occupancy and true 3D signed Euclidean distance, with a spatial posterior clipping field, blended Gaussian SDF surface regularization, and new Marching Cubes continuous 3D surface. The original learned eye geometry, PBR pupil materials, chest/front, feet, existing down and support were retained. The original 61,085 true down vertices were repositioned relative to the *new* remeshed surface. Learned neural RGBA surface colors were transferred using a 12-neighbor interpolated 3D proximity kernel (nearest-neighbor transfer initially caused stronger contour bands). No primitive sphere body was added, no SVG or camera sprite substituted, and no free asset with unclear/no-AI license was imported.
