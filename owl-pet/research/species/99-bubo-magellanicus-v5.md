Article/full text read via https://www.researchgate.net/publication/342751803_First_Report_of_a_Magellanic_Horned_Owl_Buho_magellanicus_Nesting_in_a_Balcony_Novoa_y_Blanco on 2026-11-06. Figure 1A identifies two nestlings at about 20 days; the balcony contained nesting material, and the pair ultimately fledged two young after roughly 41 and 52 days in the nest.
