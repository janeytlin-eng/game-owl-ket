# #160 Minahasa Masked-Owl — *Tyto inexspectata*

## Taxonomy
- Current project name: *Tyto inexspectata* (Schlegel, 1879).
- Old combination: *Strix inexspectata* Schlegel, 1879.
- Scope: Sulawesi endemic. The next unused project number #160 is allocated here; fixed project total remains 251.
- *Tyto multipunctata* is deliberately not allocated because the existing source index records unresolved treatment under the project’s fixed checklist scope.

## Evidence facts
- Adult diagnosis: rufous facial disc/forecrown, dark eyes, cream bill, gray-brown to golden-rufous upperparts with restrained white/dark spots, ochre barred wing/tail, pale ochre finely spotted underparts.
- Legs: ochre tarsal feathering to the toe bases; distal toes bare gray-brown/reddish-gray, claws dark.
- Sex: secondary synthesis says female larger; no universal standing-height ratio or robust sample available.
- Juvenile: Cornell’s account describes browner wings and speckled gray underparts; a secondary synthesis reports fledged juveniles fed by parents. Exact age, iris, feather sequence and flight threshold are unknown.
- Habitat: closed Sulawesi forest understory; lianas, ferns, palms and epiphytes. Nest geometry is not treated as established.

## Design decision
Use an age-unknown dependent fledgling with formed but softer/browner wings, gray speckled underparts, residual down and two-foot branch support. Do not depict hatchling anatomy, an exact age, a flight pose, or a claimed cavity nest. Adults are near-equal in displayed height with the female only subtly fuller.
