### Source synthesis and taxon boundary

- **Guide:** Mikkola printed p.148 / uploaded PDF p.150. Species 17–21 cm, 50–112 g, wing 129–152 mm; short ill-defined tufts; no sexual-size data. Adults have dark tawny/reddish-brown upperparts, black vermiculation, pale rufous crown spotting/barring, a bold pale-and-black scapular row, barred reddish-brown flight feathers/tail, whitish reddish-barred underparts with paired triangular black/white spots, golden/green-yellow eyes, light horn bill and tarsi feathered to the toe bases. Juvenile is duller reddish-brown and fluffier, narrowly barred on head/crown, more broadly on back and only faintly below.
- **Chosen race:** `O. s. hambroecki` from Taiwan only. The guide says it is slightly darker/more rufous and has no rufous morph. The National Taiwan Museum says sexes share plumage; adult eyes yellow, bill cream-yellow, tarsi feathered and toes pink-brown; juvenile is more yellow-brown with vague marks that darken/sharpen with age; natural tree hollows and low/mid-elevation primary broadleaf-forest edge are reported.
- **Taxonomy:** Avibase recognizes current `O. spilocephalus hambroecki`, Taiwan range, and original protonym `Ephialtes Hambroecki`. The broad species board is explicitly calibrated to this one race to avoid mixing geographically variable forms.
- **Unknowns retained:** exact age, juvenile iris, detailed remex/tail chronology, flight threshold, nest lining and quantified sex size. Juvenile eye remains neutral-dark in shade and both feet remain supported. Scientific verification remains false.
