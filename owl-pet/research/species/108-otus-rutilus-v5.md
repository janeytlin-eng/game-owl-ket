## Evidence actually read

### RUT-MIKKOLA-2013-P143-144 — uploaded photographic guide, freshly reopened

- Title/author/year/type: *Owls of the World: A Photographic Guide*, 2nd ed.; Heimo Mikkola; 2013; photographic guide.
- Library location read: printed pp.143–144; uploaded PDF pp.145–146, including page images.
- Facts: length 19–22 cm, mass 85–120 g; small ear tufts, yellow eyes, grey-brown bill/cere, feathered tarsi to toe bases, short tail relative to Torotoroka, three colour morphs, and humid rainforest/brush from lowlands to about 1800 m. The account says females average 18 g heavier, but does not provide a standing-height ratio. It explicitly says juvenile unknown.
- Limits/design use: use compact adult structure and one same-morph pair; female mass direction may be shown only as subtle fullness. Because the guide says juvenile unknown, it cannot supply an age, feather timetable or locomotor ability for the centre bird.

### RUT-BENSON-COLEBROOK-WILLIAMS-1976 — primary specimen series, newly read

- Title/authors/year/type: “A Contribution to the Ornithology of Madagascar”; C. W. Benson, J. F. R. Colebrook-Robjent & A. Williams; 1976; field/specimen study, *Oiseau et la Revue Française d’Ornithologie* 46:103–134.
- URL: https://protectedareas.mg/content/documents/95a20108-b580-4f0b-8c45-00bcec5dd791/59df4e079a9d49ac9ddcfc6a5d21633a.pdf
- Location read: parsed original PDF lines 825–846 in the systematic account; the retrieval did not expose the exact printed journal page for this subsection.
- Facts: two males were recorded, one at Périnet/Andasibe and one at Sakaraha; both had yellow irides, and the Sakaraha male weighed 107 g. Twelve western-savanna specimens tended paler/greyer/less tawny than nine humid-east specimens, while overlap and exceptions were explicit; the strongest rufous tendency occurred in the central and northern humid east.
- Limits/design use: the paper predates the present east/west split and mixes populations now treated separately. It supports yellow eyes and a deeper/rufous eastern direction only as a tendency. It supplies no female measurement, juvenile stage or universal colour diagnostic.
