## Evidence boundary and conflicts

- Current genus/name is fixed by Cornell/ITIS. MNHN's 2011 “Now: *Ninox*” is retained as historical chronology, not treated as a current conflict.
- Adult visual direction is bounded by the holotype, contemporary media and secondary description: earless compact owl; rusty-brown upperparts with irregular pale marks, gray-brown face/narrow pale brow, pale throat/chest and mostly plain cream belly. Eye descriptions range yellow to orange, so the plate uses restrained amber-yellow without sex/age coding.
- No species-specific juvenile development sequence, exact juvenile iris chronology, fledging age, flight ability, quantified sex-size direction or nest contents was found. The centre bird is an **age-unknown older juvenile design inference** with support and intentionally compact wing/tail; it is not scientific reconstruction and science status remains false.
- The v1 local export decodes but does not match its manifest byte/SHA record. It is preserved and excluded as a visual source.
