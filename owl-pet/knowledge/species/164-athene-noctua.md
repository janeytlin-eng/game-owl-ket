# #164 Little Owl — *Athene noctua*

- Region: nominate/western European farmland/orchard; no desert or Central Asian subspecies mixing.
- Adult: small flat head, no ear tufts, yellow eyes, pale brows, gray-green bill/olive-gray cere, brown pale-spotted upperparts, pale dark-streaked underparts, pale-feathered tarsi and bristled toes.
- Sex: female may be heavier but overlap is strong; near-equal displayed height, subtle fullness only.
- 27-day pre-fledging chick: brown/dirty soft down and fluffy diffuse mesoptile, pale/yellow-gray eyes, less distinct spotting, short soft wing/tail, slightly feathered toes, fully supported at cavity threshold; no flight or independence.
- Prohibit: adult hard remiges/regular spot grid on chick, flight pose, unsupported feet, fixed dimorphism, mixed subspecies palette.
- Science remains pending. [Research](../../research/species/164-athene-noctua.md)
