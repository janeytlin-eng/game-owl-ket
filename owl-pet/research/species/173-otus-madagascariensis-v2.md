## Evidence actually read

### TSO-RENE-THORSTROM-2009-P87-88
Lily-Arison René de Roland & Russell Thorstrom 2009, “First record of Torotoroka Scops Owl *Otus madagascariensis* nesting on the ground,” *Bull. African Bird Club* 16(1):87–88, DOI https://doi.org/10.5962/p.309872 . BHL metadata/article abstract read 2026-11-01; printed pp.87–88.
Facts: exceptional Berenty ground nest with four eggs; hatching was not confirmed. Limits: this is not a chick-development source and must not be generalized into the default nest environment.

### TSO-FUCHS-ETAL-2007-P408-418
Fuchs et al. 2007, “Geographical Structure of Genetic Variation in the Malagasy Scops-Owl,” *Condor* 109:408–418, DOI https://doi.org/10.1093/condor/109.2.408 . Abstract and detailed indexed text read 2026-11-01.
Facts: 1,449 bp mtDNA from 34 owls, low divergence and recent population split; authors judged recognition of two species inappropriate. Dry-west birds named *madagascariensis* tend greyer/yellow-brown with shorter tufts/longer tail than humid-east *rutilus*. Limits: mtDNA/checklist conclusion conflicts with current IOC/ITIS project treatment; morphology may be ecological. Preserve both treatments and do not double-count.

### TSO-ITIS-686657-2025
ITIS TSN 686657, https://itis.gov/servlet/SingleRpt/SingleRpt?print_version=PRT&search_topic=TSN&search_value=686657&source=to_print ; live page read 2026-11-01.
Facts: valid *Otus madagascariensis*, synonym *Scops madagascariensis*, IOC v15.1 reference. Taxonomy only; no morphology/development.

### Reused TSO-MIKKOLA-2013-P145
Supports short-tufted grey morph adults, moderate female direction, dry-west habitat and an age-unknown older immature with stronger-barred uppertail coverts and narrower pointed rectrix tips. Downy chick undescribed.

## Design decisions

Use common grey morph; female moderately fuller only. The centre is an older immature, not a chick: adult-like pattern is allowed, with softer contour and tail-covert/rectrix differences. No ground nest despite the exceptional record, no eggs and no flight claim.
