## Fresh evidence read this cycle

### Taxonomy and historical name mapping

ITIS TSN 686656, accessed 2026-11-04, treats *Otus mayottensis* Benson, 1960 as a valid species and cites IOC 15.1 among its taxonomic sources. Avibase identifies the protonym as *Otus rutilus mayottensis*, gives the original description as Benson 1960, Ibis 103b:60–66, and the type locality as La Convalescence, Mayotte, 400 m, 2 November 1958. The Benson paper was located through its DOI/metadata but its full text was not read in this cycle; it is therefore a bibliographic lead, not a new morphology source.

### Adult identity and sex boundary

Mikkola 2013 printed p.139 / uploaded-PDF p.141 remains the repository adult anchor. The user-uploaded guide itself was not accessible in this execution, so no fresh page image was claimed. Repository evidence supports length 24 cm, about 120 g, wing 166–178 mm, short tufts, mottled light/dark grey-brown adults, a contrasting mottled nuchal collar, sparse pale scapular areas rather than a bold stripe, streaked/barred white throat, vermiculated underparts, yellow eyes, greyish-horn bill, tarsi feathered to the toe bases and pale toes. It states that the female wing is slightly longer; it does not establish a generally larger female body. V2 therefore keeps male and female near-equal in height/mass and expresses sex only as a subtle folded-wing-length cue.

### Young-bird evidence and its limit

Voisin & Voisin 2011, Journal of the National Museum 180(2):9–30, printed pp.15–16, records MNHN C.G. 1884-2066 from Mayotte. Rasmussen identified it as an immature *Otus rutilus mayottensis*. This is direct species-specific evidence that an older immature specimen exists, but the catalogue provides no age, plumage description, iris, soft parts, feather chronology, mobility or nest context.

BirdForum Opus says juveniles are similar to adults but also says breeding information is absent. This is secondary, unpaginated and not mapped to a primary passage; it is retained only as medium-low-confidence support for an adult-like **older immature**, never as support for a chick, exact iris, flight ability or nest. A targeted search found no primary developmental study or reliably aged juvenile photograph. The honest design boundary remains an age-unknown older immature with adult-like identity, softened/less regular contour, compact formed wing, short partly hidden tail and full branch support.

### Habitat and nesting boundary

Secondary accounts place the species in lowland secondary/evergreen forest, but no species-specific nest structure or juvenile nest observation was verified. V2 uses humid Mayotte forest only as habitat ambience. It includes no nest, cavity, egg, lining or claim of flight capability.
