# 61 · Buffy Fish Owl — *Ketupa ketupu*

## Taxonomy

Current name: *Ketupa ketupu* (Horsfield, 1821). Historical combination: *Bubo ketupu*. Protonym: *Strix ketupu*. Treat these names as one species.

## Evidence facts

- Warm buff-brown adult with yellow eyes, long lateral tawny ear tufts, pale brows/face, heavy dark vertical breast streaks and broadly barred wings/tail.
- Tropical Southeast Asian wet forests and wooded parks around water are appropriate.
- A 2016 Singapore photo sequence directly documents a single down-covered nestling resting and slightly shifting within a living bird's-nest fern (*Asplenium nidus*).
- A separate 2024 record supports branch grip and parental feeding in a juvenile reported about two days post-fledging, but that later stage is not used in v1.

## Design decisions

- Adults share plumage and near-equal visible height; the female is only subtly fuller.
- V1 uses the directly documented downy fern-nest stage: low, resting, naturally supported, short down-covered wing buds and no mature remiges or long tail.
- No precise age, measured sex ratio, universal fern-nesting rule or subspecies reconstruction is claimed.

## v3 research boundary (2026-10-29)

- New dated field evidence separates the selected early nestling from the later preflight bird: BFOW-BESG-FLEDGE-2016 reports an approximately two-week chick dominated by white down between fern-frond bases, whereas the approximately six-week bird is on a branch with adult-like plumage and wing-stretch/preflight behavior.
- BFOW-SBG-BREED-2016 independently orders the same Sungei Buloh sequence from the fern-hidden 25 March chick to the 25 April branch bird. Dates are observation/discovery dates, not a measured hatch series.
- v2 was opened: adult identity, labels, support and near-equal scale pass; the juvenile's dark structured wing patches and ear-like projections overstate the chosen early stage. v3 is a juvenile-only correction to loose white/cream natal down, no ear tufts, tucked down-covered wing buds, hidden tail and deep fern/humus support.
- Unknown: exact hatch age, continuous feather geometry, flight threshold, sex ratio, subspecies variation. Scientific acceptance remains false.
