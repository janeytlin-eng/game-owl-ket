## Independent actual browser verification and its limits

Recovered original Actions ZIP and matched GLB SHA/ZIP CRC. Constructed a separate Chromium WebGL2/Xvfb/SwiftShader test loading the **real GLB-derived transformed triangles and normals** into a tiny custom normal-lit renderer: **8/8 software canvas frames** drawn without GL or JavaScript errors (front, 3/4, strict side, back in both 800×800 desktop and 390×600 simulated phone viewport). This checks only that real geometry draws in desktop **software WebGL2**. It is **not** a complete PBR glTF viewer, physical phone GPU or FPS, animation, touch, thermal or power test; no shippable pet application was produced.

## Four real Blender views inspected: quality still fails

Compared native Run86 front, three-quarter, strict profile and back renders to actual v3 rightmost source and the actual Run85 views. The new single smooth-loft profile reduces Run85's obvious snowman-like joined-spheres neck, but makes a **wedge/pear-shaped upright toy** rather than a plausible young owl. Facial heart area reads as a deep **stiff triangular embossed mask** with unnatural radial cheek scoring; round eyes remain flat black dots; bill protrudes from the profile. There is no convincing naturally buried short-wing anatomical volume, the **rear is a bald symmetric silhouette**, and 11,000 down meshes still read as gritty sparkly needles instead of fluffy pale cream mesoptile. Exposed toes and tree ledge remain anatomical/contextual failures. Critically the main skin is also **not sealed (224 boundary edges)**. No real mobile GPU/PBR/animation evidence.

**Decision: ART_REJECTED**, not `PASS`, no user-facing sample, no deployment, no change in electronic-pet release state. In future pursue documented stage-appropriate photos showing rear/side plus genuine sculpted cranial/wing/shoulder and natural groom/LOD rather than repeatedly smoothing a bag/oval and decorating with thousands of simple mesh strands.
