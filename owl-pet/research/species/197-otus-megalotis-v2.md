# 197 · Luzon Lowland Scops Owl · *Otus megalotis* v2

## Scope and correction
- Current taxon: *Otus megalotis* (Walden, 1875), Greater Luzon; old name/protonym *Scops megalotis*.
- Revision of the preserved 680×453 v1. Direct viewing found the juvenile rendered as a miniature adult with long crisp regularly spotted wing and adult-bright iris; the title omitted #197.
- V2 uses the common gray-brown/intermediate adult morph. Miranda et al. found the red morph in only 2/21 specimens; color morph is not encoded as sex.
- Sexed means are larger for females in wing, tail, culmen and tarsus, but ranges overlap; the design uses only subtle female fullness/length.

## Sources actually read 2026-11-04
### LLSO-MIRANDA-2011-P446-450
Miranda, Brooks & Kennedy, 2011, Wilson Journal of Ornithology 123:441–453. Full PDF read, especially printed pp.446 and 450. Twenty-one sexed adult *megalotis* were measured (11 male, 10 female). Means: male wing 175.09 mm/tail 94.81; female wing 190.9/tail 102.6, with overlapping ranges. Diagnostic *megalotis* traits include scapular stripes and tarsal feathers reaching the upper foot; typical plumage is intermediate, with ashy-brown underparts and brownish nape; red morph only 2/21. Scope: adults/taxonomy, not development.

### LLSO-MARSHALL-1978-P26
Joe T. Marshall, 1978, *Systematics of Smaller Asian Night Birds Based on Voice*, Ornithological Monographs 25, printed p.26. Full account lines read. Records a rufous juvenile holotype (BM 42.2.15.123), a nest in a large *Ficus* in a wooded ravine, and occurrence in lowlands, farms, villages and montane forest. It does not describe a continuous juvenile feather sequence or exact locomotor stage.

### LLSO-SANCHEZ-2012-P98-100
Michael S. Sanchez, 2012, *Our Nature* 10:96–114, printed pp.98–100. Full text read. Figure 1 labels adult and juvenile; adult description gives blackish crown/white eye stripe, gray-brown upperparts, light gray underparts with dark streaks, orange-red eyes, ivory-horn bill/cere and tarsi feathered to the toes. Breeding/general chick paragraphs partly rely on broader owl literature/captive files and are not treated as an exact wild *O. megalotis* developmental chronology.

### LLSO-WOODS-2003-JAN-APR-P29
Woods, 2003 Philippines trip report, printed p.29. Read account of two juvenile *O. megalotis* calling and perched high in trees at UPLB with one adult. Supports an older supported arboreal juvenile stage, not exact age or flight ability.

### LLSO-AVIBASE-2026
Birds Canada/Avibase, accessed 2026-11-04. Current *Otus megalotis*; protonym *Scops megalotis*; Walden 1875, p.145; Greater Luzon range. The linked BHL original page returned 403 this run, so no unviewed description text is claimed.

### LLSO-OWL-PAGES-2012-JUV
The Owl Pages photo record by Bram Demeulemeester; Mount Data, Luzon, April 2012; page explicitly identifies a juvenile supported on a moss/lichen branch. Useful for stage/support context; no age, sex or flight metadata.
