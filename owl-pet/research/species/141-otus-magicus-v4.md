## Evidence synthesis

Mikkola supports no sexual-size direction, broad colour-morph variation, yellow adult eyes, pale greyish-black bill/cere, strong feet with distal tarsus bare 6–9 mm, and an older fluffy adult-like juvenile with narrow head/crown/neck bars and fainter body spotting. Sharpe's 1875 catalogue and plate provide a historical barred-pattern comparator but cannot establish a modern field-age sequence. Sangster et al. (2013) was re-read to enforce the taxonomic boundary: Lombok birds are current *Otus jolandae* (#139), not the northern-Moluccan *O. magicus leucospilus* reference used here. ITIS maps original *Strix magica* to valid *Otus magicus* and lists *O. m. leucospilus*.

The design therefore uses a pale northern-Moluccan leucospilus adult palette and an age-unknown older feathered juvenile. Adults remain near equal display size. No nest is depicted.
