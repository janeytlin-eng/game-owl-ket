# 46 · Mottled Wood Owl · *Strix ocellata*

## Scope
- Current working name: *Strix ocellata* (Lesson, 1839); historical combinations include *Syrnium ocellatum* and *Bulaca ocellata*.
- Concept stage planned: adult male, adult female, mesoptile juvenile.
- Status: concept image v1 generated, edited, reviewed and archived; not scientifically verified.

## Evidence actually read
1. Mikkola 2013, printed pp. 324–325 (PDF pp. 326–327), species 150: 41–48 cm, no ear-tufts; females only presumed larger; mottled reddish-brown/black/white/buff upperparts, finely concentrically barred pale facial disc, dark-brown eyes, muted coral eyelids, horn-black bill; mesoptile with whiter crown and finely blackish-barred pale nape/mantle/wing-coverts.
2. Deshpande, Pawar & Mahajan, *Breeding Biology of the Mottled Wood-Owl in West-central India*, actual abstract/PDF excerpt read via indexed journal copy: nine nesting seasons (2005–2016), 15 pairs; mango 52% and tamarind 22% of nest trees. This supports the mature grove context, not precise juvenile feather sequence.
3. BirdLife current factsheet used only to cross-check the current name and distribution/status framing.

## Boundaries
- Do not turn presumed female-larger wording into a large visible standing-height gap.
- Juvenile uses the described mesoptile, without invented exact day-age.
- Precise juvenile wing/tail growth and sex-specific measurements remain unknown.
