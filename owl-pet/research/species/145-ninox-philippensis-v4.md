## Evidence actually read

### LZBO-RASMUSSEN-2012-P13-14 — adult identity
- Rasmussen et al. 2012, Forktail 28, primary taxonomic revision, printed pp.13–14 (diagnosis and measurements; paper pp.1–2 provide split context).
- Supports compact earless form; small buff/pale supercilium; plain dark auriculars and weak disc; plain brown crown to rump; limited white/pale-buff scapular and outer-covert markings; weak narrow tail bands; pale underparts with longitudinal streaking; yellow iris, pale olive-yellow bill and yellow feet.
- The pooled measurements do not establish a dependable male:female display ratio. Design inference: adults remain near equal in apparent size.

### NPH-SHARPE-1875-P167-168 — directly described young plumage
- R. Bowdler Sharpe, *Catalogue of the Birds in the British Museum*, vol. 2, printed pp.167–168; primary historical museum description.
- The entry headed “Young” describes rufous-chocolate, almost uniform upperparts; a few buffy-white outer-scapular bars; wing coverts uniform like the back; greater-coverts spotted ochraceous or white; dark primary coverts; formed dark quills notched ochraceous/buffy; darker short tail; fawn underparts with broader abdominal streaking.
- Limits: exact age, sex, locality within the modern split, iris colour and action ability are not supplied. The design therefore uses a stationary age-unknown supported young bird, dark/uncalibrated iris, and no flight or nest claim.

### NPH-PARKES-1973-P36-37 — historical range/name evidence only
- Kenneth C. Parkes, 1973, “Annotated List of the Birds of Leyte Island, Philippines,” *Nemouria* 11, printed pp.36–37.
- Parkes reports an adult female and a nestling male collected in 1937 under the old broad name *N. philippensis philippensis*; both were already no longer extant. He compares later adult Leyte males with Luzon material and gives wing lengths.
- Limits/conflict: this pre-split Leyte record cannot be assumed to represent modern strict *N. philippensis* without current island-level reconciliation, and the lost nestling supplies no observable plumage, posture or action detail. It is indexed for old-name mapping only and is not used to draw the young.

### LZBO-MIKKOLA-2013-P473 — repository-pinned guide reading
- Prior repository record preserves printed p.473/PDF p.475 notes, including white natal down and an age-unknown fledgling description.
- The uploaded guide was not freshly accessible in this execution. No new page claim is made; v4 uses the directly reopened Sharpe source for the depicted age-unknown young stage.
