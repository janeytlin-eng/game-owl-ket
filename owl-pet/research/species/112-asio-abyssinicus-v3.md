# #112 *Asio abyssinicus* v3 research and design brief

## Scope and version boundary

- Preserve v1–v2 and all historical/user records. This is a new v3 science revision, not a scientific sign-off.
- Nominate Ethiopian *A. a. abyssinicus* only. Current/old-name mapping: *Asio abyssinicus* = original combination *Otus abyssinicus*. Do not import the smaller/greyer *A. a. graueri* phenotype.
- V2’s fully concealed white chick was appropriately conservative for its then-open evidence. V3 selects a different, actually viewed age-unknown older juvenile from Ethiopia, so a visible juvenile is now supportable without inventing hatchling anatomy.

## Evidence facts and design boundaries

### Adults

- Collar & Boesman 2019, pp.158–161 / Figs.1–3 / Tables1–2: larger long-eared-owl structure; broader/fused dark upperpart markings; dense net-like underpart pattern; curved pale spots on secondaries/tail; nominate more ochre-buff than *graueri*.
- The dated Dinsho adult photograph supports orange iris, black bill, warm dark-rimmed facial disc, tall flexible central tufts, robust streaked/barred body and feathered tarsi.
- Sexes were pooled in the measured series. Do not make the female mechanically larger; use near-equal crown height and only neutral natural variation/fullness.

### Older juvenile

- Maton’s Ethiopia 1989 species-labelled juvenile photograph was opened full-screen: charcoal/gray-brown loose down, very dark face, orange irises, dark bill/narrow pale midface highlight, short emerging tufts, down-softened sides, concealed tail and foliage/branch support.
- Exact age, sex, wing/tail tract chronology, foot anatomy and sustained-flight ability are unknown. Choose an older perched juvenile: no flight pose, no spread wing, no long clean adult remiges or visible tail. Two-foot support may be mostly hidden under down, but the body must visibly rest on a thick branch.

### Place and nest

- Use a quiet Ethiopian montane-highland woodland/heath edge, with lichen/moss, muted Erica/Hagenia-like structure and cool haze. The ABC Mt Kenya report is preliminary and its suspected roost/pellets are not a nest record.
- No nest, eggs, prey, hunting or adult feeding scene. Branch is support only.

## Unknowns retained

Fixed sex-size ratio; juvenile exact age/sex/subspecies; continuous feather timetable; wing/tail lengths; sustained-flight threshold; foot/toe anatomy in the photograph; universal iris chronology; nest-site details; universal habitat and exact colour under neutral light.
