## Taxonomy
Project label follows the widespread literature combination *Bubo nipalensis*. Current lists may place the same species in *Ketupa* as *Ketupa nipalensis*; both names map to one species and must not be counted twice.

## Evidence boundary
- Adults: large stark greyish-brown forest eagle-owl; very long ragged sideways ear tufts; pale fulvous underside with black-and-white horizontal flank stripes becoming broad belly spots; feathered legs/feet, terminal toe digits bare.
- Sex: no accessible stable visible sex difference was verified; adults stay near-equal in scale with only natural pose variation.
- Juvenile: a dated field record supports pale yellow/cream plumage, light spotting, thin V-shaped breast marks, small ear tufts, creamy legs and weak/non-sustained flight while parent-dependent. A separate labelled field photograph supports branch perching.
- Environment: dense moist deciduous to evergreen foothill forest; stable mossy branch support.
