## Actual independent free asset acquisition

Primary source: [Innerscene original Owl GLB](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/owl-d696472a); [documented direct download endpoint](https://www.innerscene.com/api/library/owl-d696472a/download). The site declares the model to be an **original Innerscene creation dedicated to CC0**; [library terms](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/terms) and contributor rules were inspected. This is the publisher's declaration, not an independent chain-of-title guarantee.

A real Ubuntu GitHub Actions runner requested the public download **without an account, payment, quota workaround, or protected endpoint**. HTTP **200** returned `application/octet-stream`; the binary's GLB2 header and exact declared size both verified. The response resolved to `files.innerscene.com/library/owl-d696472a/8ecea479b865c7141318/owl.glb`.

- **Actual original native GLB:** **20,671,040 bytes**, SHA256 **`c11c94db20065c6c6f252e768e8c748f22dc69ef9b4e7199110b96de0625c12e`**.
- Independent Trimesh read: **129** distinct real geometry meshes, **62,592 triangles**, **129/129 UV-enabled**, PBR materials, XYZ bounds approximately **0.4080 × 0.5128 × 0.2121 m**. Source component names include `Great horned owl continuous thick body neck and facial volume`, `Cambered folded wing foundation`, `Articulated bird toe`. The internal mesh identity already raises a species mismatch.
- A real **Chromium / Three.js r170 / SwiftShader WebGL2** loader successfully loaded the original unmodified GLB in a **390×780 emulated mobile viewport**, reporting **129 meshes, 62,592 triangles, 129 materials and valid WebGL2**. Captured eight different 3D yaw rotations **0°, 45°, 90°, 135°, 180°, 225°, 270°, 315°** with nonempty image occupancy in each. Only one non-blocking missing-resource 404 occurred. This does **not** test physical iPhone/Android hardware or touch latency.

## Visual assessment — ART REJECTED, not a candidate for product release

I **opened and inspected the real eight-view WebGL2 montage**, not simply the workflow green checkmark: [actual eight-angle contact sheet](run322-live-evidence/run322-original-GLB-eight-real-WebGL-views.jpg). It shows an **adult great horned owl-like model**, with erect ear tufts, prominent **yellow round irises**, high protruding hooked beak, brown/gray contour plumage, long folded flight wings, extended tail, upright elongated torso, and exposed unsupported feet.

It is **not** the [#172 *Tyto castanops* v3 RIGHT chick](../../design/concepts/172-tyto-castanops-v3.png): no broad natural **heart-shaped Tyto facial disc**, no dark young masked-owl eyes with correct facial structure, no thick cream-white mesoptile or softly rounded chick outline, and no short wings buried in down / hidden tail / secure hold on an old eucalyptus hollow. The eight angles do prove real geometry, but also show stylized assembled rigid feathers rather than the required professional juvenile down sculpt.

Model complexity (**20.7 MB, 129 mesh/material groups**) is not a sign of art quality and would warrant mobile draw-call, texture, memory and loading optimization even if the species and juvenile anatomy were correct. The model **cannot be accepted, simply recolored, counted as the #172 asset, or displayed as a user-ready 3D prototype**. **ART_REJECTED / NO_RELEASE**.

A second newly found supposedly free [ArtistFrog Baby Owl Sculpt](https://www.cgtrader.com/free-3d-models/animal/bird/free-baby-owl-sculpt) was pre-screened from its **real published front preview**. It is also a smooth spherical clay cartoon with an exaggerated circular eye form, no developed heart-disc or down; unlike the first CC0 model it would additionally require marketplace download interaction. It was **not acquired** or misreported as a source GLB. This avoids spending future effort on a knowingly unsuitable form.
