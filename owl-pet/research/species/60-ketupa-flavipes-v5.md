## Evidence reconciliation and design boundary

- **Adults:** large orange/rufous-yellow fish owls with loose lateral ear tufts, bright yellow irides, weak brown facial discs, white lores/throat, black bill, coarse dark crown/nape and ventral streaking, dark tails with sparse narrow pale bands. Feathering only high/front on the tarsus; lower tarsi and toes clearly bare yellow.
- **Sexes:** near-equal crown height and same visible pattern. Female may read only slightly fuller through pose/body volume; no categorical “female larger” claim.
- **Juvenile:** one unsexed approximately-40-day Wuling nestling, already large but lower, with paler yellow-rufous down-soft head/body and finer, lighter streaking than adults. Developed rounded folded wings are irregular/down-fringed, without crisp adult covert rows or long rigid adult remige stacks; medium developing tail remains softer/shorter than adults.
- **Action/support:** calm daytime rest, preen or low supported clamber in the living-tree Y-fork. Both feet and body have natural support; no launch, unsupported brancher or flight.
- **Environment:** intact Wuling mountain-stream forest, massive hollow *Magnolia compressa* Y-fork with restrained humus and epiphytic ferns, steep clear stream below.
- **Unknown:** exact feather-tract chronology, exact age/sex of every photographed frame, first-flight competence at 40 days, quantitative sex dimensions and transfer beyond the Wuling population.
- **Target status:** `PASS_WITH_LIMITATIONS`; scientifically verified remains `false`.
