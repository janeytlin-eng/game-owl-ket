## Problem → evidence → modification → recheck

The first render gave the recent fledgling a neat adult-style white spot row and long layered remiges. Abbate et al. printed pp.22–23 say this age is fully feathered but distinguish it by shorter tail, reduced crown flecks and lighter/less crisp nape patches; the USDA synthesis adds irregular pale teardrop juvenile feather ends. One juvenile-only edit shortened and rounded the wing, removed the adult row, retained irregular juvenile tips and kept the short rufous tail. The edited full image and three crops were reopened; the change is visible and did not alter adults, labels or support.

## Iteration rule

Discovered and applied: for fully feathered recent fledglings, do not mechanically erase all formed wing feathers. Separate adult diagnostic rows from stage-specific irregular juvenile tips, and verify the whole wing geometry and tail length together. This rule was actually applied and visibly improved v4. It should be checked against older concepts only when their chosen age/stage is comparable.

## Remaining unknowns

Exact individual day/sex, juvenile iris chronology, quantitative wing/tail lengths, universal sex-size ratio, geographic colour variation, full nape-patch view and exact cavity geometry. Therefore PASS_WITH_LIMITATIONS, not scientific verification.
