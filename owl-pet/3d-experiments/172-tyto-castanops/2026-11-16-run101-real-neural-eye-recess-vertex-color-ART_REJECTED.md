## Executed experiment — genuine editable three-dimensional face vertices

This run did **not** create a fake SVG, 2D owl card, substitute low-poly primitives, detached eye spheres or mere design text. On the real Run100 smoothed Hunyuan glTF surface, physically located the existing facial eye areas by 3D camera ray tests, displaced the actual underlying mesh vertices locally to explore an orbital/eyelid relief, and wrote real 3D glTF `COLOR_0` per-vertex pigmentation. **154 original mesh vertices** received a surface displacement above 0.002 normalized units; maximum displacement was **0.0178243**. The full **79,988 original triangular faces** and **39,684 vertices** were retained, not replaced by generated spheres.

## Visual judgment from the actual 4-angle native Cycles images: reject both

**Run101A:** A small dark eye spot and an eyelid fold are visible but **both eyes remain semiclosed or poorly defined**, not the reference's bright, symmetric, open dark eyes; the heart mask reads as a small hard inset. White/cream shader makes the body look like a coarse rigid shell, not layered soft feather down. The side shows an elongated stooped adult-like torso and protruding claws, short wings merge into a scaly side texture. The back is covered in engraved/shingled plates and lacks credible soft juvenile mantle anatomy.

**Run101B:** The projected original facial pigments yield a noticeably **smudged brown patch** rather than depth-aware eye structures, without opening the eyes. The 3D facial contour, long rigid body silhouette, scaled-looking rear, lack of down and dangling ungrounded claws remain unchanged.

Both fail: **(1)** correct open-eyed 3D heart disc, **(2)** genuine cream down in multiple natural layers, **(3)** coherent short concealed wing/side/rear anatomy, **(4)** supporting eucalyptus hollow lip and physically planted feet. **No rig, animation, real smartphone GPU/WebGL frame pacing/touch verification** and **no global distribution license review** have passed. Local extra attempt to open a temporary browser WebGL test was blocked by Chromium/Playwright network administrator policy (`ERR_BLOCKED_BY_ADMINISTRATOR`); this is an unexecuted GPU gate, not an inferred pass.

**ART_REJECTED on both variants; still NO PUBLISH.** This trial falsifies the notion that small eye-area vertex displacements and concept-paint projection can solve the more basic sculpt and groom deficiencies. A real anatomy-informed juvenile retopology/sculpt with dedicated eye-orbit/bill anatomy, artist-built second-down grooming and a substantial physical support is now a prerequisite. Side/back must be judged from independently grounded owl anatomy, not a single frontal design image.
