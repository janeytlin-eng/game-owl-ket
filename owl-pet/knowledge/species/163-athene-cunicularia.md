# #163 Burrowing Owl — *Athene cunicularia*

- Region: western North American *A. c. hypugaea*; shortgrass prairie/open ground; natural mammal burrow and low dirt mound.
- Adult: small, tuftless, yellow-eyed, pale-browed; sandy brown with pale spots, pale barred underparts; gray-olive bill/cere; long sparsely feathered tarsi and bristled toes.
- Sex: evidence conflicts; keep adults near-equal, never impose a fixed female/male ratio.
- Juvenile: 29-day prefledging *hypugaea*; soft mesoptile, unspotted crown, diffuse underparts, buff breast sides, whitish face with dusky outer-eye zones, short rounded developing wing/tail, fully ground-supported.
- Prohibit: ear tufts, adult spot grid/remiges on juvenile, unsupported or flying juvenile, South American palette, exact flight claim, artificial universal dimorphism.
- Science remains pending. [Research](../../research/species/163-athene-cunicularia.md)
