Unlike repeated add-on oval wing shells, this experiment removes both disconnected source wing pads and sculpts a *continuous, nonprimitive 3D body surface* with torso/shoulder mass redistribution and shallow embedded wing/feather relief. N added correlated multiscale 3D surface microdisplacement and 2,200 very short curved contour-down wedges to test real *spatial* detail rather than alpha-shell layers or long needles. Reimported actual N GLB: **18 meshes, 78,564 triangles, 4,383,268 B**, SHA256 `f5b07e6504297792f7fbf4f70d8fd2e3eed39ae7701053ea2a9ca726e5bb612a`. Body max displacement ~0.05916 units.

**Seam QA correction, actually implemented:** Initial geometric topology query failed when `trimesh.merge_vertices()` retained intentional duplicated UV seam vertices; geometry-only `merge_tex=True, merge_norm=True` establishes true continuous geometry. The first N microdisplacement accidentally separated corresponding UV seam positions because their interpolated normals differed, so the build was changed to group original equal-position vertices and average resulting 3D locations **while retaining separate UV coordinates**, followed by complete re-export/reimport, 12 new renders and 16 new browser draws. Final M/N merged-body tests: watertight=true, winding-consistent=true, positive signed volume (~0.50331/~0.50172 units³). Raw texture-split render mesh still deliberately carries duplicate UV seam vertices.
