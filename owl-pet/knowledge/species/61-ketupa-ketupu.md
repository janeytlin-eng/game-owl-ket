# 61 · Buffy Fish Owl — *Ketupa ketupu*

## Taxonomy

Current name: *Ketupa ketupu* (Horsfield, 1821). Historical combination: *Bubo ketupu*. Protonym: *Strix ketupu*. Treat these names as one species.

## Evidence facts

- Warm buff-brown adult with yellow eyes, long lateral tawny ear tufts, pale brows/face, heavy dark vertical breast streaks and broadly barred wings/tail.
- Tropical Southeast Asian wet forests and wooded parks around water are appropriate.
- A 2016 Singapore photo sequence directly documents a single down-covered nestling resting and slightly shifting within a living bird's-nest fern (*Asplenium nidus*).
- A separate 2024 record supports branch grip and parental feeding in a juvenile reported about two days post-fledging, but that later stage is not used in v1.

## Design decisions

- Adults share plumage and near-equal visible height; the female is only subtly fuller.
- V1 uses the directly documented downy fern-nest stage: low, resting, naturally supported, short down-covered wing buds and no mature remiges or long tail.
- No precise age, measured sex ratio, universal fern-nesting rule or subspecies reconstruction is claimed.
