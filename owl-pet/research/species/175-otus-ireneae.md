## Source facts vs design inference

Source facts: adults are 16–18 cm/45–55 g, no evidence of sex-size difference, polymorphic with small tufts, pale-yellow adult eyes and the guide’s detailed gray/dark pattern. The guide says only that the juvenile resembles the adult. The 2023 field report recorded two fledglings with a pair and one chick with another pair in April, but found neither a natural nest nor box occupation.

Design inference: use an age-unknown older juvenile with adult-like formed plumage but a softer contour, fully supported with its parents on a low natural forest branch. This combines the guide’s developmental boundary with the project report’s family observation without claiming an exact age, flight threshold, juvenile soft parts or nest geometry.
