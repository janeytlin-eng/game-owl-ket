# 51 · Rufous-legged Owl · *Strix rufipes*

## Scope
- Current working name: *Strix rufipes* King, 1827; historical treatment sometimes included Chaco Owl as *S. r. chacoensis*, now retained as a separate-species conflict note.
- Planned stage: adult male, adult female, and cavity chick in downy off-white stage.
- Status: VERIFIED production archive; not scientifically verified.

## Evidence actually read
1. Holt et al. 2020, Birds of the World, DOI 10.2173/bow.rulowl1.01, as cited in an accessible species synthesis: adults compact and ear-tuft-free; rusty facial disc, white brows/lores, dark-brown eyes, dark reddish-brown upperparts with orange-buff bars/spots, buffy-white underparts with black bars; chick downy off-white, juvenile warm buff with faint dusky barring and tawny face; tree cavities typical.
2. Unusual Nesting of the Rufous-legged Owl, Journal of Raptor Research 2024: primary nesting report; two eggs in a shallow ground depression with pine needles and woody debris. Full PDF endpoint returned 403 in this run, so only title/abstract metadata is treated as verified and no unseen detail is used.
3. BirdLife International DataZone species factsheet: current accepted *Strix rufipes* species account and conservation/range framework.
4. Accessible adult/chick species synthesis with a labelled chick photograph from Ñuble forest, Chile, used only to corroborate the conservative off-white down stage.

## Design boundaries
- Use the better-supported typical tree-cavity context, not the unusual ground nest.
- Adult pair: no ear tufts; dark-brown eyes; rusty facial discs with white brows/lores; dark reddish-brown barred upperparts; buffy-white underparts with dark bars; feathered rufous-buff legs.
- Female may be subtly fuller, consistent with reported female-larger direction, but do not exaggerate standing height.
- Chick: low and securely supported in cavity litter, off-white down dominant, tiny soft emerging feather buds only; no mature remiges, long tail or flight pose.
- Temperate Valdivian/Nothofagus forest atmosphere; no subspecies claim.

## Unknowns
- Exact chick age and continuous feather sequence; cross-checked sex-specific linear measurements; locality/subspecies-level variation.
