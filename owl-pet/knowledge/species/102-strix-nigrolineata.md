- Current scientific name: *Strix nigrolineata*

## Identification boundary

Adults: rounded head without ear tufts; nearly uniform sooty black-brown crown/back/folded wings; narrow barred hindneck collar; blackish face with restrained pale brow/ruff speckling; dark reddish-brown eyes; orange-yellow bill; black throat bib; white underparts with dense narrow black horizontal bars; barred feathered tarsi and bare dirty-yellow/orange distal toes. The v2 male/female are kept near-equal in standing height; a slightly fuller female is a conservative display choice, not a universal linear-dimension formula.

Juvenile: age-unknown older mesoptile only. Dirty cream/white fluffy plumage with narrow dusky barring, dark eyes, pale orange-yellow bill, short rounded down-covered developing wings and short tail. No hatchling age, fledging threshold or adult remiges are claimed.

Habitat/nest example: four Tikal nests documented on bare orchid/bromeliad epiphyte platforms high in large live trees. This is a regional example, not a universal nest rule; no cavity, constructed straw nest or successful fledging age is inferred.

## V3 evidence-led revision — 2026-10-31

Gerhardt & Gerhardt 1997 pp.190–193 adds one measured Tikal pair: female 535 g / wing 29.3 cm / tail 18.7 cm, male 436 g / 28.6 cm / 16.5 cm, with similar visible difference reported in other unmeasured pairs. V3 therefore uses a subtly fuller, only slightly larger female—not a huge height gap. Adult crown/mantle/folded-wing white speckling was reduced toward the documented sooty dorsal pattern. Juvenile remains an age-unknown older mesoptile; no flight threshold is inferred.
