## Actual visual decision

**ART_REJECTED / NO_RELEASE.** Run324 is a native, orbitable owl-shaped sculpture with a readable heart-ish face, large feet, and some wing/bill relief; it is **not** yet the requested rightmost soft-down \`Tyto castanops\` chick. The 0°/315° back looks like a smooth/pitted oval stone; 45°/90°/270° side views show shallow, defined hard wing sculpture, not wings concealed inside volumetric mesoptile. Face has recessed uncolored eye sockets and a carved mask border, not dark living eyes framed by delicate down. Chest and sides lack cream fibrous depth, loose down clumps, feather transitions and soft silhouette. The foot/claw mass is conspicuously exposed, oversize relative to the target crouched chick; it does **not** grip a mature eucalyptus hollow lip. There is no texture, fur material or physical/mobile perf evidence. Neither triangle count nor successful WebGL2 loading is an art quality score.

The technical output can be retained as non-distributed **diagnostic shape evidence only**. Do not simply recolor the stone-like mesh and advertise it as a finished cream-white fluffy chick. It may be a rough structural proxy for downstream texture-capable AI comparison if the new technique can genuinely recover organic down and sensible side/back volumes. Any such subsequent model requires its own eight views and direct art gate.
