## Actual visual review against the original rightmost bird: ART_REJECTED

**I opened the true four-view exported-model contact sheet** and the actual v3 original plate, not only filenames/metrics. The output clearly falls far short of the original polished juvenile:

- Front face is a flat wide cream/buff oval, **not** a convincingly organic warm-rimmed heart with natural cheek feather flow; eye spheres are unnaturally large, dark, very glossy **cartoon beads**, rather than deeply set eyes.
- True side and 45° views reveal protruding eyeballs and a too-long hooked bill projected in front of the head. No believable face/skull musculature, short tucked wing architecture, or species-specific shoulder line is visible.
- The silhouette is a near-symmetric upright **white snowball / pear** with bristly sharp crown spines; white down is overexposed and reads as coarse flecks/needles rather than layered soft cream mesoptile. Back is virtually featureless except the bristles, not a plausible downy feather tract.
- Both bare feet appear like narrow branching rods, and the support is a thin faceted circular **cookie/wreath of timber**, not an old eucalypt tree hollow large enough to support belly and both feet naturally.

This outcome is no better artistic foundation than the documented Run16/Run86/Run92C negative loft/SDF route. Having a connected watertight mesh and passing Cycles/GLB cannot justify the label `visually basically acceptable`. **ART_REJECTED**, and the model must not be advertised, linked as an acceptable sample or deployed.
