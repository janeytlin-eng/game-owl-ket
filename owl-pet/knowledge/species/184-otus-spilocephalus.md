# 184 Mountain Scops Owl — *Otus spilocephalus*

- Taiwan focus: *Otus spilocephalus hambroecki*.
- Historical mapping: *Ephialtes hambroecki*; *Otus hambroecki*; older English “Spotted Scops Owl”.
- Adult boundary: small forest scops owl, warm tea/rufous-brown, small tufts, yellow eyes, cream-yellow bill, feathered tarsi; adults near-equal because no quantitative sex-size direction was found.
- Juvenile boundary: recent Taiwan fledgling with residual soft down, dark/indeterminate irides, short rounded soft wing, concealed tail and complete branch-fork support; slight open bill is compatible with reported July–August call practice.
- Habitat: Taiwan humid montane broadleaf forest; no invented nest interior.
- Unknown: exact juvenile age/sex, iris chronology, first-flight threshold, hatchling appearance and quantified dimorphism.
