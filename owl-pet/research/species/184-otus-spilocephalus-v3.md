## Evidence boundary

V2 used a cross-subspecies age-unknown juvenile posture reference. V3 improves that boundary by using a Taiwan institutional record of a **recent fledgling** calling in July–August. The design may therefore show a supported recent fledgling with slightly open bill, residual down, a compact partly formed wing and short mostly hidden tail, but may not assign a day-age, sex, iris colour, flight ability, wing-exercise behaviour or detailed nest structure. Adults remain near-equal in display size and same plumage because no read source supplies a quantitative sex-size direction.

## Conflict and uncertainty

The Forestry Bureau report permits both tree hollows and branch depressions, while the museum page summarizes natural tree hollows. Neither source supplies nest-material detail; v3 therefore shows no nest interior. Incubation and rearing periods, exact fledgling age, iris/soft-part chronology, feather-by-feather wing/tail development, first-flight threshold and quantitative sexual dimorphism remain unknown.
