## Taxonomy and name mapping

Current name: *Megascops petersoni* (Fitzpatrick & O'Neill, 1986), Cinnamon Screech-Owl. Original/historical combination: *Otus petersoni* Fitzpatrick & O'Neill, 1986. ITIS TSN 686670 treats the current name as valid and lists the original combination as a synonym.

## Actually read sources

### CIN-FITZPATRICK-ONEILL-1986 — primary description, re-read
- John W. Fitzpatrick & John P. O'Neill, 1986. “*Otus petersoni*, a New Screech-Owl from the Eastern Andes, with Systematic Notes on *O. colombianus* and *O. ingens*.” *Wilson Bulletin* 98(1):1–14.
- PDF: https://www.biologicaldiversity.org/campaigns/global_owl_project/descriptions/Megascops/Megascops%20petersoni%20%28Fitzpatrick%20%26%20O%27Neill%29%201986%2C%20Wilson%20Bulletin%2C%2098%2C%20pages%201-14..pdf
- Read printed pp.3–6. Diagnosis/holotype: small owl; warm buffy-brown/cinnamon throughout; medium tufts; dark-brown irides; pale grey-green bill; pale pinkish-flesh toes; tarsi feathered close to toes; almost complete absence of white. Adult underparts have sparse dark shaft streaks and thin irregular wavy bars. The paper says sexual dimorphism, if present at all, is slight.
- First-year boundary: printed pp.4–5 records one male with numerous paler, more heavily barred downy feathers, presumed to be completing first prebasic body molt; printed p.5 calls it nearly in adult plumage on 28 July. It does not give exact age, continuous feather chronology, foot posture, locomotor ability or flight threshold.
