## Genuine native 3D attempt, not SVG, 2D rotation or a simple-primitive surrogate

Actually implemented and executed [run272_conformal_curved_down_groom.py](tools/run272_conformal_curved_down_groom.py), preserving the original connected neural mesh and painting/color attributes. Using free NumPy, Trimesh, VTK and Pillow, samples area-weighted original triangles, protects front face/eye region and lower claws, generates real **curved, crossed 3D ribbon micro-down geometry** conformal to measured original normals; each hair has multiple spatial stations, tapered thickness and cream-tinted vertex colors. Two A/B densities: 9,000 and 26,000 tufts; 108,000 and 312,000 added genuine 3D triangles. Both use proper explicit glTF PBR, COLOR_0 and NORMAL attributes, no replacement by flat image or primitive spheres. The hair geometry is an *experimental procedural grooming study*, NOT acceptable professional bird feather grooming.

Independently performed local export, re-import and six VTK actual spatial camera renders per variant (front, three-quarter, left, right, back, above). Both GLBs and all twelve images were made before CI and compared against the real chick image. Actual image inspection: the real original eyes and face remain recognizable; the fur silhouette broadens. But individual strands look like **crystalline frost, short sharp bristles or dark scribbles**, not the reference's layered soft white/beige mesoptile feathers. Denser 26k hair makes texture busier rather than genuinely softer. The side is still a rigid adult-like long taper; wing too long and hard, face still looks plate-like in side view; feet not naturally situated on a eucalyptus cavity, which was not modeled.
