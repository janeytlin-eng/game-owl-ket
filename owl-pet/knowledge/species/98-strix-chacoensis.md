# 98 · Chaco Owl — *Strix chacoensis*

## Evidence facts

- Current species placement confirmed by USFWS; historical usage includes *Strix rufipes chacoensis*.
- Adults: no ear tufts; blackish-brown irises; yellowish bill/cere; pale gray-white facial disc with narrow dark concentric lines; off-white underparts barred dark brown.
- Feet: tarsi and basal half of toes feathered pale buff/cream; distal toes gray-brown/yellowish, claws reddish horn with dark tips.
- Juvenile: whitish downy chick; mesoptile very fluffy pale gray-brown, often buff-suffused, pale gray facial disc.
- Habitat: dense to semi-open dry Chaco forest, thorn scrub, Prosopis/quebracho and locally columnar cacti.

## Design inference and limits

v3 uses an older fluffy mesoptile juvenile because that stage is directly described. Exact age and continuous wing/tail sequence are unknown. Female measurements are often larger in very small samples, so the adult female is only modestly fuller/larger; no fixed ratio is claimed. Scientifically verified remains false.
