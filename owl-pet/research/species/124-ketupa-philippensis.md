# 124 — Philippine Eagle-Owl (*Ketupa philippensis*)

- Historical name: *Bubo philippensis*; current repository mapping follows PEO-AVIBASE-MAPPING.
- Scope: Luzon / northern nominate unit for v2; do not transfer southern subspecies details.
- Production target: v2 revision; preserve v1.
- Scientific verification: false.

## Evidence used for v2

- **PEO-URRIZA-2017** — primary Luzon post-breeding study; at least 70 days to fledging, weekly observation over five weeks. Supports a late nestling/near-fledged stage, not a universal day-by-day plumage recipe.
- **PEO-OCHAVO-2012** — firsthand Balara sequence: wing flapping, leaving the structure and later longer flight, with hatch date unknown.
- **PEO-ARTUSO-2015** — viewed Angono adult/juvenile images; supports formed juvenile wing/tail, secure perch grip and warmer/fainter young pattern. Apparent juvenile eye tone is not calibrated.
- **PEO-AVIBASE-MAPPING** — nomenclatural mapping and northern/southern unit boundary; no biometrics.

## Design boundaries

- Adults: yellow eyes, gray bill, rufous-brown/dark shaft pattern, conspicuous long ear tufts; sexes displayed near-equal with only subtle female fullness because species-specific height ratio is unverified.
- Juvenile: approximately 70-day late nestling/near-fledged; formed but juvenile-proportioned wing/tail, two-foot support, restrained wing-stretch/flap preparation. No natal-chick anatomy, unsupported flight or universalized nest.
- Setting: disturbed Luzon forest edge / stream corridor; no prey or nest assertion.
