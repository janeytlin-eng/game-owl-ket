## Current / old name mapping

The production name remains *Ninox japonica*. ITIS (TSN 1063655, IOC 15.1 source) treats *N. j. totogo* as a valid subspecies and lists *Ninox scutulata totogo* as synonym. Taiwan Biodiversity Network (record updated 2025-04-27) instead lists *N. japonica japonica* and gives *N. japonica totogo* as a synonym. The Taiwan institutional species account also records unresolved resident/migrant and subspecies questions. Therefore the plate title stays at species level; the visual uses the central-Taiwan breeding population described as *totogo* in the 2012 primary study without claiming that the subspecies question is settled.

## Applied design boundary

- Adults: near-equal, no tufts, yellow eyes, small white between-eye patch, 3–5 irregular shoulder patches, broad longitudinal ventral streaks, barred tail, cream-feathered tarsi to yellow bristled toes.
- Fledgling: age/sex unknown; fully supported low on branch near a natural cavity; fluffy pale yellow-buff underparts and fewer softer streaks; compact soft wing with no orderly adult shoulder row; short/concealed tail; no flight claim.
- Habitat: central Taiwan secondary hardwood grove and natural cavity, with orchard edge only as context.
- Unknown: exact fledgling age, iris chronology, continuous feather sequence, first-flight ability, independence, individual subspecies assignment and transfer from Taiwan to nominate populations.
- Reusable rule applied: when a source supplies growth mass but not plumage/motor chronology, do not convert the day series into an exact-age illustration.
