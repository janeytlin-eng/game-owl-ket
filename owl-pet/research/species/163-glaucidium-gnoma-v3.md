### GNOMA-DESHLER-2020-RSD-ABSTRACT
- John F. Deshler, “Higher reversed sexual size dimorphism among nesting pairs of Northern Pygmy-Owls (*Glaucidium gnoma*) in northwestern Oregon than among specimens collected at the range-wide scale,” *Wilson Journal of Ornithology* 132(3):619–627 (2020), DOI 10.1676/19-132.
- Read abstract lines 18–21 via ResearchGate landing page. It reports local Oregon females heavier and longer-winged on average than males, but with range-wide overlap.
- Limitation: pre/current-split name usage and Oregon population make direct application to current Mountain *G. gnoma* uncertain. It supports only the general “female fuller, not a fixed standing-height ratio” caution; exact v3 scale is not derived from these measurements.

### NPO-MACAULAY-418673981-2008
- Cornell Lab Macaulay Library ML418673981, John Deshler, Forest Park, Oregon, 11 June 2008.
- Read lines 54–106. Observer notes describe a 23–24-day nestling making a short flight from a stump nest with an unusually large natural side opening.
- Limitation: Oregon broad-name population and an unusually open nest; current species applicability to Mountain *G. gnoma* is unresolved. Not used to give the v3 juvenile a day age, flight action, or large side-opening cavity.
