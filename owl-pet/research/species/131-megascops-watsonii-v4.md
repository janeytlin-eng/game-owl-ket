## Evidence actually read

- **WAT-DANTAS-2021** — Dantas et al., *Zootaxa* 4949:401–444, DOI 10.11646/zootaxa.4949.3.1. Journal page and prior PDF pages 402, 406–407, 427–428 re-read. The study sampled 252 specimens; morphometric DFA found no significant differences among clades and plumage varied strongly within clades. It does not provide a plate-ready sex ratio.
- **WAT-SACC-937-2022** — SACC Proposal 937, lines 8–49 read. It records continuing disagreement about splitting the complex and emphasizes absent concrete morphological/vocal diagnoses among proposed clades. Design consequence: keep the repository's narrow geographic unit explicit, but do not invent a visually diagnostic Guiana-only character or call the plate scientifically resolved.
- **WAT-ITIS-686672-2026** — ITIS TSN 686672, lines 42–114 read. Current valid *Megascops watsonii*; old combinations *Otus watsonii* and *Ephialtes watsonii*. Taxonomy only.
- **WAT-MIK2013-235** — user-uploaded Mikkola guide, PDF p.237 / printed p.235, actually read. Length 19–23 cm, mass 114–155 g; sexual size differences not documented; relatively long tufts; dusty dark gray-brown upperparts without whitish scapular outer webs; tawny/light rusty lower underparts with fine shafts/crossbars; amber-yellow to pale brownish-orange irides; greenish-gray bill; tarsi densely feathered to ochre-brown toes. The guide states “Juvenile Not known.” Its historic distribution includes western localities now taxonomically problematic, so morphology is used as a broad adult boundary rather than proof of a narrow-unit diagnostic.
- **WAT-DAFFONSECA-2017** — reused actual Manaus photograph/caption of one of two age-unknown young: dark gray-brown fluffy barred body, pale blue-gray bill, formed wing mostly hidden, stable branch grip. It does not establish exact age, flight ability, nest or genetic identity.
- **WAT-SCAUP-2024** — reused Guyana photograph for dark gray-brown/tawny adult appearance; age and sex unverified.
- **WAT-BARROS-CINTRA-2009** — reused Reserva Ducke field habitat evidence for mature terra-firme forest and tree/snag association, not a nest claim.

The young is securely supported, but its visible right folded wing is a smooth adult-like panel and the entire down surface is rendered as uniform curls. V4 will separate adult poses, keep comparable skeletal height, replace repeated wire-like texture with matte irregular fibers, and use an age-unknown older young with uneven residual down and only a compact, down-obscured formed wing. No nest is depicted.
