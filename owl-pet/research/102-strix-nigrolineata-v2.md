Limits: historic taxonomy, tiny sample, no sex-separated or juvenile data.

### BWO-GER1994 — reused

Gerhardt et al. 1994. “Breeding Biology and Home Range of Two *Ciccaba* Owls.” *Wilson Bulletin* 106(4):629–639. https://archive.org/download/biostor-200595/biostor-200595.pdf . Full primary PDF previously read pp.629–634, 636–637. Four Black-and-white Owl nests at Tikal were bare orchid/bromeliad epiphytes in large live trees 16–26 m high; not cavities or constructed nests. All failed. An oldest chick disappearing near 24 d is not verified fledging age. Detailed chick chronology on pp.631–632 concerns Mottled Owl and is excluded.

### BWO-MIK2013-339 — reused

Heimo Mikkola, 2013, uploaded guide, printed p.339/PDF p.341, previously actually read. Adult uniform sooty black-brown head/back, barred collar, black throat bib, white underparts with dense black horizontal bars; dark reddish-brown iris, orange-yellow bill, bare yellow/orange toes, feathered barred tarsi. Female about 80 g heavier while wing length nearly equal. Mesoptile dirty whitish/cream with narrow dusky barring; no precise age or wing geometry.

## Design boundary

Facts support tuftless dark adults, horizontal body barring, a documented bare epiphyte platform, and an age-unspecified cream/dusky mesoptile. V2 uses an alert low supported crouch, short soft developing wings and near-equal-height adults as conservative design decisions. It does not infer fledging age, flight ability, a universal nest type or a universal female-size rule.
