Adult: 51–63 cm, 2055–2325 g, wing 407–447 mm, wingspan 150–153 cm; ginger-brown, irregular barring/spotting, loose tousled crown but no real ear-tufts, weak facial disc, blackish-brown eyes, black bill/grey cere, bare straw legs/toes and fishing spicules. Downy chick white; mesoptile head/hindneck/underparts whitish with dull-yellow wash, wings/tail adult-like but paler/less rufous; first full plumage paler, similar to adult by about ten months. ## Evidence synthesis and design boundary

- Adults: dark eyes, black bill/grey cere, no true ear tufts, indistinct disc, ginger-rufous plumage with irregular dark bars/spots; bare straw lower legs/toes and long fishing claws. Mikkola and Branfield support a female-larger/paler direction, but no source read here justifies a dramatic display ratio, so v4 uses only a restrained difference.
- Juvenile: choose the documented **three-month captive stage**, not an invented newborn. Prague Zoo directly supports light colour plus residual powder down; Mikkola permits paler, partly adult-like wing/tail pattern at mesoptile. The design keeps the folded wing compact and powder-down-softened, tail short, posture bilateral and supported. Captive age evidence is not converted into a wild nest claim.
- Habitat: mature riverine forest and large natural cavity remain adult ecology context. The juvenile's exact wild equivalent, first-flight threshold, iris chronology, continuous feather-tract sequence, and population-wide sex-size ratio remain unknown.
- Scientific status: **not verified**; concept calibration only.
