## Evidence → design decisions

- Adults: earless compact pygmy-owls, pale greyish-tawny/brown, extensive pale crown spots, subdued wing marks, clean white throat/underparts with rusty-brown flank streaks. Both sexes share the palette. Female is only subtly fuller, not substantially taller.
- Tail: six restrained pale bars are a declared midpoint design inference between documented five and later six/seven summaries; not a universal anatomical claim.
- Immature: label exactly **AGE-UNKNOWN IMMATURE**. Plain soft grey crown with only sparse forehead flecks; dark/indeterminate irises; soft compact folded wing with no crisp adult covert rows; short tail mostly hidden; two feet visibly gripping a stable branch. No open-wing flight pose.
- Setting: dry western-Mexico palm/thorn-to-pine-oak mosaic, subdued enough not to compete with the birds. No occupied nest or cavity interior.
