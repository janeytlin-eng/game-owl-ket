- Scope: Luzon lowland unit only.
- Historical mapping: older broad treatments included *O. everetti* and *O. nigrorum*; these are not imported into this design.
- Production target: v2 revision; preserve v1.

## Evidence used for v2

- **PSO-JOSEF-2012** — first-person WBCP observation and viewed juvenile image: two rusty-brown fuzzy fledglings with formed flight feathers and branch grip; flash-red pupils are not iris color. Exact age unknown.
- **PSO-JOSEF-TANEDO-2013** — adult/family field material in Quezon City; supports gray-brown cryptic adult appearance, not sexed-pair measurements.
- **PSO-MIRANDA-2011** — primary taxonomic/morphometric paper, actual Methods/Table 2/Results read; female group means larger with overlap, and nominate scapular stripe/tarsal feathering are supported. No height formula or juvenile sequence.
- **PSO-MACAULAY-2010** — institutional archive page read; contributor noted an age/sex-unspecified juvenile in Mt Makiling canopy screeching at 04:00. Supports a canopy action context only, not plumage geometry.
