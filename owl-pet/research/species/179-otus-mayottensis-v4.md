## Newly read sources

### MAYS-MARSHALL-1978-P19
- **Title / author / year:** *Systematics of Smaller Asian Night Birds Based on Voice*; Joe T. Marshall Jr.; 1978; Ornithological Monographs No. 25.
- **URL:** https://sora.unm.edu/sites/default/files/journals/om/om025.pdf
- **Read:** printed p.19, PDF text lines 1310-1323 and specimen list lines 1353-1360.
- **Facts:** under the then-current name *Otus rutilus mayottensis*, Mayotte birds are described as large within that complex, with the distal 5 mm of the tarsus bare; back blackish brown marked with black; belly brownish red with a *magicus*-type pattern except in the red-phase specimen; black shaft streaks conspicuous. Four BM specimens including the holotype are listed.
- **Design support:** correct v3's overstatement “tarsi feathered to toes” to feathering ending just above a short visible distal bare segment; strengthen blackish-brown/black-shaft adult pattern. The source supplies no sex ratio or immature development.
- **Limits/confidence:** historical specimen comparison, not a population colour-frequency or ontogeny study; red-phase wording does not justify assigning morphs by sex. High for examined-specimen traits, low for frequency.

### MAYS-WARREN-1966-P22
- **Title / author / year:** *Type-Specimens of Birds in the British Museum (Natural History), Vol. 1: Non-Passerines*; Rachel L. M. Warren; 1966.
- **URL:** https://wallace-online.org/converted/supplementary/specimens/1966_TypesBMNH_WSPEC291.01.pdf
- **Read:** printed p.22, entry *Otus rutilus mayottensis*.
- **Facts:** holotype is adult male BMNH 1959.5.5, La Convalescence, 1200 ft, Mayotte, collected 2 Nov 1958 by C. W. Benson; original reference *Ibis* 103B:61.
- **Design support:** stable historical-name, type-locality and adult-male mapping only.
- **Limits/confidence:** type catalogue; one male cannot support population-wide dimorphism, colour frequency or juvenile traits.

## Evidence-to-design boundary

- Adults share one conservative non-red presentation; no sex-linked morph is inferred. Near-equal standing size because no quantified sex direction was found; female may be only subtly fuller as a neutral design cue, not a claimed dimorphism.
- Show feathered upper tarsi but a short distal bare segment before the toes, not fully feathered-to-toe tarsi and not broadly bare legs.
- Use blackish-brown upperparts, brownish-red underparts, conspicuous dark shaft streaks, restrained *magicus*-type pattern, yellow eyes and short low tufts.
- Young is an **age-unknown older immature**, not a nestling or claimed fledgling: softer and less regular than adults, compact rounded partly formed wing, mostly hidden short tail, bilateral branch support. Iris, exact age, sex, flight ability, feather chronology and nest context remain unknown.
- Mayotte humid secondary-forest context; no cavity, lining, eggs, prey or invented breeding action.
