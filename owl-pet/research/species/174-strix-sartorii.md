## Taxonomy boundary

Project follows current AOS/IOC/Clements species treatment *Strix sartorii*. Historical names include *Syrnium nebulosum* var. *Sartorii* and *Strix varia sartorii*. BirdLife historically retained a lumped treatment; the index records this conflict rather than silently rewriting it.

## Evidence boundary

- Adult diagnosis: dark eyes; pale gray facial disc without strong rings; cold gray-brown, blackish-sepia and off-white pattern; barred breast and vertically streaked belly; fully feathered toes.
- Habitat: Mexican pine/pine-oak forest, 1400–2500 m.
- Juvenile: a dated 2015 Nayarit photo supports an age-unknown older juvenile/fledgling. Secondary synthesis reports developed flight feathers with downy body plumage, but exact age and flight capability remain unknown.
- Sex: no species-specific visual dimorphism source was recovered. Adults stay near-equal; any female fullness is deliberately subtle and not a scientific claim.
- Nest architecture, clutch and early down sequence remain unknown and are excluded.
