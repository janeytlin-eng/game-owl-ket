## Rule iteration

When a source says flight feathers are “considerably developed” in a down-rich chick, do not collapse the design to either a tiny wing bud or an adult-perfect folded wing. Render a bounded, stage-supported feather cluster; separately check length relative to feet, down coverage, tail concealment and action ability. This rule was applied and visually verified for #120 v4 only; broader effectiveness is not yet claimed.
