# #162 Northern Saw-whet Owl — *Aegolius acadicus*

- Adult: tiny, round, tuftless; orange-yellow eyes; black bill/cere; finely white-streaked rusty/gray-brown crown; coffee-brown spotted upperparts; whitish underparts with broad rusty streaks; short rounded wing/tail.
- Sex: same plumage; female only modestly fuller, no fixed height ratio.
- Late nestling: coffee-brown soft mesoptile; dark face with pale X/Y brows/forehead/lores; yellowish eyes; plain back/belly relative to adult; short rounded developing wing/tail; supported in cavity.
- Habitat: mature moist conifer/mixed woodland; old woodpecker cavity in snag is supported.
- Prohibit: ear tufts, adult streaked white belly on juvenile, crisp adult spot rows, long hard primaries, flight pose, unsupported feet or exact-day label.
