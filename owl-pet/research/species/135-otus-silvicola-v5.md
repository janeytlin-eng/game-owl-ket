## Current and historical names

Current name: *Otus silvicola* (Wallace, 1864), Wallace's Scops-Owl. ITIS TSN 555386 and Avibase retain *Scops silvicola* as the original/protonym combination; Avibase links the original description to Proceedings of the Zoological Society of London, 1863 part 3 p.487 and records Flores as type locality.

## Evidence actually read or re-read

### WSC-MEES-2006 — reused primary source, institutional page and species account re-read
G. F. Mees, 2006, “The avifauna of Flores (Lesser Sunda Islands),” *Zoologische Mededelingen* 80(3):1–261. Naturalis record: https://repository.naturalis.nl/pub/209697; species account printed pp.113–115, especially p.114. The account says only two available birds were adults, one of each sex; other specimens were fledglings with remiges/rectrices basally still in sheath. The type was fully feathered and transitional, with loose fine barring on head, neck, upper back and most breast plus only a few adult dark-streaked feathers emerging. No obvious adult sex-plumage difference was reported. This supports a fully feathered transitional immature—not a hatchling—but does not establish exact age or flight ability. Any apparent sex-size direction rests on one adult of each sex and is not encoded as a rule.

### WSC-MIK2013-183 — reused pinned guide evidence
Heimo Mikkola, *Owls of the World: A Photographic Guide*, 2nd ed. (2013), printed p.183. Previously read repository evidence states no data on sexual size differences; adult diagnosis includes long tufts without white inner borders, no pale collar, cold light grey-brown upperparts, herringbone shaft streaks, sparse ventral streaks/cross-bars, dull orange-yellow eyes, greyish-horn bill/cere and dense tarsal/toe-base feathering. The uploaded guide was not mounted in this execution; this is reuse, not a fresh page claim.

### WSC-ITIS-555386-2026 — reused taxonomy
ITIS TSN 555386, https://www.itis.gov/servlet/SingleRpt/SingleRpt?print_version=PRT&search_topic=TSN&search_value=555386&source=to_print. Current valid *Otus silvicola* and synonym/original combination *Scops silvicola*. Taxonomy only.

### WSC-MARSHALL-1978 and SLSO-SANGSTER-2013 — reused adult morphology
Prior repository readings support long tufts without white inner border, no white collar, dull orange iris, sparse ventral pattern, dense tarsal/proximal-toe feathering, cold-grey-brown identity, white belly centre and toe-base feathering. They do not supply known-age development or a population-level sex ratio.

### WSC-AVIBASE-2026 — newly indexed taxonomy crosswalk

It maps current *Otus silvicola* to Wallace 1864, protonym *Scops silvicola*, Flores type locality, Wallace's Scops Owl / historical Lesser Sunda Scops-Owl naming and current checklist treatment. It supports nomenclature/range only, not plumage, development or sex-size claims.
