**But not an acceptable rightmost mesoptile:**
1. Original concept shows large plush cream/ivory down, rounded crouch, very broad integrated heart disc and two dark open eyes. Generated result has strongly contrasting dark facial holes/rim and a smaller, mask-like front; open eyes are not faithfully represented. It reads as an older barn-owl-like bird, not the required age-unknown fluffy chick.
2. Side and back display conspicuous **large layered adult flight feather/wing plates**, a wedge-shaped overlong rear torso and hard/scaly plumage, not dense fine juvenile cream down and short down-buried wings.
3. Colour is grayish beige/brown rather than a unified soft cream coat, with overly hard high-frequency quill shapes; no convincing natural down fibers or down silhouette.
4. Feet appear as unsupported claws on a dark empty stage rather than two broad supported feet with belly against a substantial old eucalypt hollow lip; tail/feet anatomy and origin concept not consistently reconciled across views.
5. No actual hardware mobile iOS/Android WebGL GPU, touch orbit, FPS/thermal or groom LOD test in this run. Three.js browser testing on previous models proves only their own technical loading, NOT this new model's mobile suitability. Model/weights plus original design license and global distribution rights not signed off.
