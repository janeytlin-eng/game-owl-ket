### Actual human review of six 390×780 WebGL2 views
Both models were loaded in actual desktop Chromium using SwiftShader WebGL2 and captured from **front, right and back** cameras. All image hashes differ across views. Actual three-view contact sheets were opened side by side against the original fluffy cream chick.

**Adult barn owl — ART FAIL.** Front face has an abrupt rigid plate with two small dark dot eyes and yellow oversized beak, unnatural disc and chunky scallop chest. Side view becomes a bizarre horizontal, elongated boat/beak shape with enormous rigid leaf wings and adult tail; back is two flat blade-like folded wings. Legs are stick-like and completely wrong for the down-enveloped compact chick. White-cream color from maps does not create soft down. Despite apparent professional-sounding listing (`premium`, `modelled feather ranks`), actual model is unmistakably **simple stylized flat geometry**, not a high-quality organic *Tyto castanops* juvenile. Not acceptable even as a high-detail sculpt foundation.

**Tree stump with hollow — ART FAIL.** Front shows a simple short, rigid cylinder with a dark knothole; side and rear are virtually featureless flat tapering stump sides. It lacks credible rugged senescent eucalypt bark, natural depth, irregular old hollow geometry or the specific horizontal lip supporting juvenile feet. It cannot be used unmodified as real habitat artwork.
