# 60 · Tawny Fish Owl — *Ketupa flavipes*

## Taxonomy

Current name: *Ketupa flavipes* (Hodgson, 1836). Historical combination: *Bubo flavipes*. Protonym: *Cultrunguis flavipes*. Treat all as one species.

## Evidence facts

- Orange-rufous fish owl with messy lateral ear tufts, yellow eyes, weak facial disc, broad dark feather-centre marks and a contrasting dingy-yellow scapular band.
- Upper roughly two-thirds of tarsus feathered; lower tarsus/toes greenish-yellow.
- Mountain-stream and intact riparian-forest specialist; fast-flowing water is appropriate.
- Species-specific 1997 nest paper exists, but accessible evidence in this run did not establish exact juvenile age or a continuous feather sequence.

## Design decisions

- Adults share plumage and near-equal visible height; female only subtly fuller.
- Juvenile uses an age-unknown dependent nest-edge stage with natural substrate support, down-softened contour, short rounded wing buds and very short tail.
- No mature remiges, independent-flight pose, precise age, subspecies reconstruction or sex-specific measurements are claimed.
