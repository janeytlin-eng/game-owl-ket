## 2026-10-27 v2 calibration delta

- **New primary evidence:** `MRO-THORSTROM-NEST-1996` was visually read from the scanned newsletter (pp. 9–11; Figs. 1–3). This resolves the earlier retrieval gap.
- **Juvenile stage:** at the first known nest, two 6–7-week young later fledged at about 10–11 weeks. Fledged young were fully plumaged and generally browner than adults with reddish-orange facial/body overcast. V2 therefore uses a recently fledged, branch-supported juvenile—not an invented downy hatchling—and keeps its plumage browner/softer than the adults.
- **Nest/habitat:** natural cavity in a partly dead *Weinmannia* about 23 m high in primary humid rainforest near Ambanizana, Masoala Peninsula. Treat this as one observed nest, not universal.
- **Sex boundary:** the measured adult male was smaller than the female, but display should use moderate relative body volume, not an exact standing-height ratio.
- **Still unknown:** continuous daily feather sequence between 6 and 11 weeks and population-wide variability.
