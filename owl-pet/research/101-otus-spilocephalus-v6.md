# #101 Mountain Scops Owl — *Otus spilocephalus hambroecki* — v6 research

- Accessed: 2026-10-29
- Action: science revision of canonical #101; v1–v5 retained
- Scope: Taiwan subspecies representative, not a universal rendering of every race
- Source index: MOSO-MIKKOLA-2013-P148, MOSO-NTM-HAMBROECKI-2026, MSO-FOREST2005, MOSO-NMNS-DIGITAL-2026, MOSO-NPS-TAIWAN-2026
- Current taxon: *Otus spilocephalus*; Taiwan race *O. s. hambroecki*; older material may use *Ephialtes Hambroecki*.

## Evidence

Mikkola printed p.148/PDF p.150 describes an older juvenile as duller reddish-brown and fluffier, with narrow dark crown bars, broader back bars and very faint underpart barring. The National Taiwan Museum record states sexes share plumage and describes yellow eyes, cream-yellow bill, feathered tarsi, bare pink-brown toes, yellow-brown juveniles with vague markings and natural tree hollows. The 2005 Taiwan forestry report records late-June fledglings on branches adjacent to nest trees, mostly flapping/walking with weak flight. The two newly indexed Taiwan institution pages independently support near-equal adult presentation, compact short-tufted yellow-billed identity and dense mountain forest.

## Limits

Exact hatch age, juvenile iris and sex, continuous feather-tract sequence, first-flight threshold, quantified adult dimorphism and universal hollow dimensions remain unknown. The v6 board must remain `scientifically_verified=false`.

## Design decision

Create a full new v6 plate in the established style. Preserve three-bird layout and canonical #101 numbering. Depict one older supported fledgling with fluffy yellow/rufous-brown plumage, blurred markings and compact formed wings appropriate to documented weak flight; forbid crisp adult-regular remex rows and a long exposed tail. Adults share plumage and display height.
