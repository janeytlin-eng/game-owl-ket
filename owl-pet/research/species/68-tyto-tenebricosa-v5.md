Hyem, E. L. (1979), “Observations on Owls in the Upper Manning River District, N.S.W.”, *Corella* 3(2):17–25. PDF: https://absa.asn.au/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/Corella-3-2-17-25.pdf ; pp.20–22 read 2026-11-05.

- A nest hollow contained two young estimated at about six weeks and clothed in sooty-grey down.
- A separately described nest contained one chick with facial discs well defined and wing/tail feathers showing through sooty-grey down.
- Recorded nest hollows varied from about 40 cm to 5 m deep; one Blue Gum hollow was more than 3 m deep and about 22 m above ground. Those examples demonstrate variation, not a universal cavity template.

## Conflict resolution and design inference

1. **Sex scale:** strong reversed sexual dimorphism exists in morphometric data, but Hyem's direct pair observations varied in apparent direction. V5 therefore keeps comparable adult crown height/camera distance and allows only modest female fullness/length. Labels identify sex; silhouette is not a universal field sex key.
2. **Juvenile stage:** v4's recent fledgling remains a valid later-stage treatment. V5 intentionally uses the independently documented earlier cavity stage: approximately six weeks, sooty-grey down, defined facial discs, and wing/tail feathers just emerging through the down. It is not a newborn and not a miniature adult.
3. **Action:** the chick stays low and completely supported inside the hollow. No branch, launch, flight or unsupported standing claim is made.
4. **Nest/environment:** a large roomy old-tree hollow in moist southeast-Australian rainforest/eucalypt forest is supported. Exact depth, entrance size, height and tree species are not universalized.

## Unknowns / limitations

Exact age and sex of the separately described feather-emergence chick; continuous feather-tract chronology; locomotor or flight competence at six weeks; population-wide visual sex ratio; subspecies transfer; and universal cavity geometry remain unknown. The phrase “approximately six-week cavity chick” applies to the documented stage, not every individual. V5 must remain `PASS_WITH_LIMITATIONS` / science false.
