## Actually modeled, iterated and checked
Created and executed `build_run72.py` and registered correction `build_run72_registered.py`. Unlike more alpha-shell/needle groom retries, this experiment deforms the **actual continuous 59,520-triangle body mesh**, builds shallow recessed periocular cavities and cheek folds, and tests separate volumetric very small anterior corneas with optical highlights. Target eye registration was measured from the **actual parent front render**: original dark regions centered near screen pixel (247,160)/(319,158) at 512px; initial naive symmetric corneas created visibly duplicated eyes. The corrective E/F iteration maps corneas to asymmetric model-local x ≈ -0.029/+0.190 without modifying the original photographic texture. This corrects the obvious double-eye rendering in the front/three-quarter views. An independent F variant also tested burying the pale juvenile shank and warming the skin/toe colors; it produces anatomically unconvincing clipped shanks, so it cannot be accepted.

Four independent, actually exported and **reimported true 3D GLBs** were produced: C, D, E, F; each **21 meshes, 80,644 triangles**. E **3,850,812 bytes**, SHA256 `24e9fae8c34a3e57de1c6ee476cd77dc1d73249eec1e73438c27acd3b58c6881`; F **3,845,344 bytes**, SHA256 `0424808436f1f85d5177d5701bd21a7b0856bfb09adc0aa5318f140e285d4174`. E/F max body vertex displacement ≈0.03386 model units versus Run71 A. Welded geometric-UV seam body QA: closed=true, winding-consistent=true, positive signed volume ≈0.50476. No SVG, still image substitute or simple whole-body geometric primitive is being presented as a successful 3D pet.

## Visual quality gate — **ART_REJECTED**
Carefully examined the actual reference, four-view VTK sheets, front comparison and a real WebGL three-quarter portrait screenshot. Registered glossy corneas reduce front-view texture-only eye appearance, but the underlying design remains an abrupt smooth trapezoidal/bag-like silhouette; face is mainly frontal photo projection, short down-buried wings are visually absent, side/back stay smooth and non-anatomical, feet are blocky or incompletely hidden, and the cream down does not have credible fluffy mass. The eye-registration fix is useful only as isolated failure analysis. **None of C/D/E/F satisfies high-quality infant owl character or real phone quality requirements. Not publishable, not a demo.**
