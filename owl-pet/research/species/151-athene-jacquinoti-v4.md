## Evidence synthesis and v4 correction

The current combination is *Athene jacquinoti*, while the 2011 type catalogue and MNHN determination history preserve the older *Ninox* treatment; both are recorded instead of silently conflated. One male type plus an age/sex-unspecified living image cannot establish female size or plumage, so the adults remain near equal. The v3 juvenile’s closed eyes were a presentation choice, not a developmental observation. V4 uses an **open-eyed, age-unknown older juvenile design inference** with dark non-diagnostic irides, soft reduced mottling, short down-veiled wing above the feet, concealed tail and two-foot low branch support. No exact age, iris chronology, flight threshold, nest material, hatchling appearance or sex-size direction is claimed.
