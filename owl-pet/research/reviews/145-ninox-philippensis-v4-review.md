## Problem → evidence → modification → recheck

1. **v3 young had adult-yellow eyes.** Sharpe 1875 pp.167–168 does not supply iris colour. v4 deliberately uses a dark/uncalibrated iris. Full and tight crop confirm no yellow juvenile iris.
2. **v3 young had an elongated adult-like folded wing.** Sharpe directly records formed quills but not age or flight ability. v4 retains formed quills while shortening and rounding the folded wing, limiting pale notches and keeping the bird stationary with both feet supported. Crop confirms the wing is visibly shorter/softer than adult wings.
3. **Old Leyte nestling record could invite invention.** Parkes 1973 reports a now-lost nestling male under a pre-split name, with no plumage or behaviour detail. It was indexed only as a taxon-history warning and not used visually.

- **Support/action:** all three birds have both feet visibly gripping the same broad branch; young is stationary, not flying or begging.
- **Habitat:** humid Luzon evergreen-forest context; no nest claim.
