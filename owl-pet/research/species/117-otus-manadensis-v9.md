## Source facts versus design inference

**Source facts:** Marshall 1978 p.13 supports sepia/fuscous adult colour, orange-yellow iris direction, feathering over the tarsus-to-toe joint, and bold sparse white-ground posterior streaking with freckled crossbar remnants. eBird supports juvenile heavier crown/back barring. The dated Masarang photograph supports only a visible age-unknown immature with formed feathers and branch support. Clements supports the current monotypic mapping.

**Design inference:** an older, branch-perching juvenile is selected because this is the directly documented stage. Short compact folded wings, modest tail, irregular gray-brown ventral texture and two-foot support are bounded visual decisions, not a dated growth series. Adult male and female remain near-equal visible scale; pose differences are not biological measurements.

**Unknown:** exact juvenile age, sex, flight ability, continuous feather sequence, literal ventral feather map, population-wide iris exceptions, exact toe-feather boundary, and quantified sex-size ratio. `scientifically_verified=false`.
