## Evidence-bounded design decision

- Adults: common dark-brown morph; rounded untufted head, yellow bare parts, fine crown/nape dots, white throat, paired nape false eyes, irregular breast band, marked flanks and short banded tail.
- Sex: cited masses point heavier in the small secondary sample, but no standing-height or sexed linear measurements were recovered. Display near-equal crown height; the female may be subtly fuller only.
- Juvenile: age unknown, end-of-nest-life stage. Plain or finely streaked crown/nape, weaker diffuse underparts, soft formed wing and short tail, dusky/indeterminate iris, both feet supported at a mossy cavity lip. A low cautious sidestep adds natural posture diversity without asserting flight.
- Unknown: exact age/sex, iris chronology, continuous wing/tail sequence, first-flight threshold, independence and population-wide sex-size ratio.
- Nest/environment: humid Andean cloud forest and a natural mossy cavity are design context, not a claimed measured nest record.
