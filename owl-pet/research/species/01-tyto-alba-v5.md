# #01 Western Barn Owl — *Tyto alba* — v5 research and design boundary

## Why this revision

Versions 1–4 remain preserved. V3’s juvenile look was user-approved on 2026-10-10; v4 corrected the habitat to a sheltered timber barn. V5 does not overwrite either decision. It adds a stage-specific scientific boundary for an approximately 15-day nestling so posture, support and wing development are inspectable.

## Evidence actually read

- `WBO-KOEPPL-2005`: primary quill begins emerging on average at P13, unfurls around P25, adult length around P67; total wing growth is slow through P12 and reaches adult values only around P60–65.
- `WBO-EILAM-HAGBI-2024`: eyes open around P10–12; P11–20 chicks keep the belly low and rest on heels/tarsometatarsal segments; tall adult-like standing starts around P30; active flight is much later, around P50–60.
- `WBO-MIK2013-78-80`: user-uploaded guide, actual PDF pp.80–82 / printed pp.78–80. It supports adult identification, first white natal plumage and the caution that photographed sexes can be similar in size/colour even though females are sometimes heavier and usually darker/more spotted.
- Reused `WBO-BOT-IDAGE2026`, `WBO-BOT-NEST2026`, `WBO-BOT-BUILD2026` for bare toes/flesh eyelids, unconstructed nest substrate and sheltered barn context.

## Design boundaries

- Exactly three birds: adult male, approximately 15-day nestling, adult female.
- Adults are Western Barn Owls, not American *T. furcata* or Eastern *T. javanica*; near-equal standing height, with only a modest darker/more-spotted female direction.
- Chick: white down dominant; open dark eyes with flesh-pink eyelid margins; small pale bill; belly, heels/tarsometatarsi and both feet naturally supported on flat barn wood/debris.
- Wings: only tiny emerging quill tips/short sheaths at the sides; no layered remiges, regular adult spots/bars, tail fan, tall standing, branch perch or flight.
- Barn recess: timber/stone shelter with sparse pellets/debris, not a woven nest; all three birds must have believable contact shadows/support.
- Unknowns remain explicit: exact day, sex, individual subspecies, precise feather count and universal timing. `scientifically_verified: false`.
