### `ELWO-LIGON-1968-UMMZ-P34-35` — new, primary

J. David Ligon, 1968, *The Biology of the Elf Owl, Micrathene whitneyi*, University of Michigan Museum of Zoology, Miscellaneous Publications No. 136. Original PDF: https://deepblue.lib.umich.edu/bitstream/2027.42/56380/1/MP136.pdf. Accessed 2026-11-04. Printed pp. 34–35, including Figure 8 and the developmental chronology, were read.

Source facts:
- nestling period 28–33 days;
- 0–2 d chicks have thick white down, pink skin and large feet;
- 9–10 d birds sit up; feather tips begin to emerge;
- 17–18 d birds have body contour feathers, a short visible tail, upper wing coverts more exposed than remiges, feet almost bare, and much head down;
- at 21–22 d first flight attempts occur, but the tail is only about 1.7 cm and mostly sheathed, and the longest primary is 3.8 cm with 2.1 cm still ensheathed;
- 25–26 d birds still cannot fly effectively; flight capability appears around 27–28 d.

Limit: the chronology is a historical Arizona study and does not justify an exact-age portrait for every individual or subspecies; it is not a color plate.
