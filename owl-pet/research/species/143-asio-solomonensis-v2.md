## Evidence facts used

Loyn & Debus (2000), printed pp.288–289 / PDF pp.1–2, directly documents one rescued Bougainville young: active walking/bouncing and railing perching; long unbarred pale down on head and underparts; dark facial mask; emerging barred brown dorsal/wing feathers; orange-yellow eyes; blue-grey cere; black bill; grey feet. BirdNET+ v0.4-Oct2026 supplies the current *Asio solomonensis* display name. The existing OwlPages adult account is reused only for the broad adult identification direction.

## Design boundary

Use the photographed older juvenile stage, not a hatchling or generic *Asio* fledgling. Show long unbarred pale down over head and underparts, a dark mask and only short emerging barred wing/dorsal feathers. The bird may stand/perch securely because the paper records walking, bouncing and railing perching; do not show flight. Adults are near equal scale because no reliable quantified sex ratio was found. A forest-edge branch/wooden rail translates the documented rescue context without asserting a universal nest.
