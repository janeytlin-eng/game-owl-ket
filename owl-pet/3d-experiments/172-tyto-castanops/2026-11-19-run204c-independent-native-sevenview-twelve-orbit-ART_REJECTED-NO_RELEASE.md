## Independent visual audit: definite artistic rejection

The seven-camera evidence was actually opened, not inferred from counts. A second local offscreen VTK importer (software EGL) reimported the original GLB and independently rendered twelve yaw angles at 30-degree increments to investigate orientation and side/back consistency. **World-axis poses are not calibrated bird anatomy:** the native mesh is effectively sideways in this coordinate system, so neither set of world-axis labels should be mistaken for literal bird front/back without orientation adjustment.

Nevertheless, direct multiview observation is decisive: at its recognizable mask view, the heart-shaped face is recessed/deformed rather than smooth and continuous; eye structures are dark irregular cavities. The back and most side views become an **almost black/charcoal, coarse wrinkled/scaly elongated oval**, with no believable airy cream juvenile down. Feet/claws protrude awkwardly as isolated lumpy geometry, and no broad old-eucalypt hollow lip supports the chick. The front-only appearance is not consistent around its sides/back. These are **hard morphological and coloration mismatches**, not a minor color grade or lack of animation. The asset's 36,212 triangles alone do not constitute a high-quality character. Artist acceptance: **ART_REJECTED**.

The exact SHA256 was added to `known_rejected_asset_sha256_registry.json` on this research branch to prevent future recolor/LOD/reuse of the rejected result as approved art. The local 12-orbit supplementary screenshots were not publicly distributed as any product sample; the original GLB + seven renders remain preserved within the expiring isolated GitHub artifact.
