#148 宿务鹰鸮 / Cebu Boobook

- Scientific name: *Ninox rumseyi*
- Taxonomy: Rasmussen et al. 2012; formerly within the Philippine hawk-owl complex.
- Concepts: [v1](../../design/concepts/148-ninox-rumseyi-v1.png) · [v2](../../design/concepts/148-ninox-rumseyi-v2.png)
- Scientific verification: false

Evidence read: Cornell/eBird identification (CEB-EBIRD-2026), BirdLife factsheet (CEB-BIRDLIFE-2026), Rasmussen et al. 2012 public abstract/diagnosis excerpt (CEB-RASMUSSEN-2012-LEAD), and Jakosalem, Collar & Gill 2012 habitat-study abstract (CEB-JAKOSALEM-2012). Adults: earless rounded head, dark-brown weakly barred upperparts, orange-brown underparts, white wing speckles, pale shoulder bar, bright yellow eyes, white eyebrow V and large white throat. Sexes visually similar; no strong dimorphism imposed. Juvenile evidence remains inadequate; v1 uses an age-unknown older fluffy bird with shortened rounded wing/tail and two-foot support. Full primary papers were not fully retrieved in this run, so exact juvenile sequence, sex ratio, nesting details and feather geometry remain unknown.

v2 full-text correction (2026-11-01): Rasmussen et al. printed pp.16–17 specifies short separate whitish supercilia rather than a continuous white V, strongly/narrowly barred upperparts, variable weakly marked warm underparts, pale-to-bright lemon-yellow irides, dull pale-olive bill/cere, bright-yellow feet, half-feathered tarsi and mostly black claws with pale bases. Jakosalem, Collar & Gill's full field results document a radio-tracked juvenile and two natural trunk-cavity nests, but give no juvenile plumage or explicit flight threshold. v2 therefore keeps adults near equal and the older juvenile age unknown, eyes closed, softly patterned and fully supported. Image edit/recheck result is PASS_WITH_LIMITATIONS; science remains false. See [v2 research](../../research/species/148-ninox-rumseyi-v2.md).
