## Sources actually read

### BRU-KHOURY-ALSHAMLIH-DHEIB-2021-P286-287 — new primary field record

- Fares Khoury, Mohammed Alshamlih & Ahmad Dheib, “The first breeding record of Pallid Scops Owl *Otus brucei* in Jordan”, *Sandgrouse* 43 (2021):286–287.
- URL: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/357327177_The_first_breeding_record_of_Pallid_Scops_Owl_Otus_brucei_in_Jordan
- Author-uploaded full text actually read 2026-11-06, printed pp.286–287, especially p.287.
- Facts: in old Jordan Valley palm plantations, the observers heard juvenile hissing/begging calls and saw two recently fledged juveniles together. The visible young had little ear tufts, a rather short tail, and fine cross-barring on breast and belly without streaks.
- Limits: one short night observation and a low-quality plate; no hatch date, sex, measured wing geometry, sustained-flight test or continuous feather sequence.
- Design decision: use a supported **RECENT FLEDGLING**, short tail, little tufts, fine transverse juvenile bars and an open-beak begging pose; do not infer an exact day or flight threshold.

### BRU-ITIS-555375-2026 — new taxonomy record

- Integrated Taxonomic Information System, TSN 555375, report opened 2026-11-06.
- URL: https://itis.gov/servlet/SingleRpt/SingleRpt?search_topic=TSN&search_value=555375
- Facts: valid current name *Otus brucei* (Hume, 1872); listed synonym/original combination *Ephialtes brucei* Hume, 1872; four direct subspecies are listed.
- Limits/design use: taxonomy and old-name mapping only; no morphology, sex or development inference.

### BRU-SANGHA-MALIK-2010-P176-177 — new adult field observation

- Harkirat Singh Sangha & Dhanraj Malik, “Observations on wintering Pallid Scops Owl *Otus brucei* at Zainabad”, *Indian Birds* 5(6):176–177 (2010).
- URL: https://indianbirds.in/pdfs/IB_5.6.176-177.pdf
- Full two-page PDF text read 2026-11-06, printed pp.176–177.
- Facts: nominate wintering birds were grey/creamy-grey with fine sharp dark streaks; posture and apparent roundness changed with relaxed versus sleeked plumage and ear-tuft position.
- Limits: sex unknown and wintering adults only; posture variation cannot be converted into sex-size difference.
- Design decision: fine streaks and restrained small tufts; do not map relaxed/dumpy posture to female or sleek posture to male.

### BRU-BENDOV-KIAT2016 — re-read

- Amir Ben Dov & Yosef Kiat, *Dutch Birding* 38:146–158 (2016), printed pp.151–152 and 156–157.
- Reused facts: mature date-palm cavities; about-26-day fledging for the studied population; young pale sandy-grey with deep-yellow iris, pale eyebrow and fine horizontal bars.
- Boundary correction: population “about 26 days” does not date the Jordan individual or prove universal first-flight competence.

### BRU-CHANDRAN-2016 and BRU-MIK2013-126 — reused

- Chandran et al. 2016 remains the adult identification boundary: uniformly pale grey/sandy ground, long thin dark shaft streaks, weak variable crossbars, subtle buff scapulars and no bold white adult patches.
- Repository-pinned Mikkola printed pp.126–127/PDF pp.128–129 was reused; the uploaded file is not mounted in this run, so no fresh page read is claimed. Its female-heavier direction has no standing-height ratio and supports only slight fullness.

## Design boundaries

- Adults: near-equal visible height/wing length; female only subtly fuller. Colour and posture are not sex keys.
- Juvenile: recent fledgling, exact age/sex/subspecies unknown; no claim of sustained flight.
- Palm cavity/plantation: documented regional example, not universal nesting geometry.
- Current/old name: *Otus brucei* = *Ephialtes brucei* Hume, 1872.
