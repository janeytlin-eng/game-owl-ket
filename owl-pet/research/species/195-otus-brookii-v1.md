# 195 · Rajah Scops Owl · *Otus brookii solokensis* v1

## Taxon and scope
- Current roster species: *Otus brookii*; concept scope is the Sumatran race *O. b. solokensis*.
- Historical protonym for the species: *Scops brookii* Sharpe, 1892 (Cornell/Avibase).
- The uploaded guide treats Bornean nominate *brookii* and Sumatran *solokensis* as subspecies and notes that some authorities may split them. This v1 does not resolve that taxonomic question.

## Sources actually read (2026-10-28)
- **RAJ-MIK2013-P168** — Heimo Mikkola, *Owls of the World: A Photographic Guide* (2013), printed p.168 / uploaded PDF p.170; page text and pixels read. Adult: 21–25 cm; long tufts with white inner webs; broad white crown-side band; deep dark/reddish-brown irregular mottling; white double collar; orange-yellow to dark-orange eyes; pale yellow bill; heavily feathered tarsi. Females are said to be a little larger, without weights. Juvenile mesoptile is reddish-brown with fine dark cross-bars and whitish-rufous underside freckled with darker reddish-brown/dark streaks. The solokensis caption states browner/yellower upperparts, three distinct hindneck/nuchal collars and broader blackish shaft-streaks. Habitat 900–2500 m, mainly 1200–2400 m.
