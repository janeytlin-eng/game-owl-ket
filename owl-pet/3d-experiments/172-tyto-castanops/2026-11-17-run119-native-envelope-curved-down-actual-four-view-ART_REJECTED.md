## Actual visual comparison: worse than acceptable, ART_REJECTED
I opened the real four-view contact from GitHub and compared it with Run117's four real views and the documented rightmost v3 design. The new cream material and physical fibers do **not** resolve the underlying biological/anatomical problem. In fact the face and coat visibly degrade.

1. **Heart disc disappears:** front is an excessively white, almost circular face with two small button-like brown eyes instead of a distinct, broad, warm-buff, integrated heart-shaped mask. Ear/crown and cheek interpretation are incorrect.
2. **Severe material/surface artifacts:** large black angular holes/patches appear across both breast, sides and back, alternating with blown-out ivory areas. This is a serious visual shading/material/geometry failure, not fine natural down.
3. **Wrong lateral and rear anatomy persists:** the 45-degree and profile renders retain a long backward slope and developed layered flight-feather tracts; the back has conspicuous unnatural cutout-like striping. The side and rear do not plausibly represent one soft, compact juvenile.
4. **Groom quality fails:** thousands of genuine fibers create only a bristly outer edge; they cannot hide the huge sculpted rigid feathers or create natural dense cream down.
5. **No convincing perch/support:** both feet appear dangling, with no aged eucalypt hollow lip or shared weight-bearing body contact. The source cannot be corrected adequately by whitening/softening alone.

The target is a faithful, attractive **true three-dimensional juvenile** from all directions, not simply an importable GLB. **Run119 is not visually close to an acceptable pet. Do not publish or link it as a finished sample.**
