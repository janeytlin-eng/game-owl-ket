## Evidence boundary

Adult direction is supported by the eBird identification account and photographs: earless, short-tailed, dark rufous-brown above, barred scapulars, variable wing/tail marks, dark face without a prominent white eyebrow, yellowish bare parts, plain pale throat, dark barred upper breast and barred pale lower underparts. Forest/edge habitat and range are supported by eBird and BirdLife. Taxonomic/name mapping is supported by Avibase.

No reliable species-specific juvenile development sequence or quantified sex-size difference was found. Adults are therefore shown near equal scale. The juvenile is explicitly an age-unknown older juvenile inference, with reduced soft barring, compact short rounded wing/tail and two-foot branch support; it is not evidence of an exact nestling stage.

## Review and iteration

The first generated juvenile carried adult-like long layered remiges. It was rejected and edited. The final review confirmed exactly three birds, retained adult diagnostic traits, shortened and rounded the juvenile wing, softened juvenile barring, and preserved natural two-foot support. Labels and title are correct. Science status remains false because juvenile sequence, sex ratio and fine feather geometry are unresolved.
