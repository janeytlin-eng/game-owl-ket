## Name and older combinations

Current project name: *Strix sartorii* (Ridgway, 1874), Cinereous Owl. Historical combinations retained: *Strix varia sartorii* and *Syrnium nebulosum* var. *sartorii*. AOS 2021 treats it as a species; older checklists treated it within Barred Owl.

## Actually read evidence

### CIO-BAIRD-RIDGWAY-1874-P29 — full public-domain account read
- Baird, Brewer & Ridgway, 1874, *A History of North American Birds*, vol. III, printed p.29, Project Gutenberg text: https://www.gutenberg.org/files/54169/54169-h/54169-h.htm
- Adult female type USNM 43,131 from Mirador, Veracruz “pine region”: blackish-sepia and clear white; brown much darker/less reddish than Barred Owl; lower markings nearly black; face plain dirty white without the darker bars/semicircles of *nebulosum*.
- Same primary-spot and tail-band counts as *nebulosum*. Type measurements: wing 14.80 in, tail 9.00, culmen 1.05, tarsus 2.20, middle toe 1.60.
- **Design use:** strong cold-dark adult palette and an absolute “no dark concentric facial rings” gate.
- **Limit:** one adult female type; no male comparison, living soft parts or development.

### CIO-PIEPLOW-ETAL-2020-P221-227 — full paper re-read and newly indexed
- Pieplow et al. 2020, *Western Birds* 51:221–227, DOI 10.21199/WB51.3.3; full PDF: https://archive.westernfieldornithologists.org/archive/V51/51%283%29-p221-p227.pdf
- Printed pp.221–223 confirm scattered historic records at ~600–3,000 m; montane pine/pine–oak forest; Rancho La Noria/Cerro San Juan relatively humid pine–oak near 1,650 m. Surveys in 2016, 2017 and 2019 detected the species only at that Nayarit site.
- **Design use:** current-site habitat and rarity context only.
- **Limit:** not a sex or developmental study.

### CIO-EARBIRDING-2015-FLEDGLING — direct field account re-read and newly indexed
- Nathan Pieplow & Andrew Spencer, 7 June 2015, “Mexico’s Mystery Owl”: https://earbirding.com/blog/archives/4905
- Lines 25–32 directly identify a begging fledgling; observers returned before dawn, reported that the same young bird flew out of sight, heard two apparent feeding events, and later observed an adult.
- The same account distinguishes cold grey Cinereous plumage from warm Fulvous and notes toe feathering down to claw bases.
- **Design use:** **age-unknown flight-capable fledgling**, still shown supported; fully feathered toes; cold rather than warm palette.
- **Limit:** one fledgling and one adult at one site; no exact age, early down sequence, nest or sex measurement.

### CIO-AOS-2021-P263 — current checklist treatment revalidated and newly indexed
- American Ornithological Society, 62nd Supplement, 2021, printed p.263, DOI https://doi.org/10.1093/ornithology/ukab037
- Species rank; protonym *Syrnium nebulosum* var. *Sartorii*; pine/pine–oak 1,400–2,500 m checklist habitat; separation based on vocal, mtDNA, plumage and habitat evidence.
- **Limit:** taxonomy/habitat, not development.

### CIO-MEXICANBIRDING-2020-PHOTO — new supplemental adult photo page
- Mexican Birding / Antonio Robles, Rancho La Noria, July 2020; page read 2026-11-07: https://www.mexicanbirding.com/cinereous-owl/
- Page and photograph show a real adult in humid forest and list vertical dark-brown chest/belly streaking, grey-brown facial disc and buff-brown neck bars.
- **Design use:** supplemental living-adult/habitat corroboration.
- **Limit/confidence:** photographer/travel field page, not a museum or measurement study; sex not established.

## Fact, inference and unknowns

- **Fact:** adults cold blackish-sepia/white, dark-eyed, unringed face; toe feathers reach claw bases; Nayarit fledgling was directly observed flying and apparently being fed.
- **Design inference:** male and female share the same appearance; female only subtly fuller. The female type does not justify a quantitative sex ratio.
- **Fledgling boundary:** exact age unknown; soft/fluffy juvenile contour plus developed but not exaggerated folded flight feathers, both feet supported.
- **Unknown:** exact fledgling age/sex, plumage sequence, nest/clutch, flight onset, living soft-part chronology and species-specific sex-size ratio.
- User-uploaded Mikkola file was unavailable this run; no new page assertion was invented.

## Review gates

1. Exactly three birds and exact labels/order.
2. No ear tufts; dark eyes; plain pale face without dark concentric rings.
3. Cold blackish-sepia/white palette, not warm Fulvous gold.
4. Adults near equal height; no sex colour split or generic large female.
5. Fledgling soft but flight-capable, with supported feet and bounded wing development.
6. Humid Nayarit pine–oak setting; no nest/eggs/prey.
