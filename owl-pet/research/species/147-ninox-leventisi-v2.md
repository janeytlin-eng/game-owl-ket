## Evidence actually read

### CAMBOO-RASMUSSEN-2012-FORKTAIL

- Primary taxonomic paper: Rasmussen et al. 2012, “Vocal divergence and new species in the Philippine Hawk Owl *Ninox philippensis* complex,” *Forktail* 28:1–20.
- URL: https://www.asianscientist.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/08/Vocal-divergence-and-new-species.pdf
- Accessed 2026-11-01; printed pp.3, 15–16 and 19 read, especially diagnosis and type description on pp.15–16.
- Facts: authors combined sexes for measurements because many key specimens were unsexed and explicitly deferred sexual-size analysis. They excluded immatures from diagnoses because data were sparse; generally, immatures are fluffier and less well marked. *N. leventisi* is large with a long deep bill and full lax plumage; short auricular filaments; no pale supercilium; weakly defined warm-brown face; regularly barred crown/nape; boldly barred wing coverts; large white outer scapular areas; extensive bright-white throat; broadly and irregularly barred warm underparts. Irides are gray/whitish to very pale yellow-green; eye-ring mustard; bill/cere mustard with olive base; feet mustard; tarsi thinly bristled; claws mostly pale with dark tips. Holotype and paratype are females; no visual male/female ratio follows.
- Limits: only two specimens plus photographs; no species-specific juvenile sequence, iris chronology, nest or locomotor threshold.

## Design inference and uncertainty

- Adults remain near equal scale; repository must not infer a universal female-larger rule from two female types or unsexed photographs.
- v1's very round heads, compact bill, largely dark claws and extremely regular adult pattern are corrected toward the primary diagnosis.
- Because no species-specific juvenile record was found after searches by current name, former *N. philippensis spilonota* usage, “juvenile/fledgling/nest,” and museum terms, v2 uses an age-unknown older immature. It is fluffier and less marked, eyes closed, with a down-softened compact folded wing and hidden/very short tail. It is branch-supported and makes no exact-age, iris-development, flight or nest claim.
- Scientific verification remains false; result may be `PASS_WITH_LIMITATIONS` only after actual image review.
