### Actual visual verdict

Inspected baseline, A, B and C across front, three-quarter, profile and back. A produces hard bristles / hedgehog halo. B's higher density is still rigid and spiky, especially on side/back. C's tangent-bias shortens the spikes and slightly softens side and back, but the silhouette still reads as a mature Tyto with long folded remiges and elongated rear rather than the requested round down-buried-wing chick. Eyes and heart-mask boundary are still hard, and the eucalypt support still looks like an artificial sculpted cookie/stone plinth. Very high density worsens mobile mesh budget and alpha overdraw; actual mobile GPU tests are absent. Thus **none of A/B/C is art approved or released**.
