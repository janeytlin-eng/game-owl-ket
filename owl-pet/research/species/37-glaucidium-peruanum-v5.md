## Design boundary

- Adults: near-equal height, alike plumage, female only subtly fuller; yellow eyes, false eyes, spotted crown/back, streaked pale underparts, long broken-barred tail.
- Juvenile: age unknown, fully feathered, plain crown, soft short wing and tail, both feet on a broad weathered adobe ledge; the support is documented habitat/perch context, not a nest or burrow claim.
- Habitat: dry western-Andean riparian/thorn scrub with old willow/mesquite and cactus hints.
- Unknown: exact juvenile age, continuous natal-down/feather sequence, iris chronology, flight threshold, degree of independence and population-wide sex-size direction.
