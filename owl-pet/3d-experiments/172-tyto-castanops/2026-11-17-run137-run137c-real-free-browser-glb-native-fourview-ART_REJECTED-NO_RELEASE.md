2. **45° and side:** body is overly rigid/elongated; obvious side wing plates and elongated hard toes. Too much adult-bird structure for a down-buried, compact crouched juvenile. No broad eucalypt hollow lip or natural two-foot support in the native model.

4. **Materials / pet:** binary GLB has **0** materials/images/textures/animations. Neutral clay in Run137C is diagnostic studio lighting only. Coloring it pale would not turn hard angular mesh into natural down or supply rigging, mobile texture/groom assets, or a believable pet. Physical mobile GPU, touch interaction, frame time and real user-agent WebGL remain **untested**, deliberately gated after failed art review.
