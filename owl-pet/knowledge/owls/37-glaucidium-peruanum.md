# 37 Peruvian Pygmy Owl — *Glaucidium peruanum*

## Taxonomy and names
- Current repository name: *Glaucidium peruanum* C. König, 1991.
- English names in current sources include Peruvian Pygmy Owl and Pacific Pygmy Owl.
- Historical treatment: formerly included in the *G. brasilianum* complex.

## Evidence boundary
- Adults: tiny untufted owl, yellow eyes, pale-spotted crown/back, nape false eyes, streaked pale underparts and long broken-barred tail; sexes alike, female only subtly fuller.
- Lüthi 2011 printed p.49 records a probable young third bird and later a juvenile at some distance from an adult pair on adobe walls. It does not provide exact age, plumage sequence or a flight test.
- Greeney et al. 2020 records breeding and a nest in a hollowed-out Pale-legged Hornero nest, but not a known-age growth series.
- Exact juvenile age, iris chronology, first-flight threshold, independence and population sex-size direction remain unknown.
