## Design boundary and factual limits
Adult male left and adult female centre near-equal size because species-specific sex-size evidence was not located. Sex labels are design roles, not visually diagnostic sex identification. No ear tufts; no pygmy-owl white crown spotting or large white scapular row. Grey-brown face, bright yellow adult iris, brown crown/mantle with fine ochre vermiculations; cinnamon-buff shoulder patches; brownish-white underparts with bold bright rufous longitudinal streaks, no horizontal breast barring. Brown/buff barred closed wings and tail; feathered tarsi to yellowish toes. Bill conflict recorded above: dark horn base with a paler olive-horn ridge/tip is an explicitly uncertain reconciliation.
Young: guide-described age-unknown mesoptile, softly fluffy and ventral streaks less distinct; no invented neonatal traits or calendar age. Three-quarter crouching on a broad naturally supported low mossy root, tucked short soft wing area, no spread flight feathers or flight claim. Young iris/bill precise ontogeny unknown; subdued partly closed eyes avoid asserting a developmental iris stage. Nest unknown: no cavity, egg, cup, straw or constructed nest. Habitat Andaman lowland evergreen/secondary forest. Composition support is a design inference, not observed young behaviour.
