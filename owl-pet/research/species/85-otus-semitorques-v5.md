## Sources actually read

### SEM-NOHARA-HONDA-2025-P75-78 — new primary field record

- Kiyoshi Nohara & Rina Honda, “A Reliable Breeding Record of Collared [Japanese] Scops Owl in Saitama Prefecture, Japan”, *Bulletin of the Saitama Museum of Natural History* 19 (2025):75–78. DOI: https://doi.org/10.24715/smnh.19.0_75
- Full four-page PDF read 2026-11-06, especially printed p.76 and fig.3.
- Facts: an adult and one chick/fledgling were observed 25–26 June 2024 in a Cryptomeria plantation with broadleaf/bamboo patches. Adult identity included orange iris, feathered toes, ear tufts and a brownish-white nuchal patch. The young was perched about 9–10 m high on cedar on both days, moved little, was covered over its whole body in down, had clear ear tufts, and had incompletely grown flight and tail feathers; authors inferred it had fledged only recently. Iris colour could not be confirmed. The exact nest was not found.
- Limits/design use: one breeding event and no hatch date or continuous feather atlas. Use a supported **VERY RECENT FLEDGLING** with whole-body down, incomplete wing/tail feathers, clear small tufts and little movement; do not invent exact age, iris colour, flight ability or a cavity.

### SEM-ITIS-1063233-2026 — new taxonomy record

- ITIS TSN 1063233 opened 2026-11-06. https://www.itis.gov/servlet/SingleRpt/SingleRpt?print_version=PRT&search_topic=TSN&search_value=1063233&source=to_print
- Facts: valid current species *Otus semitorques* Temminck & Schlegel, 1845; three direct subspecies. Historical treatment is documented in the cited Deignan 1950 “Races of the Collared Scops Owl, *Otus bakkamoena*”.
- Limits/use: taxonomy/current-name boundary only; no plumage or development inference. Preserve historical mapping *Otus bakkamoena semitorques* as an old treatment, not the current species name.

### SEM-TOKYO-ZOO-2026 — new institution summary

- Tokyo Zoological Park Society, Tama Zoo species page, opened 2026-11-06. https://www.tokyo-zoo.net/en/tama/encyclopedia/japanese-scops-owl/index.html

- Limits/use: institutional overview, not a measured developmental sequence. It corroborates early down but does not justify depicting an unaged hatchling; the primary 2025 fledgling record controls the v5 stage.

- Adults: near-equal visible height/wing length; sex labels are game roles, not evidence of fixed dimorphism.
- Juvenile: very recent fledgling, whole-body down, clear small tufts, incomplete flight/tail feathers, little movement and stable branch support; exact age/sex/iris/flight threshold unknown.
- Setting: Cryptomeria plantation with broadleaf patches; no cavity because the field study did not find the nest.
- Current/old name: *Otus semitorques*; historical *Otus bakkamoena semitorques* treatment.
