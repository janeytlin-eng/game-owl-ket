### PSC-MIKKOLA-2013-P210 — new, uploaded photographic guide

Adult: 23–26 cm, 145–170 g; females average about 20 g heavier; rather prominent tufts; pale gray-brown with fine shaft-streaks/coarse irregular bars; true white scapular shoulder band plus a second pale closed-wing covert band; thin blackish shaft streaks/broken vermiculation below; yellow eyes; greenish bill/cere; feathered tarsi to toe bases. The guide says juvenile undescribed, so it is not used for juvenile stage. Historical/current name both *Megascops cooperi*; no list split/count change. High confidence for printed description/photo; sex direction is average mass only, not display-height formula.
