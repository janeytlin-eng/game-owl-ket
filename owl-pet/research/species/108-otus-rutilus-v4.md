## V3 audit and change decision

The actual v3 PNG was opened. It has the correct three-role layout, readable labels, yellow eyes and humid-forest setting, but the adults read as large long-legged horned owls rather than compact 19–22 cm scops owls: long rigid tufts, oversized facial discs, elongated bodies and extremely long exposed toes/talons. The juvenile is rendered as uniformly curly wool with adult-scale toes, unlike the opened Andasibe photograph’s loose brown down, dark mask, pale brow/crown down and compact gripping feet. V4 therefore requires a whole-bird identity/stage correction rather than another narrow pattern edit.
