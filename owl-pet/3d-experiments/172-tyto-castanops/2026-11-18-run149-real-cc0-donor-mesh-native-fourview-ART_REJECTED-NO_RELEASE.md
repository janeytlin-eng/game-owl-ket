## Independent visual art judgement — ART_REJECTED

I inspected both **actual** four-view Cycles contact sheets. Despite the source model having real organic vertices, its morphology is materially incompatible with the user's desired chick, **before and after vertex-space alteration**:

1. **Face:** two unnatural pointed upward projections/fins on the head; small, rigid eye structures and protruding pale/yellow bill, with no wide, smoothly integrated soft heart-shaped young *Tyto* facial disc.
2. **Wings / lateral anatomy:** strikingly long, stiff adult wing masses jut far behind the body in quarter/strict side views; clearly outlined adult remiges. A compact down-buried chick cannot look like this.
3. **Back / tail:** rigid exposed long feathers and strongly visible pointed rear silhouette; back is not a uniformly plush cream-white juvenile with hidden tail.
4. **Down:** largely smooth, hard/featureless pale surfaces and some speckled outlines — **no true soft natural dense cream down** or credible mobile groom.
5. **Legs / support:** long uncovered legs and exposed claws suspend the body off the ground, no broad supported hollow lip. The separate downloaded stump model does not fix this.
6. **A/B test:** the modified head/body height ratio changes shape slightly, but **does not fix** adult wing topology, head protrusions, back, feet, down, or true young proportions. The A/B variant remains **ART_REJECTED** and may look more distorted.

Therefore two **actual valid native GLBs** and 8 native renders were created/tested, but **zero aesthetically acceptable #172 pet models**. Do not represent the CC0 adult source as rightmost chick.
