# #162 Northern Saw-whet Owl — *Aegolius acadicus*

## Evidence facts
- Very small, compact, round-headed owl without ear-tufts; adult orange-yellow eyes and blackish bill/cere.
- Adults: rusty/gray-brown fine white-streaked head; coffee-brown white-spotted upperparts; short spotted wing/tail; whitish underparts with broad reddish-buff streaks.
- Juvenile: white down initially; coffee-brown downy mesoptile with dark face, pale eyebrow/forehead/lore X or Y and yellowish eyes; juvenile back lacks adult spotting and belly is darker/plain.
- A primary Oregon nest-camera study reported a 30-day nestling period in its successful nest. Cornell's synthesis places nest departure broadly around 28–32 days from its stated behavioral sequence.
- Secondary cavity nester; old woodpecker/flicker holes and snags in mature conifer/mixed forest are supported.
- Female-heavy direction varies seasonally; no fixed displayed size ratio.

## Design decision
Use a “LATE CAVITY NESTLING” with coffee-brown soft mesoptile, pale X/Y on dark face, short rounded developing wing/tail and full cavity support. Do not claim an exact age or flight. Adults stay near-equal in height, female modestly fuller.

## Unknowns
Exact mesoptile day, individual flight threshold, toe exposure, standard cavity geometry, universal sex ratio and regional/subspecies palette.
