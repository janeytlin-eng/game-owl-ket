# 64 Tyto glaucops — Ashy-faced Owl

Hispaniola endemic, species distinct from Tyto alba. Grey heart face/reddish rim, dark eyes, yellow-brown breast, dark brown wings. Female larger per institutional summary, no measured height ratio. Juvenile design uses buff-down nestling with emerging wing feathers from Curti et al 2018 p18; age unknown, no mature remiges. Unusual Palmchat nest cavity treated as recorded locality example, not universal habit. Initial white hatchling down is a different stage.
