Limits: the paper does not give chick age, sex, iris colour, feather-tract chronology, first-flight date or a quantitative adult sex ratio. Its comparisons with other *G. gnoma* populations do not license transferring their chick development to *hoskinsii*.

### HOS-BREWSTER-1888-P136 — original description, primary adult specimen basis
William Brewster. 1888. “On Three Apparently New Subspecies of Mexican Birds.” *The Auk* 5(2):136–139; p.136 read in the scan: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/11/The_Auk_%28IA_auk51888amer%29.pdf

Facts used: original *G. gnoma hoskinsii* was based on three adult males from Sierra de la Laguna; described as smaller and grayer than *californica*, with more white on forehead/facial disc and less distinct upperpart spotting. The male description records ashy/faded-brown crown/back, small indistinct rusty-white spots, white/rusty-white scapular spots, white underparts, reddish-brown throat/breast side areas, coarse longitudinal flank streaks, and imperfect white tail bands not touching the shaft.

Limits: this is a male skin series, not live-colour evidence for eyes/bill, a female description or juvenile development. No female-size rule is inferred.
