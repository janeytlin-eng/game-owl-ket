# #223 Omani Owl — Strix butleri v1
Batch 20261123T113345Z, cycle5. Research read 2026-11-23 (UTC); concept scientific calibration pending.
## Name boundary
Current S.butleri includes S.omanensis Robb et al2013 as junior synonym, demonstrated by DNA in Robb et al2016. Old widespread Hume's Owl S.butleri sensu lato is now largely S.hadorami (#126/#164 already archived); do not count these as #223 or transfer their young. Reused existing DESERTOWL-PUBMED-KIRWAN-2015 index boundary only; no sex measurements from hadorami applied.
Uploaded Mikkola2013 guide is accessible in this project, but its pre-split broad butleri account is unsafe as current-species juvenile evidence; no page for current young is claimed.

### SBUT-ROBB-2016-REDISCOVERY
Magnus S.Robb et al2016, The rediscovery of Strix butleri..., Avian Research7:7, DOI https://doi.org/10.1186/s40657-016-0043-4 . Original https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40657-016-0043-4 . Read abstract, Methods, Results Morphology Oman/Iran, Taxonomy; Fig2 image actually downloaded and viewed (Oman2Mar2015/Iran23Jan2015). Adults: rounded head/no tufts, orange-yellow iris, green-grey bill, grey disc/dark upper brow and median crown stripe, ginger breast/narrow shaft streaks, white belly, feathered white toes, horn-grey claws. Type/DNA synonym robust. One captured bird measurements cannot establish male/female size direction; illumination changes apparent colour. Supports adult morphology and taxonomy, not young.

## Design inference and limits
Near-equal adults neutral scale because sex difference unknown, same adult pattern. Older upright rock-perching young from plate233, slight head turn is conservative pose inference. Preserve formed folded wing because visibly present in reference, alongside obvious pale body down. No hatchling, precise day, wing-flap, flight or nest. Arid rock wadi not rainforest. All6feet visibly supported. Adult posture/relative scale are concept choices; young exact age, full development sequence, sex metrics and nest material remain unknown.
