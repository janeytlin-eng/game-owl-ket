## v5 — 2026-11-12

Preserves v1-v4. Naturalis Schlegel 1862 printed pp.16–17 was actually read for a historical Colombian adult specimen: compact/dark form, numerous rufous tail bars, white throat, irregular pale/rufous marbling, light-rufous lower underparts with narrow dark shaft marks, down-covered tarsi and bare yellowish toes. ITIS and PeruAves were re-read; Sibundoy and the repository-pinned Mikkola page were reused without duplicate IDs. V4 was opened and found somewhat heavy/eagle-owl-like and overly symmetric at the juvenile. V5 corrects the adults toward a compact screech-owl silhouette, retains near-equal scale, and gives the age-unknown older juvenile a slight supported lean. Initial v5 juvenile wing was still long and adult-layered; one targeted edit shortened it into a rounded down-obscured patch. Initial/final plates and all six role crops were opened.
