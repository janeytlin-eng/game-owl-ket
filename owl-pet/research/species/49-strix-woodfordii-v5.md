### AWOO-SANBI-2018 — institutional species account, re-read
- URL: https://www.sanbi.org/animal-of-the-week/african-wood-owl/
- Accessed: 2026-11-05.
- Facts: adults alike; medium owl about 33 cm and c.290 g; round untufted head, dark eyes, yellow bill/cere, pale-rufous facial disc, barred flight feathers/tail and barred russet/brown/off-white underparts. Juveniles fledge in pale-rufous down retained up to five months. Natural tree holes; 30–37 days in nest, about three weeks near parents, flying well around 46 days.
- Limits: southern African synthesis, not a *nigricantior* serial study; “down retained” does not say wings remain wholly downy or fix an individual’s flight ability.
