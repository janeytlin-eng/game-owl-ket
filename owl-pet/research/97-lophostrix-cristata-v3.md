# 97 — *Lophostrix cristata* v3 research and review

- Current name: *Lophostrix cristata*
- Historical mapping retained: *Syrnium griseatum* appears in the MNHN specimen determination history; it is not treated as the current name.
- Action: science revision of the existing species concept; v1 and v2 remain preserved.
- Scientific status: **not verified**

## Read sources and usable facts

- `CRI-SI-2014` — Smithsonian repository landing for Rocha & López-Baucells (2014), accessed 2026-10-29. Repository citation gives pp.35–37 and links the downloadable item.
- `CRI-ROCHA2014-PDF` — actual three-page PDF, printed pp.58–60. Figure 1 on printed p.59 depicts the juvenile after capture and reports it flew on release. This supports an older, flight-capable juvenile stage; exact age, sex and first-flight threshold are not supplied.
- `CRI-MNHN-2010-261` — MNHN specimen record, female from French Guiana, age indeterminate, with historical determination *Syrnium griseatum*. Useful for name history and locality, not juvenile plumage.
- `CRI-ONET-GALE144450` — photographed juvenile in Panama, August 2013; age absent and northern locality limits transferability.
- Existing uploaded Mikkola guide, printed pp.358–359 — reused for white mesoptile, dark face, short tufts and adult-like flight/tail feather direction.

Pagination correction: the repository landing's pp.35–37 conflicts with the actual PDF's printed pp.58–60. Design facts cite the printed PDF pages; the landing range is retained as conflicting metadata.

## Design inference and unknowns

The plate uses an older branch-supported juvenile with white mesoptile and developed folded feathers because the source documents flight on release. It does not depict a hatchling or claim a precise day age. Adult sexes retain near-equal skeletal height; the female is only subtly fuller. Precise juvenile age and sex, first-flight threshold, fixed sex-size ratio and universal nest form remain unknown.

## Review

First render passed visual review at original 1536×1024 and on a 560×720 juvenile crop. Exactly three birds, diagnostic adult face/crest, natural two-foot support and stage-consistent juvenile feathering were present. No edit was warranted. This is a concept image pending scientific calibration, not a scientifically verified plate.
