### DESO-BENDOV-2017 — primary breeding/development study

- Amir Ben Dov, Aviam Atar, Erez Baruchi, Kochav Levi & Nir Sapir (2017), “Breeding biology of Desert Owl in Israel,” *Dutch Birding* 39:285–307.
- Full-text author page: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/320517643_Breeding_Biology_of_Desert_Owl_in_Israel
- Institutional record: https://cris.haifa.ac.il/en/publications/breeding-biology-of-desert-owl-in-israel/
- Actually read pp.285–289, 292–301 and Table 4 on p.299; captions for plates 392, 394–398, 406–421.
- Supported young decisions: the study directly photographed fledglings on rock/nest shelves; first flight was observed at an estimated c30 days, c35-day fledglings were mobile and feeding on a cliff ledge, while fresh breast feathers were noted only around c70–75 days. Use the c35-day stage, perched and fully supported, with abundant residual down plus formed wing feathers. Do not use a nestling younger than the photographed stage.
- Important limitation: the authors explicitly state that development information was limited and their age schedule partly used Tawny Owl as a comparison. Therefore “c35 days” is an estimate, not a known hatch date; exact feather geometry, iris colour and continuous flight competence remain uncertain.
