## Iteration rule

Inspect the full plate plus adult and juvenile/support crops. If the juvenile wing becomes too crisp and adult-perfect, soften only repeated rows while preserving formed length. If one foot is hidden or the stretch becomes takeoff, edit only support/action. Do not erase evidence-supported formed remiges simply because earlier species required shorter wings.
