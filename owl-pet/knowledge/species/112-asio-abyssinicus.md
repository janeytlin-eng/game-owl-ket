- Current/old mapping: *Asio abyssinicus* = original combination *Otus abyssinicus*.
- Keep nominate Ethiopian *A. a. abyssinicus* separate from smaller, greyer *A. a. graueri* when a source is geographically specific.
- Collar & Boesman’s measurements pool sexes; do not convert species means into a fixed female-larger layout. Near-equal display is the evidence-safe default.
- A species-labelled, actually viewed Ethiopia older juvenile supports charcoal/gray-brown loose down, dark face, orange iris, short emerging tufts, concealed tail and supported perch, but not exact age, flight, feet or a feather timetable.
