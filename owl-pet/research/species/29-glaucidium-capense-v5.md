The new directly observed BirdLife sequence supports a more natural stage-specific action: freshly fledged chicks near the nest made only short hops and stretched their flight muscles. V5 therefore keeps the age unknown but shows one supported wing stretch on a low fork, not flight. Adult sexes remain same-plumage and near-equal scale. Exact iris, feather sequence, age, sex, first sustained-flight day and subspecies remain unknown.
