## Actual art gate: **ART_REJECTED**

Visual inspection of front/three-quarter/side/back comparison charts and portrait captures: C marginally improves the excessively square crown; D's fold causes a lateral **swelling**, not a believable short wing buried in second down. Rear and side retain the characteristic smooth featureless sack. Face is still front-photo-projected and anatomically flat at other angles. The cream down, juvenile head/body transition, partially buried feet/toes, and supported eucalypt hollow are not at the requested professional standard.

Measured mean absolute RGB change vs same-camera Run73 R baseline in 512×512 VTK views: **C** front 0.978 / 3/4 1.329 / side 1.265 / back 1.028 (out of 255); **D** front 1.635 / 3/4 1.914 / side 1.683 / back 1.947. These are small true rendered changes; **not** a measure of fidelity to the reference and **not** a passing art score. Neither is publishable.
