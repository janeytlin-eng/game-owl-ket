## Evidence facts

- Mikkola 2013 printed p.284 / uploaded PDF p.286: 39–44 cm, 575–815 g; sexes alike but females average ~200 g heavier. Warm rufous/yellow-brown barred adults, dark eyes, slate bill/cere, bluish-gray toes. Down white. Mesoptile light reddish-brown to dull yellow, narrowly barred throughout, blackish eyebrow zone, weak tufts; wings/tail similar to adult. Dense lowland rainforest, edges, secondary forest and plantations.
- eBird/Cornell current page: current *Ketupa poensis* name; medium rufous-brown owl with long tufts, dark eyes and clean barred underparts in/around humid forest.
- eBird/Macaulay western taxon page: *K. p. poensis* media from Ghana supports the western adult palette and current subspecies boundary.
- National Museums Scotland skin record: physical *Bubo p. poensis* skin from Victoria, 1956, confirming historical-name/specimen mapping.
- Owl Pages breeding synthesis: breeding biology little known; ground and tree-hole evidence conflict; young may remain dependent long after fledging. Not used to invent a nest.
