## Taxon and scope
- Current species: *Otus megalotis* (Walden, 1875); Luzon, Marinduque and Catanduanes.
- Miranda et al. (2011) and the Clements 2012 update split former lowland races *everetti* and *nigrorum* as full species. This board does not borrow their traits.
- Adult panels use one rufous morph so sex comparison is not confounded by colour morph.
