## Fact / inference boundary

Source facts support a brown-morph adult, near-equal adult presentation, and a larger fledged juvenile associated with one natural laurel cavity. Design inference: place the fledged juvenile stationary at a cavity rim with both feet gripping wood; render formed but soft-edged compact wing and tail, loose residual body fluff and no visible ear tufts. Do not claim exact juvenile age, sex, iris colour, first-flight threshold, independence timing, universal cavity dimensions/lining, or population-wide adult sex-size direction.

## V4 inspection and V5 correction

V4 has exactly three supported birds and sensible near-equal adults, but its centered juvenile reads too orderly/adult-hard in folded wing/tail and the habitat is a generic branch. V5 keeps the successful layout while making the juvenile visibly fledged yet softer and irregular, and integrates one natural Yungas laurel cavity as a bounded single-record setting. The juvenile stays perched; no unsupported flight or jump is invented.
