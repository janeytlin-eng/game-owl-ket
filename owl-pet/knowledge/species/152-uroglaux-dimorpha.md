# 152 — Papuan Hawk-Owl (*Uroglaux dimorpha*)

## Naming

- Current accepted name used here: *Uroglaux dimorpha*; ITIS TSN 555493.
- Historical/original combinations retained for lookup: *Athene dimorpha* (ITIS TSN 1063560, invalid original combination) and *Ninox dimorpha*.

## Design boundary used in v4

- Adults: compact/small head, conspicuously long barred tail, yellow irides, streaked pale underparts and barred/mottled brown upperparts. Male and female are near equal because consulted secondary summaries conflict and no primary sexed measurement series was secured.
- Juvenile: source-supported downy stage with white face and white underparts; dark eye, compact down-soft wing, concealed tail and two-foot branch support. Exact age and flight ability are unknown.
- Environment: generic New Guinea lowland/gallery rainforest; no unsupported nest cavity or interior material.
