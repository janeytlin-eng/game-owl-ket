# 62 · Barred Eagle-Owl — *Ketupa sumatrana*

## Taxonomy

Current project name: *Ketupa sumatrana* (Raffles, 1822). Historical combination: *Bubo sumatranus*. Original combination: *Strix sumatrana*. Name endings vary across checklists and are retained in the source index for retrieval.

## Evidence facts

- Large dark-eyed eagle-owl with very long horizontal ear tufts, large yellow bill and finely barred underparts.
- Rainforest, plantations and well-wooded gardens are appropriate regional habitats.
- Species-specific image records describe juvenile plumage as overall white with dark bars; a regional observation documents a branch-perching juvenile.

## Design decisions

- Adults share plumage and near-equal visible height; female only subtly fuller.
- V1 uses a supported dependent later juvenile: whitish with softer dark barring, shorter/softer wings and tail than adults, no flight action.
- No precise age, universal nest substrate, measured sex ratio or subspecies reconstruction is claimed.
