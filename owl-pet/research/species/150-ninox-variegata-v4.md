## Evidence → v4 design boundary

- Adult identity remains earless, compact and short-tailed with strong tarsi, dark rufous/chocolate upperparts, barred scapulars, variable wing/tail marks, dark face without a strong white brow, small pale throat, darker barred upper breast and broader rufous bars on paler lower underparts.
- Adults remain near equal. The two syntypes and a modern New Ireland specimen all have indeterminate/unknown sex; no measured species-specific direction was found.
- V3's closed-eye juvenile pose was a design choice, not an observed developmental fact. V4 uses an open-eyed, low-alert, fully supported age-unknown older juvenile. Iris colour is kept dark/non-diagnostic; the action is a conservative weight shift, not flight or independent dispersal.
- No species-specific nestling/fledgling plumage, exact age, iris chronology, remex/tail timing, flight threshold, nest position or nest material was found. V4 must not call the bird a nestling or fledgling and must not add a cavity, nest, eggs or invented hatchling detail.
- Humid New Ireland forest and one mossy branch are range-compatible presentation, not a nest-site assertion.
