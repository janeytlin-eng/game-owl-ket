### Reused or re-read source facts

- `WAT-DANTAS-2021` — Dantas et al. 2021, DOI 10.11646/zootaxa.4949.3.1, pp.401–444. The article studied 252 specimens and 83 recordings from 65 individuals. Morphometric analyses did not find significant differences among recovered clades, while plumage varied strongly within clades. It supports a disputed taxonomic problem, not a plate-ready sex-size rule or a visually diagnostic Guiana character.
- `WAT-SACC-937-2022` — SACC Proposal 937, especially lines 12–49 and 83–102. The proposed additional species were not accepted; comments emphasize short internal branches, incomplete genomic/geographic sampling and no concrete morphology/vocal diagnosis. The repository may keep its Guiana Shield concept label, but v5 must not present that split as universally settled.
- `WAT-DAFFONSECA-2017` — WA2661782, Manaus, 15 Aug 2017, captioned as one of two young, age/sex undefined. The previously viewed photograph supports a fluffy gray-brown barred older young, pale blue-gray bill, compact mostly concealed formed wing and stable branch grip. It does not establish hatch age, first flight, independence, a nest structure or genetic/vocal assignment.
- `WAT-MIK2013-235` — repository-pinned Mikkola guide PDF p.237 / printed p.235. Adult morphology only: 19–23 cm, relatively long tufts, dusty dark gray-brown upperparts without whitish scapular outer webs, tawny/rusty underparts with fine shafts/crossbars, amber-yellow to pale brownish-orange irides, greenish-gray bill and densely feathered tarsi. The guide explicitly states that the juvenile is not known. Its historic range is broader than the narrow concept and is not transferred uncritically.

No genuinely new stable source was added; four sources were re-read/reused without duplicating IDs. Two boundaries were corrected: the Guiana split remains disputed, and the photographed Manaus young cannot be called a nestling, fledgling or flight-capable individual.
