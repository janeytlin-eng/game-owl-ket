## Design boundary and unknowns

V3 must not duplicate the short-winged 14–18-day v2 crawler. It uses developed but soft folded wings, a short visible tail and firm low grass support; youth remains visible through fluffy crown, mesoptile barring, dark face/ruff and irregular soft wing marks. No sustained flight pose or adult-regular pattern rows. Current taxonomy conflict is recorded, not silently resolved by borrowing named-form geometry. Exact age, first-flight threshold, continuous feather sequence and universal sex ratio remain unknown.
