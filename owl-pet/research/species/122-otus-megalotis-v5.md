## Evidence boundary and design decision

- Adults: gray-brown morph, long tufts, warm subdued orange-brown eyes, ivory/horn bill, pale scapular stripe and feathered tarsi; near-equal crown height with only subtle fullness variation.
- Juvenile: age-unknown dependent later juvenile/fledgling, approximately 75–80% apparent adult standing height as a visual staging choice, not a measured species ratio. Retain rusty fuzzy body, reduced tufts, compact irregular formed remiges and short concealed tail. Allow one slight wing lift/stretch while both feet grip a broad living branch.
- Habitat: Luzon lowland/urban-edge secondary forest family-roost context; no claimed nest.
- Unknown: exact age, sex, healthy flight threshold, iris chronology, continuous feather sequence, bill transition, normal standing-height ratio and universality of branch/cavity use.
