## Evidence actually read this cycle

### `MOSO-NTM-TMAV3451-2026` — National Taiwan Museum collection record

URL: https://collections.culture.tw/ntm_collectionsweb/Collection?GID=MFMQMRMFMNMAMRMLME

The record identifies *O. s. hambroecki*, preserves the original 1870 citation, and gives one sexed female specimen (TMAV3451: wing 119.3 mm, tail 52.7 mm, tarsus 41 mm). Its institutional description says adult sexes are alike, adult iris yellow, bill cream-yellow, tarsi feathered and exposed toes pink-brown; plumage is tea-brown with dark facial-disc rim, pale brow/tuft V, vermiculated back, and barred flight feathers/tail. It describes juveniles only qualitatively as yellow-brown with vague markings that darken and sharpen with age, and notes natural tree hollows and low/mid-elevation primary broadleaf forest edge.

**Boundary:** near-equal adult scale and same plumage; no sex-linked colour or fixed female-larger ratio. Use a yellow-brown, low-contrast age-unknown immature; no exact age, iris chronology, flight readiness or feather-tract schedule.

### `MOSO-NTM-TMAV4405-2026` — second National Taiwan Museum specimen

URL: https://collections.culture.tw/ntm_collectionsweb/Collection?GID=MSMPMEMRMQMAMRMLME

A second Taiwan specimen (TMAV4405) has wing 138.72 mm, tail 80.74 mm and tarsus 36.71 mm, but sex is not listed. It is useful as a warning against converting one specimen into a sex-size rule; the two museum records differ, but do not form a sex-comparable sample.

### `MOSO-NMNS-CATALOG-2026` — National Museum of Natural Science knowledge unit

URL: https://catalog.digitalarchives.tw/item/00/6e/d9/35.html

The institutional catalog states no obvious sex difference, Taiwan broadleaf/mixed-forest habitat at roughly 400–2300 m, and that breeding had not yet been recorded in that knowledge unit. This older summary conflicts with newer museum wording about natural hollows only in evidentiary completeness, not necessarily biology. The hollow is therefore treated as a general institutional summary, not a measured nest case or proof of a specific juvenile stage.

### `MOSO-COMMONS-JUV-2018` — visual comparator, non-Taiwan race

URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mountain_scops_owl_juv.jpg

The page documents a directly photographed juvenile *O. spilocephalus* at Sattal, India on 26 May 2018 (Atoano44, CC BY-SA 4.0). It is a real juvenile visual comparator but not a Taiwan-race developmental study and supplies no exact age or capability. It is not used to claim subspecies-specific colour, iris or flight timing.

## Design inference versus source fact

The age-unknown immature's dark-neutral iris and short down-dominant wing are conservative visualization decisions to avoid asserting adult-bright iris or mature flight feathers. They are **not** source facts about a named age. The natural hollow is background context supported by one institutional description, not a measured universal hollow.
