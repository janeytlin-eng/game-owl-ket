## Sources actually read

### ELE-MOE-JAPAN-2026-REREAD — current government assessment
Japan Ministry of the Environment, Daito Scops Owl Red List/RDB account, https://ikilog.biodic.go.jp/rl_rdb/a/792.html

- Current target name *Otus elegans interpositus*; approximately 20 cm and 90 g.
- About 86 g male and 96 g female means are reported with large individual variation; this supports only subtle female fullness at near-equal skeletal height.
- Fledging occurs June–July and young continue to receive parental food after fledging before dispersal.
- Main habitat is narrow windbreak/shelterbelt forest among fields; most documented nests use introduced Casuarina cavities, with some native fan-palm hollows.
- This supports an age-unknown recent fledgling on a low shelterbelt branch, not an exact plumage day or independent-flight claim.

### ELE-HORIUCHI-ETAL-2026-REREAD — new primary owlet behavior
Horiuchi et al. 2026. “Do Ryukyu Scops Owl owlets on Minami-daito Island recognize kin relationships based on male hoots?” *Ornithological Science* 25(2):147–156. DOI https://doi.org/10.2326/osj.OSJ-2025-033. J-STAGE article and abstract read: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/osj/25/2/25_OSJ-2025-033/_article/-char/en
