## Scope

- Planned stage: adult male, adult female, photographed very white young bird.
- Status: concept v1 generated, reviewed and archived; not scientifically verified.

## Evidence actually read

Mikkola 2013, printed pp.329–330 (uploaded PDF pp.331–332), species 153: 35–40 cm, 375–392 g; female slightly larger; gray and rufous morphs; round head without ear tufts; two pale wing rows, broadly banded wing/tail, heavily streaked pale underparts; feathered tarsi/toes; juvenile referenced to a Hong Kong photo captioned very white.

3. The Owl Pages: current name *S. nivicolum*, formerly treated as a Tawny Owl subspecies; medium, round-headed, no ear tufts.

## Boundaries

- Do not convert “female slightly larger” into a large standing-height difference.
- Use gray morph and generic mountain-forest context; no exact subspecies/locality claim.
