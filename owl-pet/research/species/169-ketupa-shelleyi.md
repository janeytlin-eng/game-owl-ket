# #169 Shelley’s Eagle-Owl — *Ketupa shelleyi* — v1

- Historical name in Mikkola (2013): *Bubo shelleyi*.
- Current name used by eBird/IOC/ITIS: *Ketupa shelleyi*.
- Scope: adult male, adult female, and one age-unknown mesoptile. Scientific verification: **false**.

## Verified evidence

1. **SHEO-MIKKOLA-2013-P288** — Mikkola, *Owls of the World*, printed p.288 / uploaded PDF p.290, actually read 2026-10-27. Adult: 53–61 cm; one male 1,257 g; large dusky tufts; dark barred upperparts; heavily barred yellowish-grey underparts; pale facial disc with fine concentric lines and blackish ruff; dark-brown eyes; creamy-horn bill with bluish base; feathered tarsi. Female mass is unknown and only believed heavier. Downy chick explicitly undescribed. Mesoptile yellowish-grey and brown-barred all over, wings/tail adult-like, eyes dark blue. Primary lowland forest near rivers.
2. **SHEO-EBIRD-2026** — Cornell Lab/eBird, https://ebird.org/species/sheowl1, lines 68–75 actually read 2026-10-27. Current name and living adult identity: very large dark rainforest owl; dark upperparts, heavily barred underparts, dark eyes; lowland rainforest/edge.
3. **SHEO-BIRDLIFE-2022-ATEWA** — BirdLife International, “Lost and found”, 26 Aug 2022, https://www.birdlife.org/news/2022/08/26/lost-and-found/, actually read 2026-10-27. Rob Williams’s 16 Oct 2021 Atewa Forest field photographs confirm a living adult; about twenty museum specimens were then known. This is adult/locality evidence only.
4. **SHEO-ITIS-2025** — ITIS IOC v15.1 record, https://www.itis.gov/servlet/SingleRpt/SingleRpt?categories=All&search_credRating=All&search_kingdom=every&search_span=containing&search_topic=Scientific_Name&search_value=Ketupa+shelleyi&source=html, actually read 2026-10-27. Valid current combination *Ketupa shelleyi*. Taxonomy only.

## Design boundary

The plate uses the adult diagnosis and Mikkola’s age-unknown mesoptile. It deliberately omits the undescribed downy chick, exact age, flight ability, nest form, quantified sexual size dimorphism and universal morph frequency. Near-equal adults prevent converting an unmeasured belief into a fixed ratio.
