- Production target: v1, Taiwan race *O. s. hambroecki*
- Current species name: *Otus spilocephalus* (Blyth, 1846)
- Subspecies mapping: *O. s. hambroecki* (Swinhoe, 1870); protonym *Ephialtes Hambroecki*
- Production stage: VERIFIED archive
- Scientific verification: false

## Evidence boundary

Mikkola printed p.148 / PDF p.150 was read and viewed. The guide supplies the adult pattern, explicit absence of sexual-size data and an evidence-supported juvenile: duller reddish-brown and fluffy, narrow head/crown barring, broader back barring and only faint barring below.

The board uses only the Taiwan race. The National Taiwan Museum collection account says the sexes share plumage; adults have yellow eyes, cream-yellow bill, feathered tarsi and pink-brown toes. Its juvenile is yellow-brown with less distinct markings that darken/sharpen with age; it reports natural tree hollows and low/mid-elevation primary broadleaf-forest edge. Avibase preserves the current race and original name.

## Design decisions

- Near-equal adult scale and same plumage; no automatic larger female.
- Slightly darker/more rufous Taiwan adults; short indistinct tufts and natural support.
- Older fluffy juvenile, smaller/rounder, dull yellow-rufous brown, soft marks; neutral-dark shaded iris because iris chronology is not documented.
- Compact developing wings and short tail; no flight claim.
- Mossy Taiwan broadleaf-forest branch with optional subtle natural hollow; no lining, eggs or hatchling.
