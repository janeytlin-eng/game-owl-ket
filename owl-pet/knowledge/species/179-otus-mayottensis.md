# #179 Mayotte Scops Owl — *Otus mayottensis*

- Concept: [v1](../../design/concepts/179-otus-mayottensis-v1.jpg)
- Current name: *Otus mayottensis*
- Historical combination: *Otus rutilus mayottensis*
- Scientific verification: false
- Latest review: 2026-10-29

## Identity and adult boundary

Small compact scops owl, about 24 cm and c.120 g in Mikkola p.139. Short ear tufts; mottled grey-brown upperparts with dark streaks and vermiculation; mottled nuchal collar; sparse pale scapular areas rather than a broad white brace; barred darker flight feathers, indistinctly barred tail; strongly streaked/barred white throat and vermiculated brownish underparts. Adults have yellow eyes, greyish-horn bill, heavily feathered tarsi and pale toes with dark claws.

Sexes are visually similar. Only slightly longer female wing is supported, so v1 keeps body mass and display height near equal.

## Older immature boundary

MNHN printed p.16 / PDF p.8 records a species-specific immature specimen under *Otus rutilus mayottensis*. A secondary species account says juveniles resemble adults, but breeding information is unknown. V1 therefore selects an age-unknown older immature, not a chick: adult-like grey-brown identity, softer irregular contour, compact rounded formed wing, partly hidden tail, neutral-shadowed visible eyes and complete branch support. No nest, flight or exact age is claimed.

## Unknowns

Exact age, sex, juvenile iris and bill colour, feather-tract sequence, first-flight threshold, nest, clutch, incubation and universal morph proportions remain unknown. Mikkola's “Juvenile Unknown” still blocks any neonatal design. Science false.
