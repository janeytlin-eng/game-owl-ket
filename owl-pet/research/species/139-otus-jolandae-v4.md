## Current and historical names

ITIS TSN 1063240 verifies current valid *Otus jolandae*. Sangster et al. 2013 documents historical assignment of the Lombok series to *Pisorhina albiventris*, *Scops albiventris* and *Otus magicus albiventris*. Those are recorded as historical specimen assignments, not treated as current synonyms.

## Evidence facts

- Sangster et al. 2013 diagnosis and specimen text: adults are dark cinnamon-brown, less boldly patterned than *O. m. albiventris*, with warm cinnamon breast and paler finely barred belly; golden-yellow iris, brown bill and dull-ochre feet on holotype label. One female specimen retained scattered white-to-buffy juvenile crown/nape feathers.
- Mikkola printed p.188 / PDF p.190: about 23 cm, no sexual-size data, long ear tufts, warm-brown upperparts, whitish scapular band, golden-yellow eyes, blackish-brown bill, feathered legs to dull-ochre toes; juvenile undescribed; forest/degraded habitat to 1350 m.
- Related *O. magicus* juvenile text on the following page is not transferred wholesale to this species.

## Unknowns retained

Exact immature age, iris, sex, flight ability, complete feather chronology, nest form and population sexual-size relation are unknown. The older immature is a conservative concept based on a single specimen retaining juvenile feathers. Scientific status remains false.
