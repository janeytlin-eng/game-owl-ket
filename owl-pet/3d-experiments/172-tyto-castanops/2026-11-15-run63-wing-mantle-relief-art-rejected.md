## Executed 3D trials
Re-imported the Run62 corrected M GLB. Authored and executed `build_run63.py` using open-source Python trimesh/numpy/scipy and retained original owner-derived texture:
- **P:** genuinely continuous three-dimensional body surface deformed into an integrated, tapered, down-buried side wing mantle, crown softened.
- **Q:** P plus multi-frequency, spatially correlated 3D surface down relief on crown, back, sides and lower breast, deliberately protecting the existing face geometry; not true feathers.
- Each new GLB is 3,703,872 bytes, 17 meshes, 69,764 triangles; fully exported and independently re-imported.
- An initial audit caught UV-seam displacement cracks after modifying split vertex normals, so we grouped duplicate XYZ and applied shared averaged normals, rebuilt, and verified the welded body watertight, winding-consistent and positive volume (P +0.584835; Q +0.587785).

Eight actual VTK renders (4 views × 2), plus comparison sheets. Sixteen actual headless Chromium/ANGLE/SwiftShader WebGL2 draws (4 angles × portrait 390×600 + desktop 800×800 × 2), 1 embedded texture loaded, 0 gl errors and no page errors. These are desktop software WebGL2 tests, not physical phone hardware, frame-rate, touch, animation or standards-compliant PBR/GLTFLoader validation.

## Art review — both rejected
P's wing-mantle reads as a broad unnatural shoulder bulge rather than short down-buried wing. Q's microrelief looks like faint uneven plaster, still smooth/pear-shaped, not dense fluffy cream mesoptile; side and rear remain inferred, weak and unrealistic. The reference photo is still projected on the frontal mask, the foot anatomy is poor, and the rendering does not meet high-quality electronic pet requirements. No model has been released or presented as satisfactory.
