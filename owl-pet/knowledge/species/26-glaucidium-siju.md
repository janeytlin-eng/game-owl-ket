# 26 · 古巴鸺鹠 Glaucidium siju

研究日期：2026-10-12（Asia/Shanghai）。v1为概念图，科学核验未通过。

## 证据与设计边界

Cornell Birds of the World物种档案的公开索引支持现名、古巴特有分布、三亚种以及成年体长约16–17 cm；成年有灰褐与红褐色型，灰褐型较常见，黄眼、无耳簇，后颈有一对深色假眼斑。Owl Pages的古巴成年实物照片用于交叉核对圆头、褐灰底白斑、横斑尾和林缘栖枝外观。

公开可读资料没有给出足以转绘的连续幼鸟日龄、羽序、尾长或动作能力。本图因此只标“近期离巢、年龄未定”，不声称具体日龄；雌雄分性别线性测量也未获得，成年仅以极轻微体态差区分。

## 生成、检查与迭代

图中恰好三只：两只成年鸟和一只近期离巢幼鸟；三只脚均自然抓枝，背景为古巴湿润林缘与天然树洞。右侧成年鸟三分之四回首，使成对后颈假眼斑完整可见。初稿幼鸟头翼白斑及尾部横斑过于接近成年，已定向编辑为更蓬松、斑纹更弱、尾更短的状态并复查。标题、学名、三鸟数量、无耳簇、黄眼、洞巢环境和支撑均通过目视检查。

仍未知：画面个体所属亚种、精确幼龄与比例、幼羽换羽顺序、分性别测量。详见[来源索引](../../research/SOURCE_INDEX.md)。

## v2 revision — 2026-10-26

Fresh primary research read Garrido (2002), who examined 152 *Glaucidium siju* specimens. For widespread Cuban adults, the table reports mean male wing 91.0 mm, tail 56.2 mm and weight 57.1 g versus female wing 99.3 mm, tail 61.2 mm and weight 64.7 g. v2 therefore makes the female subtly fuller and slightly longer-tailed, while keeping standing height close.

The paper also warns against collapsing geographic variation: Pico Turquino *turquinensis* is darker gray-brown and much less spotted; Isle of Youth *vittatum* is larger and most spotted. v2 deliberately uses a generic gray-brown species direction and does not assign a race. The same paper supports abandoned woodpecker/natural/palm cavities and behavioral display of the nape eyespots.

No primary juvenile series or continuous feather-development evidence was found. The center bird is therefore only an age-unknown older dependent juvenile: smaller/fluffier, unspotted crown, weak upperpart spotting, short rounded folded wings, short weakly barred tail, subdued eye color and two-foot support. These are conservative design bounds, not an exact age diagnosis.

The first v2 render passed visual review without edit: exactly three birds, correct labels, close adult height, female eyespots, natural cavity and six supported feet. ## v3 evidence increment — 2026-10-28

Pyle et al. (2004; printed p.138 / PDF p.3) directly examined/captured Cuban Pygmy-Owls and supplies the juvenile evidence missing from v2: juvenal body plumage is downy, the back unmarked, underparts streaked and crown spots indistinct. The account also supports a reddish-brown female with 95–101 mm wing chord versus a grayish-brown male at 84–94 mm, while warning that some males can be reddish-tinged. v3 therefore uses sex color only within this species evidence and keeps displayed height close; female is fuller, not automatically taller.

The juvenile remains age-unknown because the study's HY window is calendar aging, not a day-specific growth series. Eye color is only a tentative age cue in the paper, and flight/tail chronology are absent. v3 uses natural branch support, no flight action and no exact tail-length claim. Science remains false.


## v4 full-size revision — 2026-10-30

Pyle et al. (2004; printed p.138/PDF p.3), Garrido (2002; printed p.52/PDF p.8) and the adult species/photo account were reopened. The primary evidence still supports a downy juvenile with unmarked back, streaked underparts and indistinct crown spots, while duller juvenile eye colour is tentative and exact age, flight and tail chronology remain unknown.

V4 uses the full 1536×1024 production standard. The age-unknown juvenile is low and fully supported at a broken-palm cavity threshold: downy round body, plain back, faint crown marks, streaked lower front, dull iris, and wing/tail largely concealed rather than assigned invented lengths. The gray-brown male and redder, subtly fuller female remain close in displayed height; the female's false-eye patch is visible. The first render and a 520×650 juvenile crop were opened and passed without edit. `scientifically_verified=false`.
