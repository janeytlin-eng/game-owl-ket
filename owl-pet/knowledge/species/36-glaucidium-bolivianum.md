# 36 Yungas Pygmy-Owl — *Glaucidium bolivianum*

## Taxonomy
- Current working name: *Glaucidium bolivianum* König, 1991.
- Historical treatment: formerly treated as part of the *G. jardinii* complex; current project treatment keeps it as a monotypic species.
- Scope remains provisional until the repository adopts a fixed 251-species checklist version.

## Evidence used
- **SRC-WEB-036-01 — Birds of Bolivia species account / SK Herzog photograph.** Adult field image and Bolivia occurrence; read 2026-10-12. https://birdsofbolivia.org/species-fact-sheets-2/owls/glaucidium-bolivianum/glaucidium-bolivianum-2/
- **SRC-WEB-036-02 — Birds of the World account (Holt et al. 2020), DOI landing/citation.** Adult size and plumage summary, cloud-forest habitat, and explicit statement that breeding biology is almost unknown; read through indexed account summary 2026-10-12. https://doi.org/10.2173/bow.yupowl1.01
- **SRC-WEB-036-03 — Birds of Bolivia adult photograph, S. K. Herzog, 2013.** Adult physical reference; no juvenile-stage evidence. https://birdsofbolivia.org/birds-olivia-photography-gallery/yungas-pygmy-owl-glaucidium-bolivianum-2-s-k-herzog-copy/

## Supported design facts
- About 16 cm; female mass evidence suggests modest size difference, not a universal or exaggerated “female much larger” rule.
- No ear tufts; yellow iris; pale-spotted crown/back/upperwing; pale underparts with longitudinal brown streaking; short pale-banded tail.
- Brown, rufous, and rare gray morphs occur; v1 selects the brown morph.
- Humid montane/cloud forest with dense undergrowth, moss, and epiphytes is supported.
- Breeding biology and juvenile plumage are poorly documented; old woodpecker holes are reported as an inference, not a directly established species-specific nest record.

## Design boundary and uncertainty
The juvenile is an explicitly conservative, age-unspecified concept. No exact day age, feather sequence, or species-specific juvenile markings are asserted. It is shown with softer body feathering, shortened wing/tail proportions, and natural branch support. This is a design inference and **not scientific validation**.
