# 48 · Himalayan Owl · *Strix nivicolum*

## Scope
- Current working name: *Strix nivicolum* (Blyth, 1845).
- Mikkola 2013 uses *Strix nivicola*; current institutional sources use neuter agreement *nivicolum*.
- Planned stage: adult male, adult female, photographed very white young bird.
- Status: RESEARCH_READY; no image yet; not scientifically verified.

## Evidence actually read
1. Mikkola 2013, printed pp.329–330 (uploaded PDF pp.331–332), species 153: 35–40 cm, 375–392 g; female slightly larger; gray and rufous morphs; round head without ear tufts; two pale wing rows, broadly banded wing/tail, heavily streaked pale underparts; feathered tarsi/toes; juvenile referenced to a Hong Kong photo captioned very white.
2. BirdLife current factsheet: accepts *Strix nivicolum* and supports current species-level name and broad range.
3. The Owl Pages: current name *S. nivicolum*, formerly treated as a Tawny Owl subspecies; medium, round-headed, no ear tufts.

## Boundaries
- Do not infer exact juvenile age, wing growth or action ability from “very white”.
- Do not convert “female slightly larger” into a large standing-height difference.
- Use gray morph and generic mountain-forest context; no exact subspecies/locality claim.
