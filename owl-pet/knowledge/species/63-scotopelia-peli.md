# 63 Scotopelia peli — Pel's Fishing Owl

Historical Bubo peli is the same species. Region: African riparian forest; juvenile posture metadata from Botswana. Adults ginger-rufous, no ear tufts, dark eyes, black/grey bill, bare straw lower legs. Sexes similar; female larger/heavier per secondary synthesis, no standing-height ratio inferred. Juvenile whitish with rufous wash, age unknown, natural branch support. Short soft wing shape is conservative design, not measured developmental fact.
