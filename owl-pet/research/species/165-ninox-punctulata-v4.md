## Sources actually read

### SPBO-OCONNELL-ETAL-2022-P291-292 — primary breeding record, reread
Darren P. O’Connell et al. 2022, *Bulletin of the British Ornithologists’ Club* 142(3):278–301, DOI 10.25226/bboc.v142i3.2022.a2; full PDF https://boc-online.org/bulletins/downloads/BBOC1423-PDFa.pdf
- Printed pp.291–292 and Fig.3A reread on 2026-11-12.
- A single juvenile was observed on Buton on 8 August 2013 after locals found it at the base of a large tree in lowland tropical forest near Labundobundo; possibly fallen; it was returned later that day.
- Fig.3A depicts a dark, fluffy juvenile temporarily held for observation. This supports darkness/fluffiness and the record context, but not exact age, flight ability, nest geometry, stable colour calibration or a hand-held final design.
- Design decision: retain an age-unknown dark fluffy juvenile, fully supported on a natural broad root, and exclude humans/rescue handling.

### SPBO-SHARPE-1875-P183 — historical adult female account, reread
R. B. Sharpe, *Catalogue of the Birds in the British Museum*, p.183, https://wallace-online.org/converted/supplementary/specimens/1875_BMbirds_WSPEC351.2.pdf
- Deep chocolate-brown upperparts with minute dull whitish-buff spots; white superciliary circle, cheeks and throat; white centre below, narrow chocolate upper-chest band, brown flanks spotted/barred whitish, and five faint pale tail bars.
- One adult female description does not establish male–female size direction or stable colour dimorphism.
- Direct v3 view found its lower body too uniformly clean white. V4 restores the narrow chest band and patterned brown flanks while keeping the white centre.

### SPBO-MNHN-HOLOTYPE-2010-288 — museum type record, newly indexed
Muséum national d’Histoire naturelle, specimen MNHN-ZO-2010-288, https://science.mnhn.fr/institution/mnhn/collection/zo/item/2010-288
- Current record maps the holotype *Noctua punctulata* Quoy & Gaimard, 1830 to current *Ninox punctulata* and gives Sulawesi provenance.
- Sex and age are explicitly indeterminate. This source supports name/provenance mapping only, not adult sex appearance or juvenile stage.

## Iteration rule

When a historical adult description specifies a central white field plus a narrow chest band and patterned flanks, do not simplify the whole underside to clean white. Preserve the central field and restore bounded flank/chest pattern; verify in adult crops. This rule is applied here but not generalized beyond evidence.
