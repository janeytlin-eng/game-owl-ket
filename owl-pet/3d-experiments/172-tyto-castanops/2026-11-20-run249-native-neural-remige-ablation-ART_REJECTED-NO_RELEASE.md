## Actual visual art inspection — rejected

Rendered the original, A and B with local VTK offscreen renderer from **four genuine spatial cameras each** (front, side, oblique, back); actually opened the 3×4 contact sheet at 1800×1350. The original adult-length drooping remige tip was truncated in both new meshes, a measurable 3D difference. However the trailing wing has turned into an unnatural protruding rounded horizontal flange or shelf, particularly obvious at the back. The side still shows an adult-like embossed folded remige; tall upright silhouette, hard heart-mask/eyes and flat rear lack the dense natural cream second down. Existing broad horizontal Y-up ledge still looks like pale scalloped stone/cookie, not a living old eucalyptus hollow. The shape is **worse than a true infant owl despite technically valid vertex topology**. Neither version is art-approved and neither is a new creative baseline.
