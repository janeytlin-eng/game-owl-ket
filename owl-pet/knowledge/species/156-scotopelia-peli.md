# 156 · Pel's Fishing Owl · *Scotopelia peli*

## Taxonomy
Current scientific name: *Scotopelia peli* (Bonaparte, 1850). U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service and BirdLife South Africa use this name; Owl Pages records the historical combination *Bubo peli*. BirdLife South Africa treats the species as monotypic.

## Evidence read on 2026-10-22
- PFO-BLSA-REDLIST-2025 — BirdLife South Africa, “Pel's Fishing Owl” regional Red Data Book account, 2025 assessment. Identification and Biology & Ecology: 63 cm, 2.14 kg; orange-rufous, round head, dark eyes, no distinct facial disc; sexes visually similar, female slightly larger, male deeper rufous; dark spots/bars/transverse lines; bare tarsi with fish-gripping spicules; juveniles retain most pale white down for the first six months; river systems/backwaters with riparian trees and natural cavities. https://www.birdlife.org.za/red-data-book/red-list/pels-fishing-owl/
- PFO-FWS-TAX-2026 — U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service species profile confirming *Scotopelia peli*. https://www.fws.gov/species/pels-fishing-owl-scotopelia-peli
- PFO-OWLPAGES-2020 — Owl Pages account, updated 2020, cross-checking ginger-rufous barred adults, dark-brown eyes, black/grey bill, bare straw-coloured lower legs/toes, natural cavity nesting, 68–70 day fledging and older-juvenile calls. https://www.owlpages.com/owls/species.php?s=1430

## Design decisions for v1
Adults: no ear tufts; dark eyes; round/tousled head; ginger-rufous plumage with irregular dense dark bars, spots and chevrons; barred flights/tail; black bill with grey base; bare straw lower tarsi/toes. Female only subtly larger and slightly less deeply rufous; no dramatic dimorphism.

Juvenile: conservative supported natural-cavity nestling, mostly pale white down with very short developing rufous feathers and short down-covered wing buds. The source supports prolonged pale down but not exact day-by-day geometry, so no exact age or mature remiges are asserted.

Habitat: mature sub-Saharan riverine forest with quiet permanent water and a large natural tree cavity.
