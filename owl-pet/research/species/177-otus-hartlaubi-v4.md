### STSO-NAUROIS-1975-P323-326-327-329 — new primary life-history and plumage evidence
- **Title/author/year:** *Le Scops de l'Île de Sao Tomé Otus hartlaubi (Giebel)*, René de Naurois, 1975, Bonner zoologische Beiträge 26:319–355.
- **URL:** https://www.zobodat.at/pdf/Bonner-Zoologische-Beitraege_26_0319-0355.pdf
- **Read:** printed pp.323, 326–329; French original and English/German summary were checked.
- **Facts:** one young was taken on the ground on 25 October while having difficulty taking flight; two late-November chicks were still unable to fly, and the younger chick's remiges were only half-grown (pp.323, 326). The nest had not been found; tree-cavity placement was explicitly an author hypothesis, not an observation (p.326). Adults have yellow to slightly orange eyes, yellow cere/yellowish bill with bluish-grey tip, yellow/ochre toes, no true facial disc, absent or vestigial tufts, and mean wing 131 mm in males versus 136 mm in females (p.327). Two observed juvenile plumages already forecast the colour phases: deep rufous/chestnut, still downy with paler belly; or grey-brown/chestnut-grey upperparts with regularly spaced narrow dark-brown bars, paler underparts with fine ochre bars and white inner tertial edging (pp.328–329).
- **Limits:** only two juvenile plumage observations; exact age, eye/bill chronology and sex are unstated. Specimens arrived decomposed and some sex determinations were impossible. The 131/136 mm means support only subtle folded-wing direction, not a body-height rule. The paper's tree-cavity discussion is inference and is not rendered.
