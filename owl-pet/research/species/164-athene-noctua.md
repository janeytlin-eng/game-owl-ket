# #164 Little Owl — *Athene noctua*

## Evidence facts
- Nominate/western European design: small, flat-headed, tuftless; gray/dark brown with whitish crown streaks and upperpart spots, pale boldly streaked underparts, yellow eyes, gray-green bill, olive-gray cere, pale brows and long pale-feathered tarsi.
- Mesoptile is adult-like but fluffier, more diffuse and less distinctly spotted; specialist guidance describes brown/dirty down, pale eyes and slightly feathered toes.
- BTO states chicks may leave the cavity at c.25–28 days, roughly a week before 30–35-day fledging. A 27-day chick can therefore stand at the threshold but must not be depicted flying or independent.
- Females average heavier, but BTO notes strong biometric overlap; adults should remain near-equal in displayed height.
- Western European farmland, mature orchard/hedgerow trees and farm-building cavities are supported.

## Design decision
Use an old orchard/farm stone-wall cavity. Center a **27-DAY PRE-FLEDGING CHICK** at the cavity threshold, fully supported on stone/wood, mostly downy with short soft developing wings, diffuse markings and no flight. Adults flank the cavity, near-equal in height with the female only subtly fuller.

## Unknowns
Exact individual day-27 plumage, departure/flight timing, cavity geometry, universal sex ratio, and palette beyond nominate/western Europe.
