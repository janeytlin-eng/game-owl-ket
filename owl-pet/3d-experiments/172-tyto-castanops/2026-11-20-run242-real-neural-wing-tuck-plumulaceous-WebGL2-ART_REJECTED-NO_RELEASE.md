## Independent artistic judgment: REJECTED

The front is now a recognizable light-coloured learned owl with a developed heart-like disc and two visible eyes. But both versions remain **far below** the target high-quality downy mesoptile. The new atlas introduces some real halo fuzz near the crown and outlines, but at actual browser framing it still resembles sparse prickly hair, not a dense, soft layered cream nestling coat. A's wing is still a long carved adult-style relief; B contracts it mostly sideways, narrows the front body considerably and **does not** erase the elongated descending wing/tail side silhouette. Rear remains a bland continuous white mound, eyes look rigid/ring-like, feather transition around the facial disc is not natural, and the feet remain floating without a broad photorealistic eucalypt hollow ledge. B is **not** a design improvement worth promoting. A may be a technical control, not a finished character.

These are not failures of the GLB container; they are unresolved high-fidelity biological/artist sculpting and grooming shortcomings. Neither version met the right-hand original concept's anatomical/art quality gate, let alone physical phone GPU performance or interaction acceptance.

Next defensible route is an artist-directed local true-3D resculpt/retopology of the **juvenile folded-wing anatomy** and rear/cheek volume, plus several layers of short, fine mesoptile that actually cover the silhouette in true multi-angle views; merely stronger lateral shrinkage or another 4-cell line atlas is not sufficient. A genuine scanned/sourced broad hollow eucalypt edge, with verified license, must also be integrated and optimized before an eventual mobile/WebGL benchmark. A future study may explore [Poly Haven's CC0 models](https://polyhaven.com/license), but **no trunk asset was downloaded or integrated during Run242**.
