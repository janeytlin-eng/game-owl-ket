## Evidence boundary

Loyn & Debus (2000), printed pp. 288–289 and Plate 79, describes one rescued Bougainville juvenile under the historical name *Nesasio solomonensis*: long unbarred down on head and underparts, dark facial mask, orange-yellow irides, blue-grey cere, black bill, grey feet, and emerging barred light/dark-brown feathers on the dorsum and wings. It was lively, walked/bounced and perched on railings. Its exact age, sex and flight ability were not reported. The hollow lowland-forest tree nest statement is secondhand in that paper. ITIS maps current *Asio solomonensis* to historical *Nesasio* and *Pseudoptynx* names. The user-uploaded Mikkola guide was not accessible in this execution.
