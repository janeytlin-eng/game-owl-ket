## Evidence-to-design boundary
- Female is clearly fuller/heavier-looking but only modestly longer-winged/taller; linear measurements overlap. This is an evidence-supported exception to any blanket “near-equal” rule, not a universal owl rule.
- Adults share one brown morph and must show fully bare tarsi and natural long toes.
- Recent fledgling is explicitly c.8–12 weeks, fluffy/barred and a clumsy flier. It remains supported and crouched; no mature adult wing, no flight action.
- A narrow trunk fissure may appear as background context only. No lining, egg, nest-interior pose or cavity geometry beyond a restrained natural split.
- Iris choice is non-universal; exact individual sex, first-flight timing and feather-by-feather development remain unknown.
