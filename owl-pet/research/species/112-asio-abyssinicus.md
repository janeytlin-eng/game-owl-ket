# 112 — Abyssinian Owl (*Asio abyssinicus*)

- Current name: *Asio abyssinicus*
- Historical/comparison name: sometimes treated as African Long-eared Owl or under *Asio otus* in older treatments; current project retains species status following the active checklist and adult evidence in ABO-COLLAR-BOESMAN-2019.
- Production target: v2 revision; preserve v1.
- Scientific verification: false.

## Evidence used for v2

- **ABO-COLLAR-BOESMAN-2019** — full taxonomic paper actually read; adult museum morphometrics, plumage distinctions and species status. No juvenile or sex-height formula.
- **ABO-CARLBERG-2016** — first-hand Bale Mountains report, pp44–45: adult with two downy chicks on an old twig platform at a tree trunk; photo shows only white down beneath the parent. Exact chick age and exposed anatomy are not documented.
- **ABO-OWLPAGES-2020** — specialist secondary adult identification, qualitative female-size direction, tree/giant-heath setting and old stick nests. Comparative A. otus breeding notes are not transferred.
- **ABO-OGADA-MURIITHI-2017** — primary field report documenting uncertainty around Mt Kenya occurrence/heath dependency; it does not establish a juvenile sequence.

## Design boundary

Show two adults with only subtle sex-size direction. Use a Bale Mountains tree-platform scene as a documented observation context, not a universal habitat claim. Depict the juvenile conservatively as a mostly concealed mound of white natal down; do not invent eyes, bill, feet, wing or tail morphology and do not borrow *A. otus* development.
