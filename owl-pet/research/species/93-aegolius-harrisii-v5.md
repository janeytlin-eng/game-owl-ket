## Current and historical names

Repository target remains southeastern *Aegolius harrisii iheringi* (Sharpe, 1899), a recognized child taxon of valid species *A. harrisii*. Historical mappings retained: protonym *Gisella iheringi* and species-level synonym *Nyctale harrisii*. The subspecies is not counted as another species.

## Evidence boundary and design decision

Adults are near-equal visible height. The 2012 paper’s “possibly male by smaller size” is one field inference and does not justify a universal visible ratio. The juvenile uses the documented approximately 24-day stage: recognizable facial disc and substantial juvenile feathering, but residual dark-grey down, shorter/rounder/softer wing, concealed short tail, low cavity-floor crouch and no flight. The cavity is a schematic cutaway; the real entrance was not adult-body-wide.

## Unknowns retained

Exact nestling sex and within-day age, tract-by-tract feather chronology, quantitative sex-size ratio, individual flight competence at 24 days, iris chronology, population transfer and exact cutaway geometry remain unknown. Result can pass as an evidence-bounded concept, not scientific approval.
