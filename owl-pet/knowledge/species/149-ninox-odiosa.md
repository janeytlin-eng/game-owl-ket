## Evidence boundary

eBird supports a small earless dark chocolate-brown owl with orange-yellow eyes, white eyebrows and throat, broad dark chest band, pale mottled lower underparts and scattered white upperpart/wing marks. BirdLife supports New Britain endemicity and forest/modified habitat use. Avibase supports current and historical naming.

No reliable species-specific juvenile growth sequence or visual sex dimorphism was found. The v1 adults are therefore near equal scale. The juvenile is an explicitly age-unknown older concept with reduced diffuse pattern, compact rounded wing/tail and two-foot branch support; no exact nest stage is claimed.

## Review

Initial generation had a juvenile with adult-like layered wing and crisp spot rows. One targeted edit shortened and softened only the juvenile wing/tail and markings. Final review retained exactly three supported birds, readable labels, orange-yellow eyes, white brow/throat, broad chest band and rainforest context. Exact juvenile sequence, sex ratio and fine feather geometry remain unknown.
