## Evidence boundary
- Adult sex difference is primarily documented colour: male greyer/olive-brown, female more cinnamon/rufous. No source read this cycle justifies a dramatic standing-height gap.
- Original prepared immature material supports a plainer dusky-grey crown/nape and weaker adult head pattern. It does not establish exact age, living iris/bill, continuous feather chronology, nest position or flight ability.
- V3 therefore uses male–female–immature order, near-equal adult height, a compact fully supported age-unknown older immature, no nest interior and no locomotor claim.
