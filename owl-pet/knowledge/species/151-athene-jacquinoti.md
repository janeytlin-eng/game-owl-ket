## Evidence boundary

eBird supports the current combination, small size, orange eyes, lightly spotted brown upperparts, paler underparts, western Solomon distribution and well-developed forest habitat. OwlPages independently supports the earless form and historical common/scientific names. World Bird Names is retained only for the historical name trail and original-combination metadata.

No reliable species-specific juvenile development sequence, quantified sex-size direction or precise nest-site evidence was found. Adults are shown near equal scale. The juvenile is explicitly an age-unknown older juvenile inference, not a reconstructed nestling stage.

## Review and iteration

The first generated juvenile had long layered adult-like remiges. A targeted edit shortened and rounded the central wing, softened its surface, shortened the tail, and retained two-foot branch support. Both adults, orange eyes, title, labels and forest scene remained intact. Exact juvenile feather geometry and all sex ratios remain unresolved; science status stays false.
