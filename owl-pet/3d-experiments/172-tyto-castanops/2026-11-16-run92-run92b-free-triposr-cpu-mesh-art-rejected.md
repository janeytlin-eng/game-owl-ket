## Actual visual rejection, not mistaken binary success

Opened **actual Run92B Blender front/strict side/back and actual Three.js initial desktop pixels**. The reconstructed chick is grossly tipped over/rotated with the facial features on the side; projected eyes/face are mangled/incompletely reconstructed, a mass of large lumpy or bubble-like down texture and thick tube-like protrusions stand in for wings/feet; back and sides are incomplete amorphous prior shapes, not credible Tyto anatomy. Taubin 12× removes little vertex noise and does **not** fix these failures. Some source eye texture was degraded by automatic background removal; this route relies on **single frontal input** and cannot validate back/side anatomy even with a successful watertight mesh. **No native realistic soft cream feathers, correct heart face, realistic juvenile anatomy and support, rig, blinking, dynamic fluff or validated phone WebGL performance**. Thus this is not even a visually reasonable electronic pet preview; **ART_REJECTED** despite true glTF 2.0, shading, Blender and browser successes. The old QA front-orientation eye-node heuristic from the prior hand-sculpt is not applicable to TripoSR; did not claim its warning is a proper new anatomy test.
