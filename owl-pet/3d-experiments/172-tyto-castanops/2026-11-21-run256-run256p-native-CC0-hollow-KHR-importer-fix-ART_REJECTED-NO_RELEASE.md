**Crucial real importer defect surfaced**: initial Trimesh 4.11.1 read pack `KHR_mesh_quantization` **normalized** signed INT16 `POSITION` and unsigned UINT16 `TEXCOORD_0` as raw coordinate values (roughly -32767…32767, 0…65535), while baking node hierarchy with small metric node scales. This grossly distorted CC0 mesh proportions, part placements, material UVs; the first original importer pass made the adult owl look anatomically exploded and the hollow like a flat polygonal orange trunk. Such images are **invalid** for source-art evaluation, even though they came from real GLBs and passed simplistic technical load checks. Reject Run256's external-source geometry, preserve it only as a reproducible importer negative-control. The actual CC0 raw binary formats were opened to confirm accessor `componentType`=5122/5123, `normalized: true`, extension requirement and per-node matrices.

[Run256 original evidence artifact](https://github.com/janeytlin-eng/game-owl-ket/actions/runs/44841460740/artifacts/14681471466) was actually downloaded and independently inspected: 10,681,487-byte ZIP, SHA256 `958dcd3fd9514a345d3aa99171505bfbe273d5c537b5dc58b4346a2b6eb495c5` matching GitHub, **36/36 entries CRC verified**, three real GLBs re-imported with expected topology, 21 browser screenshot hashes crosschecked. Visual comparisons were actually opened. No release.
