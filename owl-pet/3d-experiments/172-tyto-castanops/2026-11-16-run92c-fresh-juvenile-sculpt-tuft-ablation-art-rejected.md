## Visual evidence: categorical rejection after actual PNG review

The three-row by four-view Cycles contact sheet was **actually opened**, alongside the six real browser frames (not merely reading metadata). Replacing the stripe-layer old torso genuinely removes some conspicuous rubbery ridges, but the new model is **an almost featureless upright white egg / seed**. It lacks readable head/neck/shoulder segmentation and has no plausible wing plane in strict profile or rear. The tan heart is a shallow, graphic sticker shape with bead eyes; the tiny feet barely protrude, and the hollow-lip prop reads like a broken cylindrical pipe rather than a broad supported nest edge. Higher-density short-tufts make a scattered stipple/white-grain halo, **not natural soft cream down**. Side and back still cannot plausibly represent young *Tyto*. This aesthetic regression means neither new GLB is useful for a public pet sample even though the topological and rendering controls pass.

The TripoSR Run92/92B path (true CPU neural reconstruction) was also already **ART_REJECTED** for gross geometry / orientation / single-image back-side hallucination; this independent sculpt experiment should not be described as a cure. It establishes a more explicit negative result: a smooth connected 3D volume plus correct matte PBR and 4,500 soft alpha cards can remain **less** biologically convincing than an older visibly striped model. Stop iterating by changing SDF ellipsoid placement, material colour or hair count alone. The next meaningful art route requires anatomically authored form, a credible feather tract and real multiview references or specialist-quality source sculpt, followed by physical phone GPU performance.
