## Evidence summary

Mikkola printed p.136/PDF p.138 was read and viewed. Adults are very small with very small tufts; females are a little larger/heavier. The grey-brown morph is dark and comparatively uniform, with large pale scapular areas, deep yellow eyes, yellow bill/cere and the distal quarter of the tarsus unfeathered. The downy chick is explicitly undescribed. The only authorized juvenile is the paler, fully finely barred mesoptile.

The BirdLife/ICBP red-data account pp.326–327 records October fledglings plus secondary-forest occurrence, but gives no young-bird plumage or age. A 2021 living-adult photograph from Mé-Zóchi was viewed through GabonBiota/iNaturalist and used only for adult palette, low tufts and irregular markings.

## Design boundary

- Same grey-brown morph adults; female only modestly fuller/larger.
- Supported paler finely barred mesoptile, neutral-dark shaded iris; no hatchling, nest or flight claim.
- Exact juvenile age, iris chronology, remex/tail sequence, flight threshold, nest geometry and sex-specific plumage remain unknown.
- Habitat may combine humid primary/secondary forest and restrained clove/mango foliage.
