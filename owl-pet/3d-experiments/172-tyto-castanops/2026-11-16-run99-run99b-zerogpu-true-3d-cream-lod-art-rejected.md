## Run99 — actually produced real neural 3D

Created isolated research branch `research/owl172-run99-free-hunyuan-neural-geometry-probe`, reproducible source `tools/run99_free_hunyuan_neural_probe.py`, and dedicated workflow `.github/workflows/owl-pet-172-run99-free-hunyuan-neural-probe.yml`. Used the public Hugging Face [Tencent Hunyuan3D-2 Space](https://huggingface.co/spaces/tencent/Hunyuan3D-2) through **anonymous Gradio + public ZeroGPU**, no login, token, paid fallback, credits purchase or subscription.

The actual rightmost young owl crop from the 1536×1024 archived three-owl image was `[945,179,1525,983]`, 580×804 pixels. Opened the cropped reference and checked it: actual desired cream plush bird, soft heart mask, tucked body, short wing and both feet supported on aged tree hollow.

The public neural `/shape_generation` call genuinely produced binary glTF GLB (magic and embedded length checked) in 9.26s service-call time. Output `run99_neural_source_ART_UNAPPROVED.glb` is **18,333,652 bytes**, SHA256 **`eb95158d8a40fc869caef310a70af7e42f5731061725e2a1754aded0e7a8d683`**. Blender 4.0.2 actually imported it as **1 mesh / 348,101 vertices / 696,834 triangle faces measured by Blender**, rendered four independent Cycles views (front, 3/4, strict side, rear), and exported a separate validated **58,519,516-byte** native Blender roundtrip GLB. The Gradio metadata separately said `number_of_faces=1,179,598`; do **not** substitute that provider statistic for independently reimported triangle counts without explaining the discrepancy. The new Blender dimensions were `[1.5553, 1.9562, 1.9120]` XYZ.

The first CI workflow run [43787156074](https://github.com/janeytlin-eng/game-owl-ket/actions/runs/43787156074) generated the GLB but failed at Blender argument parsing and at a SHA256 race with an actively written tee log. Both problems were fixed in the script. The second CI [43787343999](https://github.com/janeytlin-eng/game-owl-ket/actions/runs/43787343999) succeeded including original GLB, Blender reimport, four original Cycles PNGs, contact and signed-by-hash evidence in `research-evidence-run99-HUNYUAN-ZEROGPU-ART_UNAPPROVED/`; all archived hash checks passed. The evidence was re-read on the remote GitHub research branch and the contact JPEG was actually decoded and visually inspected from raw Actions logs, not inferred from metadata.

**Actual art review:** The gray untextured neural mesh is a meaningful geometry improvement over the older white eggs: naturally crouched juvenile outline, real heart-shaped cranial relief and plausible feathered head/torso are visible, with consistent three-dimensional side and back. It is **not** an approved pet: entire model is gray clay, not softly layered cream down; feathers are etched or rough shell relief, not groomed soft filaments; face/eyes remain small/ambiguous, side silhouette is too long and hunched, legs/toes too exposed, concealed short wings lack reliable tract anatomy; it lacks a broad supporting eucalyptus hollow. Side/back are hallucinated from one front reference, not independently anatomically verified. Rig, behavior and actual mobile GPU performance were not tested.

## Run99B — actual cream-colored lower-poly GLBs from neural source, not SVG

Implemented `tools/run99b_free_neural_cream_lod.py` and an isolated one-off free Blender workflow `.github/workflows/owl-pet-172-run99b-cream-pbr-lod-qa.yml`. Actually imported the **same Run99 source GLB**, performed genuine mesh decimation to two different face budgets, gave each real 3D mesh a cream, rough, nonmetallic PBR material, exported new GLBs, **reimported each** and compared surviving faces, then rendered eight independent Cycles views and checked SHA256 manifests.

- **72k-face LOD**: original Blender preexport **72,000** triangles / 35,684 vertices; roundtrip **71,989** triangles (11 degenerate/invalid omitted during glTF export); **6,048,344 bytes**, SHA256 `a8332d7c4da5863e055afa0de6cb001dae3956e590e3075bf41a07f0ee903718`.
- **32k-face LOD**: Blender preexport **32,000** triangles / 15,684 vertices; roundtrip **31,982** triangles (18 omitted); **2,687,828 bytes**, SHA256 `b0f9a25185365da23891598030fed9c37c7bf9f85511ee3a77082cf562461460`.
- First LOD job [43788050920](https://github.com/janeytlin-eng/game-owl-ket/actions/runs/43788050920) stopped correctly because an overstrict invariant rejected 11 lost invalid triangles, despite a real GLB being exported. Fixed only the test acceptance to tolerate <=0.1% face omission and explicitly record the discrepancy; no geometry or art success was inferred. Successful job [43788263049](https://github.com/janeytlin-eng/game-owl-ket/actions/runs/43788263049) completed generation, physical Blender reimports, **8 native rendered PNGs**, two four-view contacts, integrity checks and permanent evidence push to `research-evidence-run99B-CREAM-PBR-LOD-ART_UNAPPROVED/`. Both GLBs remain unapproved and private to the research branch, not deployed.

**Direct visual comparison of saved native 2×2 contacts:** The 72k warm-neutral variant shows severe black mottled crater/shredded-plumage appearance across face/breast, and facial relief is now harder to read than high-poly gray source. The 32k version is worse: heart rim and recessed eyes become indistinct amid faceted/flake-like noise, side and back coarse/crumbly. They are real 3D, but **not** smooth fluffy cream chicks. The unchanged original image-to-mesh face structure is not enough; straightforward decimation sacrifices high-frequency plumage geometry, and a uniform cream PBR color does not create the required soft feathers. These LODs need proper sculpted/groomed base, normal/height/albedo bake and topology cleanup. No actual-phone GPU or WebGL2 draw/FPS, animation, touch, memory/thermals were exercised. Ignore the CI success as a pet acceptance signal.
