## Fact versus design
Fact: adult feathered tarsi/bare cream digits/pale horn claws in actual guide, bare digits visible on printed photograph.
Observed v4: feathered proximal toe surfaces and very dark claws. Bounded edit: only adult digits/claw palette, no change to tarsal feathers, toe count, grip, juvenile feet, iris or bill. Adult proportions stay neutral, not evidence of equal measured sexes. Juvenile yellow-cream stage remains exact age unknown, supported roost/no flight/no nest.
Unknown: open-eye adult colour conflict, bill conflict, juvenile continuous chronology/iris trajectory, first flight, exact sex-size, nesting site. Prompt shortening/foot palette are design decisions, not species-wide new measurements.
