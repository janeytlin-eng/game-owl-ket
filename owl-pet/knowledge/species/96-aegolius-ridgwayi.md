# 96 · Unspotted Saw-whet Owl · *Aegolius ridgwayi*

## Existing v1 evidence
RID-EA-JUV (Eisermann & Avendaño natural-history page and attributed juvenile image), RID-OP2020 (Owl Pages adult/habitat synthesis), and RID-MIK2013-446 (Mikkola printed p.446/PDF p.448) support a plain warm-brown, narrow pale-rimmed, honey/brown-eyed adult and an age-unknown older fluffy juvenile. Exact juvenile age, wing geometry, flight ability and sexed morphometry were not established. [v1 original](../../design/concepts/96-aegolius-ridgwayi-v1.png); scientifically_verified=false.

## 2026-10-27 v2 research-ready
RID-EISERMANN-AVENDANO-2018 was newly added after actually reading the full article landing/indexed species account: Guatemalan highlands 1,900–3,700 m (rarely 1,400 m) and first national breeding records in 2008–15. It validates the highland-forest context and breeding presence, but not a universal cavity, exact juvenile age or feather sequence. Reused RID-EA-JUV, RID-OP2020 and RID-MIK2013-446.
