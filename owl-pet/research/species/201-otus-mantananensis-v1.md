# 201 · Mantanani Scops Owl · *Otus mantananensis* v1

## Taxon and scope
- Current species: *Otus mantananensis* (Sharpe, 1892), small-island populations of the western Philippines and northern Borneo.
- The juvenile evidence is explicitly *O. m. romblonis* from Semirara Island. The board uses that Philippine juvenile as the developmental boundary and does not claim it represents every population.
- Historical crosswalk: Avibase records protonym *Otus romblonis* and Peters' older *Otus scops romblonis*; current treatment is *O. mantananensis romblonis*.
- No reliable sex-size direction was found; adults remain near-equal. Scientific status remains false.
