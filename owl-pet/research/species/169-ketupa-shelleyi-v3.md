## Design boundary

The v3 image uses a dark adult morph grounded in the 2021 primary photograph and specimen comparison. Adult eyelid margins remain dark, while the conflicting pale-eyelid field note from Cameroon is retained as possible variation rather than universalized. The center bird remains an age-unknown mesoptile with formed but softer barred wings and short tail; “recently fledged” dates cannot be fused into exact age or flight ability. No nest is drawn because no nest has been found. Adults remain near-equal in skeletal size; only subtle female fullness is allowed.
