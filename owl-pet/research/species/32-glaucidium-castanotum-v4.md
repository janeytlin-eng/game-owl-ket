## Evidence and design boundary

**Source facts.** CBO has a much redder mantle and fewer chest bars than Jungle Owlet in the 2025 captured-bird sample. The dated Sinharaja photograph and 2014 family observation support a fully feathered older juvenile on natural support; the latter remains parent-associated. Institutional adult photo records support wet-zone localities and real adult variation. Peregrine Fund explicitly states that nestling duration and post-fledging dependence remain little known.

**Design inference.** V4 is a full-resolution re-render of the supported older-juvenile concept. Adults remain near equal in displayed height because the research does not provide sex-stratified size data. The juvenile is slightly smaller, softer, with muted chestnut mantle, diffuse markings, compact low-contrast wing and short soft tail, fully supported on a branch. No exact age, independence or flight claim.
