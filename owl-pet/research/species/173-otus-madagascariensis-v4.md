## Reused / re-read evidence

- **TSO-RENE-THORSTROM-2009-P87-88:** full paper re-read. Typical natural tree-hole nests averaged 5.8 ± 2.2 m (n=7); the exceptional ground clutch had no nesting material and hatching was not observed. Use dry-west context only; do not depict a ground nest or derive a chick stage.
- **TSO-FUCHS-ETAL-2007-P408-418:** western birds trend greyer/yellow-brown with shorter tufts, longer tails and smaller claws than eastern birds; paper challenges species separation but supplies no sex/age sequence.
- **TSO-ITIS-686657-2025:** valid species record and historical synonym; nomenclature only.
- **TSO-MIKKOLA-2013-P145:** repository transcription only; short-tufted grey morph, moderate female fullness direction and an age-unknown older immature with stronger-barred uppertail coverts/narrower pointed rectrix tips. Exact age/flight unknown.

## Problem → evidence → v4 change

- **Problem:** v3 needed an edit because the model leaked tall tufts and adult-hard wing/tail layering into the older immature.
- **Evidence:** western form evidence supports very short low tufts; the immature cue is limited to local tail/coverts characters and does not establish adult-complete remiges or flight.
- **Change:** put tuft height and immature wing softness into whole-image constraints and separate crop gates from the first generation. The older immature takes a low stable forward-peering crouch with both feet on one broad branch; this varies posture without claiming flight or nest departure.
- **Taxonomy boundary:** the new 2022 molecular paper strengthens the split/lump conflict; retain the project name but do not imply the conflict is scientifically resolved.
