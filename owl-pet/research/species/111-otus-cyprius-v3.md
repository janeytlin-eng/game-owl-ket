## Evidence → design boundary

- Keep a formed barred wing/tail on the selected fledgling: direct 2013 post-fledging field evidence supports it.
- Soften/shorten the juvenile folded wing and make the belly diffuse, because exact age and adult-like shaft geometry are unsupported.
- Adults stay near-equal in height; broad-guide female-mass direction allows only subtle fullness.
- Preserve two legitimate kinds of pale marks: scapular/outer-covert spots and flight/tail bars. Do not erase these while removing mechanical repetition.
- Use an olive-grove branch after fledging, not a reconstructed nest.
- Exact fledgling age, fine bar count/spacing, sex-specific standing dimensions and flight capacity remain unknown. `scientifically_verified=false`.
