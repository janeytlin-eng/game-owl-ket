Run87B is an anatomy and texture-morph feasibility experiment on an
existing, adult great-horned owl art model. It is not anatomically
verified Tyto castanops or a high-quality age-unknown mesoptile.
The adult source is licensed as CC0 on its host but its provenance
must be verified. No release, claim of infant accuracy, or deploy.
Human review of real 4-sided render, native PBR glTF parity,
moving avatar and real mobile GPU have NOT passed.
