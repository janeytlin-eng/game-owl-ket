## Design decision and limits

- **Problem in v3:** image is anatomically supported and the edited juvenile wing is bounded, but the juvenile remains a nearly static cavity portrait.
- **Evidence-led change:** move the juvenile to a separate branch and show modest open-bill food begging, using the documented older mobile *O. l. cnephaeus* stage. Do not show prey or flight.
- **Adults:** near-equal crown height and overlapping size; no forced female-larger direction. Dark eyes, compact scops proportions, warm cryptic pattern and feathered tarsi retained.
- **Juvenile:** older and mobile, age unknown; weak/indistinct tufts, soft gray-brown cover, compact formed folded wing, short tail, both feet gripping a branch.
- **Environment:** humid lowland secondary woodland edge. No nest or lining is shown, avoiding an unsupported transfer between subspecies and life stages.
- **Unknown:** exact age, sex, individual flight ability, nominate-stage chronology and exact subspecific colour remain unknown. Scientific verification remains false.
