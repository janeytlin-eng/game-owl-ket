## Four-angle original Cycles image inspection — REJECT

I inspected each original remotely archived Blender image and compared with the actual owner rightmost v3 design and Run85 original renders. The new C1 radius profile does remove the conspicuous horizontal snowman neck/waist seam from Run85 and creates a somewhat continuous head-breast shape. But the replacement is still **a broad wedge/pear-like abstract body**, lacking credible organic young-owl skeletal/wing proportions, especially in strict profile and rear. The heart facial disc has a hard triangular wedge appearance and visible ray/scoring at the oblique cheek; dark eyes became flat dots rather than anatomically embedded living eyes. The side bill protrudes unnaturally and the swept flank still has no believable fully down-hidden folded wing. The back remains a near-featureless symmetric pear. **11,000 actual mesh fibre guides read as glittering grain/prickles instead of short velvety, dense cream second-down.** Three-toed feet look detached/overlong on a stylized slab, not grounded juvenile feet/belly against an old tree cavity lip. Main body mesh has 224 open boundary edges; no real phone/PBR/animation acceptance.

**Verdict: ART_REJECTED — no sample should be shown to user, released, assigned PASS, or deployed.** This proves a free 3D construction/roundtrip and renders, not an acceptable character. Need better age-appropriate documented side/back references and an anatomy-first professionally sculpted cranium/wing/body with groomed natural feather layers; cease using additional minor smooth-loft parameter or mesh-guide tricks as art success.
