## Actual independent art inspection — **ART_REJECTED**

Opened both authentic four-angle contacts and compared them to the original v3 rightmost bird, not only automatic metrics. **Positive:** two dark eyes are now clearly open in the true 3D front/quarter view instead of absent eye sockets; the head and eye region cast real three-dimensional shadows and GLB reimport preserves the edited body. Surface-sampled cards soften the *front chest outline slightly* and add a coarse fluffy fringe. This is a genuine narrow geometric advance.

**Why it is not an acceptable baby owl:** the head still presents a **small pale bowl-shaped mask** inside a much larger rough crown rather than a broad soft heart face with correctly proportioned, symmetric orbits; eye size/location and beak cannot be regarded as professional anatomical sculpt. Profile remains a highly elongated hunched wedge with adult-like exposed talons. The short fluffy wing is not convincingly integrated. Side/back still resemble overlapping flakes or a rough pinecone, not dense, naturally layered cream second down; added cards read as sparse straw-like spikes. The feet and belly **float without the mature eucalypt hollow lip support** required in the target. No acceptable tail occlusion or scientifically grounded side/back. Even this true-3D + moderate file size does not overcome the main juvenile anatomy defect.

**Technical limits:** Cycles rendered four views successfully and Blender re-imported both GLBs, but **no real iOS/Android GPU, mobile WebGL frame timing, touch, animation quality, memory/thermal** or browser shader parity was tested for these GLBs. Standard Three.js GLTFLoader compatibility is not automatically proven by Blender import. Artist approval and distribution license are both separate negative gates. Run99 Hunyuan3D source carries Tencent's **community-license territorial/output restrictions** and is not cleared for global commercial release; no permission is inferred from MIT licensing of unrelated models.
