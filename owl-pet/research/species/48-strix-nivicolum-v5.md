## Evidence actually read this cycle

1. `HIML-NTM-YAMADAE` — National Taiwan Museum collection TMAV0249, live record lines 73–113. Confirms accepted display name *S. n. yamadae*, round untufted head, yellow-green bill, feathered toes, dark-bordered tawny/brown facial disc, dark back/cream scapular spots, buff-barred wing/tail and crossed ventral marks. Its juvenile description is very pale, with sparse white down and blurred ventral marks that darken/sharpen with age. The specimen itself has damaged wing and tail, so no wing/tail metric is transferred.

The accessible text gives no hatch dates, feather chronology or locomotor threshold and is not used to age the Taiwanese juvenile.

4. `HIML-TAIEOL-2013-YAMADAE` — Taiwan Encyclopedia of Life account lines 70–106. Adult dark eyes, pinkish thin eye ring, yellow bill, crossed underpart pattern and >1,800 m mixed-forest habitat; tree holes or other birds' old nests and May–June departure are population-level context, not an individual-age key.
